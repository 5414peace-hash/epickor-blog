(Commit: b89598e)` → `Found .vercelignore` → `Removed 156 ignored files` and was **Ready in 3 minutes**; the CLI archive deploy logged **`Extracted 20,230 deployment files`** (4.5 minutes just to extract), errored after 45 minutes, and left two more deploys stuck in Queued behind it.
