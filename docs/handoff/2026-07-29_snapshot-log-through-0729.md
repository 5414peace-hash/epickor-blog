## Current Snapshot - 2026-07-26 (evening)

- **Strategy reset: the goal is now clicks x10, not impressions x10 — confirmed by the representative.** GSC 07-24 analysis showed impressions are the wrong target: 94.5% of our impressions already rank position 1-10, but 88,064 of them are *definition* queries ("ahjussi meaning") that Google answers in the SERP, converting at **0.048%**. Purchase-intent queries convert at **2.0%** — 42x better. Simulation: 10x impressions on the current query mix yields only 1.3x clicks; fixing CTR alone at flat impressions yields 9.2x. **New posts must target conversion, not reach.**
- **Amazon funnel measured end to end.** 339,670 impressions → 1,234 clicks → 42 affiliate clicks/month → 0 orders. Blog-click-to-affiliate-click is **10.2%, which is healthy** — the leak is after the click. Three causes found: (a) **473 clicks/month (38%) come from countries where our amazon.com links can't convert** (India 119, Singapore 59, UK 47, Canada 41, Germany 41, Australia 39) — only US 238 (19%) is currently monetizable; (b) 75% of in-body links were Amazon *search* links, not product links; (c) the `affiliate_amazon_click` GA4 event has been recording page/CTA/tag detail for months and **nobody has ever looked at it**. Full plan: `output/strategy/epickor_amazon_plan_2026-07-26.md`.
- **Statistical correction worth remembering:** I initially reported "42 clicks / 0 orders" as proof the funnel is broken. It isn't proof — at a normal 3% conversion, 0 orders from 42 clicks happens **28% of the time**. Judging conversion needs ~100 accumulated clicks (~2.5 months). Do not re-diagnose before then.

- **DEPLOY PROCEDURE CHANGED — stop running `vercel deploy` manually.** `CLAUDE.md` had said git push does not trigger a deploy and that `npx vercel deploy --prod --archive=tgz --yes` is required. **That is now wrong and it caused an outage today.** Evidence from the same afternoon: the git-triggered deploy logged `Cloning ... (Commit: b89598e)` → `Found .vercelignore` → `Removed 156 ignored files` and was **Ready in 3 minutes**; the CLI archive deploy logged **`Extracted 20,230 deployment files`** (4.5 minutes just to extract), errored after 45 minutes, and left two more deploys stuck in Queued behind it. Git deploys never see them because both are gitignored. **Publishing now ends at `git push`.** Cleared the jam with `npx vercel remove {url} --yes` on the three stuck deployments.
