Ansungtangmyun vs Shin Ramyun: which Korean ramyun to buy first

✓ LAST UPDATED AUG 2026 12 MIN READ

At a convenience store a single bag of either runs about ₩1,000; five-packs at E-Mart cost markedly less per serving. The real difference is the base: Ansungtangmyun is soybean paste (된장), Shin Ramyun is chili.
