Ansungtangmyun vs Shin Ramyun: which Korean ramyun to buy first

LAST UPDATED AUG 2026 12 MIN READ EVERY FIGURE SOURCED

Buying your first Korean ramyun is a coin toss between two red packets — except one of them is not actually the spicy one, and the difference is printed on the soup sachet if you know what colour to look for.

At a convenience store a single bag of either runs about ₩1,000; five-packs at E-Mart or Homeplus cost markedly less per serving. The real difference is the base: Ansungtangmyun is built on soybean paste (된장), Shin Ramyun on chili.
