Ansungtangmyun vs Shin Ramyun: which Korean ramyun to buy first

안성탕면 대 신라면 — 순한 신라면이 아니라, 다른 요리입니다
Verified
August 2026
Reading
12 minutes
Sources
Korean-language

They are not a mild and a spicy version of the same soup. Ansungtangmyun is built on soybean paste, Shin Ramyun on chili — and the powder colour settles it before you boil water. At a convenience store a single bag of either runs about ₩1,000; five-packs at a mart cost markedly less per serving.
