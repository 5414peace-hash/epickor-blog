# Template Notes

Use this template only for Type B-1 client stories.

Hard blockers before drafting:

- Representative selected the company.
- Tripclip actually produced video work for the company.
- Client consent is confirmed.
- Public video embedding permission is granted.
- Sensitive information is cleared for publication.

The site loader will not publish a `client-story` post unless `consentStatus` is `confirmed` and `videoEmbedPermission` is `granted`.
