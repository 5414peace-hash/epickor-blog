## Why Korean Salons Feel Different
The biggest difference is process discipline. Many salons treat each appointment like a mini-consulting session, not a quick transaction.

- shape analysis,
- scalp condition check,
- maintenance-level discussion,
- personalized aftercare advice.

This reduces mismatch between “Instagram inspiration” and real daily styling.

## Scalp-First Culture: The Hidden K-Beauty Core
Korean hair aesthetics are built on scalp condition. Head spa and scaling are popular because users see hair quality as a skin-care extension.

Benefits often include:
1. reduced product buildup,
2. better root volume,
3. cleaner texture response for styling.

## Foreigner Booking Playbook
To increase success rate:
- message in advance with current hair photos,
- explain how much daily styling time you can invest,
- confirm language support before booking,
- ask for realistic maintenance cycle.

Good districts for first timers often include Hongdae, Myeongdong, and parts of Gangnam.

## Pricing Reality
- Basic cut: often moderate pricing.
- Color/perm: higher, but service depth and finishing quality are usually strong.
- Premium experience bundles: can include beverages, hand care, and tailored aftercare recommendations.

## FAQ
### Are Korean salons foreigner-friendly?
Many are, especially in tourist and expat-heavy areas. Advance messaging helps avoid communication gaps.

### Is head spa worth the extra money?
If your scalp is sensitive or oily, yes—many users report clear comfort and styling improvements.

### Should I bring a reference photo?
Absolutely. It improves clarity and reduces expectation mismatch.

### How often should I revisit for maintenance?
Depends on cut and treatment, but many people follow 4–8 week cycles.

## Final Takeaway
Korean salon quality comes from systemized consultation + scalp care + service detail. Use the booking playbook and maintenance plan, and you can get results that feel both stylish and sustainable.
