## Why Seoul Temples Matter for Travelers
These spaces offer psychological contrast to high-speed city life. Many visitors describe temples as “reset points” in busy itineraries.

Benefits:
- quieter sensory environment,
- historical grounding,
- low-cost cultural immersion.

## Visitor Etiquette (Simple Rules)
1. speak quietly,
2. use side entrances where indicated,
3. avoid flash/close photos of active worship,
4. dress respectfully.

Respectful behavior improves both your experience and local perception.

## FAQ
### Can non-Buddhists visit Seoul temples?
Yes, most temples are open to all respectful visitors.

### Is there an entrance fee?
Many city temples are free, though donations are welcome.

### Is photography allowed?
Generally yes outdoors, but be careful around ceremonies and prayer moments.

### Are temple stays suitable for beginners?
Yes, if you choose beginner-friendly programs and follow schedule guidance.

## Final Takeaway
Seoul temples are not just tourist landmarks—they are active cultural sanctuaries. If you visit with etiquette and intention, they become one of the most memorable parts of a Korea trip.
