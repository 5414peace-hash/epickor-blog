## Why Haenyeo Matter Globally
Haenyeo are not simply divers; they are a living model of sustainable extraction.

- no oxygen tanks,
- seasonal/area discipline,
- community norm enforcement.

This is why UNESCO recognized the tradition.

## Why They Dive Without Tanks
This is a deliberate ecological constraint. By limiting dive time to breath capacity, harvest pressure remains naturally bounded.

Related cultural context: [Korean social fabric](/blog/139), [Korean nature and lifestyle balance](/blog/065).

## Respectful Visitor Checklist
1. do not interrupt working divers,
2. avoid drone noise near active zones,
3. buy local products through legitimate channels,
4. prioritize museum/education centers before beach selfies.

## FAQ
### Are there still young Haenyeo entering the field?
Numbers are limited, but schools and cultural programs are trying to preserve continuity.

### Can tourists watch Haenyeo safely?
Yes, in designated viewing contexts and local cultural centers.

### Why is this tradition fading?
Urban migration and high occupational risk reduce new entrants.

### Is this only about seafood?
No. It is a full social system linking labor, ecology, and female-led community governance.

## Final Takeaway
Haenyeo culture is one of Korea's strongest living heritage systems. Visit with context, not just curiosity, and you will understand why the world studies Jeju's sea women model.
