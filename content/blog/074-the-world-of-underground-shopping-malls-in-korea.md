Seoul underground shopping malls can confuse first-time visitors because they do not always look like malls from the street. You may enter through a subway exit, follow a corridor, pass a row of cosmetics stores, turn a corner, and suddenly realize you are inside a shopping district that feels like a small city under the road.

That is normal in Seoul. Some of the city's most useful shopping spaces sit underground because they connect subway stations, department stores, bus terminals, office towers, restaurants, and weather-proof walking routes. In winter, summer rain, or rush hour, these underground malls are not just places to buy things. They are part of how Seoul moves.

This guide explains the Seoul underground shopping malls most travelers actually ask about: COEX Mall at Samseong Station, Gangnam Station Underground Shopping Center, Express Bus Terminal's Goto Mall, and the Hongdae question that appears often in search results.

## Quick Answer: Which Seoul Underground Mall Should You Visit?

If you want the easiest first choice, go to **COEX Mall**. It is large, polished, and connected to major attractions like Starfield Library. It feels more like a modern indoor complex than a bargain hunt.

If you want affordable fashion, accessories, phone cases, beauty items, and quick browsing, choose **Gangnam Station Underground Shopping Center**. It is convenient, busy, and very Seoul.

If you want the classic local treasure-hunt feeling, go to **Goto Mall** at Express Bus Terminal. It is especially good for budget fashion, home items, flowers, accessories, and long browsing sessions.

## COEX Mall: The Easiest Underground Mall for First-Timers

COEX Mall is the most beginner-friendly option because it is spacious, famous, and easy to combine with other plans. If someone wants a Seoul underground shopping mall that feels safe, clean, and easy to explain, COEX is the answer.

The mall is connected to the Samseong Station area in Gangnam, and it is known for shopping, restaurants, cafes, exhibitions, a cinema, and Starfield Library. It does not feel like a narrow underground arcade. It feels more like a full indoor destination.

![Samseong Station COEX Mall exterior](/assets/images/posts/074/106_epickor_%EA%B1%B4%EC%88%9C.mp4_20240919_111617.641.jpg)

COEX is best for visitors who want comfort. You can browse, eat, rest, take photos, and escape bad weather without constantly checking your map. It is also a good choice if your group has different interests. One person can shop, another can sit in a cafe, and another can take pictures around the library.

The tradeoff is price and atmosphere. COEX is not the best place if your main goal is cheap local fashion. It is more polished and less chaotic than Gangnam Station or Goto Mall. That makes it easier, but also less of a treasure hunt.

Choose COEX if you want:

- A clean and modern indoor mall
- Easy food options
- Starfield Library photos
- A Gangnam-area plan that works in bad weather
- Shopping without the pressure of a crowded bargain street

If your Seoul trip already includes Gangnam, Bongeunsa, or a convention/exhibition plan, COEX fits naturally into the day.

## Gangnam Station Underground Shopping: Fast, Cheap, and Busy

Gangnam Station Underground Shopping Center is closer to what many visitors imagine when they hear "Seoul underground shopping." It is practical, crowded, and full of small shops selling fashion, beauty, accessories, socks, phone cases, bags, and trend items.

This is not a slow luxury mall. It is a quick-decision environment. You walk, scan, compare, and keep moving. If you like that energy, it is fun. If you hate crowds, go during quieter hours and avoid peak commute time.

Gangnam Station is also useful because the surrounding area is packed with restaurants, cafes, clinics, offices, and nightlife. The underground shopping area works well as a quick stop before dinner or as a weather-proof shopping break.

The main advantage is convenience. You do not need to plan a whole day around it. If you are already near Gangnam Station, you can spend 30-90 minutes browsing and leave with something small. This is where visitors often buy affordable accessories, beauty basics, hair clips, simple clothing, or quick gifts.

The main disadvantage is that shops can start to look similar. Do not expect every store to feel unique. The fun comes from comparing small differences: price, color, texture, fit, and trend timing.

## Goto Mall and Express Bus Terminal: The Treasure Hunt

Goto Mall is the underground shopping experience many Seoul locals think of when they want long, affordable browsing. It is connected to Express Bus Terminal Station, a major transfer point served by multiple subway lines and bus connections.

That station matters. A lot of search queries mention "subway station underground shopping mall multiple lines" because travelers are trying to identify the big station-based shopping areas. Express Bus Terminal is one of the clearest answers.

![Express Bus Terminal Underground Shopping Mall](/assets/images/posts/074/106_epickor_%EA%B1%B4%EC%88%9C.mp4_20240919_111556.496.jpg)

Goto Mall is especially good for budget fashion, home decor, flowers, simple accessories, bags, socks, and seasonal items. It can feel repetitive at first, but that is part of the strategy. You walk the rows, compare similar items, and slowly learn the price range.

For travelers, Goto Mall works best when you have patience. If you only want one famous photo spot, go to COEX. If you want to browse like a local and maybe find small deals, go to Goto Mall.

## Does Hongdae Station Have an Underground Shopping Mall?

This question appears often because Hongdae is one of Seoul's most famous youth districts. The answer needs nuance.

Hongdae Station has underground station facilities, shops, connected passages, and plenty of shopping nearby. But if you are looking for a major underground shopping mall in the same sense as Goto Mall or Gangnam Station Underground Shopping Center, Hongdae is not the best match.

Go to Hongdae for street energy, cafes, small fashion stores, busking, restaurants, clubs, photo booths, and youth culture. Go to Gangnam Station or Goto Mall if your actual goal is underground shopping.

That distinction can save you time. A traveler searching for "Hongdae station underground shopping mall multiple lines" may really want one of two things:

- A shopping-heavy underground station area: choose Gangnam Station or Express Bus Terminal.
- A youth-culture neighborhood with shopping nearby: choose Hongdae.

Both are useful, but they are not the same experience.

## What Should You Buy?

Seoul underground shopping malls are best for light, affordable, trend-sensitive items. Think accessories, socks, hair clips, casual clothes, bags, phone cases, beauty basics, stationery, and small gifts.

They are less ideal for major luxury purchases unless you are in a department-store-connected area. If you are buying clothing, check sizing carefully. Some shops may have limited fitting options, and return policies can vary.

For beauty shopping, underground malls are useful for casual browsing, but if you want a specific skincare product, compare with Olive Young or brand stores. ## FAQ About Seoul Underground Shopping Malls

**Q: What is the best underground shopping mall in Seoul?**
Simply put, COEX Mall is the easiest for first-time visitors, Gangnam Station Underground Shopping Center is best for quick affordable fashion, and Goto Mall is best for long bargain shopping.

**Q: Which subway station has the biggest underground shopping area?**
Simply put, Express Bus Terminal Station is one of the most important station areas for underground shopping because it connects to Goto Mall and a major transport hub.

**Q: Is Gangnam Station Underground Shopping Center cheap?**
Simply put, it is known for affordable fashion, accessories, cosmetics, and quick trend items. Prices vary, but it is more budget-friendly than luxury shopping above ground.

**Q: Is COEX Mall underground?**
Simply put, COEX is a large indoor mall connected to the Samseong Station area. It feels more like a full modern shopping complex than a narrow underground arcade.

**Q: Does Hongdae have an underground shopping mall?**
Simply put, Hongdae has station shops and lots of nearby street shopping, but it is not the same kind of major underground mall as Goto Mall, Gangnam Station Underground, or COEX.

## The Easiest Rule

If you only remember one thing, remember this: choose the underground mall by your mission.

Go to COEX for comfort. Go to Gangnam Station for quick trend shopping. Go to Goto Mall for bargain hunting. Go to Hongdae for youth culture and street energy, not for a major underground mall.

Seoul's underground shopping malls are not just backup plans for bad weather. They are part of the city's everyday rhythm. Once you understand how they connect stations, shopping, food, and movement, you start to see why Seoul often feels like two cities at once: one above the street, and one quietly running below it.
