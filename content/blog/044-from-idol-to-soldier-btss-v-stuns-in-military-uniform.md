## The Short Answer: What Happened?

V, whose real name is Kim Taehyung, fulfilled his mandatory service in the Republic of Korea Army after enlisting in December 2023. Korea.net reported that RM and V were honorably discharged on June 10, 2025, with RM having served in the Army's 15th Infantry Division military band and V in a special mission team of the military police. BIGHIT MUSIC's Weverse notices also asked fans not to visit enlistment or discharge sites because those spaces are reserved for military personnel and can become unsafe if crowds gather.

That is the factual spine. The cultural meaning is bigger. BTS's military period was watched globally because each member's enlistment and discharge affected the timing of solo work, group activity, fandom energy, and the symbolic return of all seven members.

## What Is the SDT / Special Mission Team?

International fans often use "SDT" when talking about V's service. In English reporting around his discharge, Korea.net described his role as a special mission team of the military police. Fan language may vary, but the important point is that it was not a normal entertainment schedule or symbolic uniform shoot. It was part of his actual active-duty service.

That distinction matters because some viral coverage turned the topic into pure aesthetics: black uniform, beret, serious expression, movie-poster energy. The image spread because it was visually strong, but the real Korean context is duty, discipline, and service structure. For Korean readers, military service is not only a celebrity content theme. It is a shared social institution that affects families, workplaces, and public reputation.

For V, the role amplified a public image already associated with warmth, style, and stage charisma. It added a harder, more disciplined chapter without replacing his artist identity.

## Why Military Service Matters So Much in K-pop

South Korea requires most able-bodied men to complete military service. For male K-pop idols, that means a major interruption to the usual entertainment cycle. Albums, tours, variety appearances, ads, livestreams, and social content all have to bend around enlistment. Fans may experience it emotionally as a hiatus, but Korean society frames it as national duty.

That is why agencies are careful with enlistment and discharge notices. They usually ask fans not to visit military sites, not because fan support is unwelcome, but because training centers and discharge locations are not concert venues. BIGHIT MUSIC repeated this safety message across BTS notices.

The service period also changes public language around idols. The star is no longer only a performer. He is temporarily a soldier, a citizen, and a member of a national system that many Korean men pass through.

## V's Image Before and After Service

Before military service, V's public image mixed several traits: deep vocal color, expressive face, luxury-fashion presence, photography interest, emotional warmth with fans, and an unpredictable artistic streak. BIGHIT's artist profile emphasizes his vocal tone, stage presence, "I purple you" connection with fans, and individual personality.

Military service did not erase that. It gave the image a new chapter. When he returned, the story was not "idol becomes soldier forever." It was "artist completes duty and comes back with a more mature timeline." That matters because K-pop careers are built through eras. Military service became one of BTS's largest era breaks.

For international fans, this is a useful way to understand Korean celebrity continuity. A hiatus is not always brand death. Sometimes it becomes the narrative bridge into the next phase.

## The Discharge Moment and Fan Etiquette

RM and V's discharge on June 10, 2025 was widely covered, but fans should remember the repeated official guidance: do not visit military sites in person. BIGHIT's discharge notice said no special events were planned at the sites and asked fans to send support from the heart because overcrowding can create safety risks.

This is one of those moments where good fandom is restraint. The most supportive thing is not always physical presence. For military events, restraint protects the artist, soldiers, staff, local residents, and other fans.

That principle applies beyond BTS. If a Korean celebrity's agency asks fans not to gather, do not turn the location into a fan event. The camera moment is not worth creating risk.

## What Not to Say About V's Service

Avoid turning V's service into a fantasy action storyline. It is fine to say the images were striking. It is not responsible to exaggerate duties, invent classified details, or treat a military unit as a fan concept. Stick to confirmed facts: enlistment, service completion, discharge date, and official safety guidance.

Also avoid saying the service "proved" one fixed idea of masculinity. Korean military service is complex. Some people feel pride, some feel pressure, and many experience it as an ordinary civic obligation. V's case drew attention because he is globally famous, not because celebrity service is more meaningful than everyone else's service.

## FAQ

**Q: When was BTS V discharged from the military?**
V was honorably discharged on June 10, 2025, the same day as RM, according to Korea.net's report on the BTS discharge milestone.

**Q: What unit did V serve in?**
Korea.net reported that V served in a special mission team of the military police. Fans often refer to this broadly with SDT-related language, but the safest wording is to use the confirmed reporting.

**Q: Did BIGHIT allow fans to visit discharge sites?**
No. BIGHIT MUSIC's notices asked fans not to visit military sites because no official events were planned and crowding could create safety risks.

**Q: Why did V's military service receive so much attention?**
BTS is a global group, and V's service was part of the larger timeline toward all seven members completing duty and returning to group activity.

**Q: Does military service change an idol's career?**
Yes. It interrupts promotion, changes public perception, and creates a return narrative. It can also refresh attention when the artist comes back.

**Q: What is the best way for overseas fans to support V after discharge?**
Respect official boundaries, avoid military sites, support releases through normal channels, share safe messages, and celebrate the comeback era without chasing private schedules.
