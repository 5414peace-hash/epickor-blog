## Why MU:DS Is Different From Typical Museum Shops
Most museum shops in the world are still postcard-heavy. MU:DS took a different path: it treats Korean heritage as **contemporary lifestyle design**. Instead of only making products for tourists, the line is developed for local demand first, which naturally raises quality and repeat purchase potential.

Three reasons this matters for visitors:
1. **Design consistency:** color palette and form language are modern enough for real daily use.
2. **Story value:** every product ties back to a Korean artifact or historical motif.
3. **Resale and scarcity behavior:** fast sell-outs create urgency, especially for limited drops.

A common mistake is spending the full budget in one stop. A better method:

1. **On-site first:** buy one signature item you can only get at MU:DS.
