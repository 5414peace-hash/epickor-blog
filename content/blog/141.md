## Why the Craze Exploded
Running along the Han became a social trend because it provides:

- safe and visually attractive routes,
- low entry barrier,
- community identity through crews,
- strong social media moments.

## New Etiquette Pressure and Group Limits
Some districts introduced guidance against large groups because crowding caused safety issues.

Core rules:
1. avoid full-track width occupation,
2. keep moving if taking photos,
3. control group noise at night.

## Night Running Strategy
Night is preferred due to cooler temperatures and better comfort.

- run reflective-friendly routes,
- keep hydration intervals,
- finish near transport nodes.

## FAQ
### Do I need a running crew?
No. Solo and pair running are very common and often easier logistically.

### Are Han River routes safe at night?
Generally yes, especially in active segments with regular foot traffic.

### Can tourists run without local apps?
Yes, but route planning and weather checks in advance help.

### What is the biggest mistake beginners make?
Starting too fast in crowded zones and stopping suddenly on the track.

## Final Takeaway
Han River running is best treated as a shared public system. Respect lane etiquette, keep your group compact, and you will enjoy one of Seoul's best lifestyle experiences.
