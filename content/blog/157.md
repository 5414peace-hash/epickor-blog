## Day-by-Day Flow (Balanced, Not Rushed)
### Days 1–3: Seoul
- Day 1: Gyeongbokgung + Myeongdong.
- Day 2: Seongsu + evening skyline.
- Day 3: DMZ or museum/café alternative.

## Budget Control Rules
1. lock transit in advance,
2. use one premium meal per city,
3. avoid moving hotels every night,
4. keep final day low-risk near departure node.

## FAQ
### Is 7 days enough for Korea?
Yes for highlights. It is enough for a strong first-pass experience without burnout.

### Should I buy KORAIL pass?
If doing Seoul-Gyeongju-Busan continuously, it is often worth considering.

### Is this route suitable for families?
Yes, with slower pacing and fewer late-night transfers.

### Can I swap Busan with Jeju?
Yes, but flights and weather risk management become more important.

## Final Takeaway
A 7-day Korea trip works best when route logic is clean and fatigue is controlled. Use this 3-city structure, keep transfer friction low, and prioritize memory quality over checklist quantity.
