Tteokbokki isn't one dish.

Six versions share one chewy rice cake, and ordering the right one is the difference between "why is this so hot" and "why didn't anyone tell me sooner."

Swipe for the six styles Koreans actually order:
- Classic gochujang (the default, order twigim with it)
- Cheese (easy mode if you're spice-shy)
- Rabokki (add ramyeon, now it's dinner)
- Rose, jjajang, and the non-spicy royal-court original

Start at a market stall. Cheapest portion, best first taste.
