Your cute summer suitcase is probably lying to you.

Korea in July and August is not just "hot." It can be sun, sudden rain, freezing cafes, wet shoes, mosquitoes, festival crowds, and a phone battery problem in the same day.

Save this before packing:
- compact umbrella
- waterproof phone pouch
- SPF you will actually reapply
- small power bank
- card pouch
- quick-dry layer
- repellent for evening plans
