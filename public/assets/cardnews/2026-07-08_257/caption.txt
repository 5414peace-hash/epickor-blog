Your Incheon layover is not "free time." It is a countdown. ✈️⏱️🧳

Before you leave ICN, count usable time: landing, immigration, AREX, queues, return security, and boarding all eat the clock.

Under 3 hours? Stay airside.
3-6 hours? Reset inside the airport.
6+ hours? Seoul can work, but only with a buffer and a simple plan.

Save this before your next Korea layover.
