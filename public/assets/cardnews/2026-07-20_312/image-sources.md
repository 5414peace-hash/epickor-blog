Prepared 2026-07-20. Four images are post-owned (reused from the published Blog 312 image set, per the
post-owned-first sourcing rule); three ingredient-source images were newly sourced for this carousel.
Every image was opened and manually inspected at full resolution.

Korea-first note: the COSRX serums, Pyunkang Yul jar, and drugstore shelf are verifiably Korean. The
snail, honeycomb, centella, and rice images are botanical/ingredient close-ups of the exact substance
each card explains. They are culturally neutral rather than foreign-looking, and each caption states
only what the image depicts, so they do not misrepresent Korean context.

Render notes: all cards use `image_tone: food` with tuned `image_opacity` because the renderer default
veil (rgba 0.24 -> 0.92) buries photographs. Full-bleed layouts where text sits over the photo use
0.58-0.74; split-panel layouts (B/C/E) use 0.80-0.86.
