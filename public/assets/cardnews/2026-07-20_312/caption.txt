Korean skincare doesn't sell you "radiance." It sells you the ingredient, with the percentage printed on the front.

Once you can read the front of the box, the whole aisle gets easier.

Swipe for the four hero ingredients:
- Snail (snail secretion filtrate) for slip and hydration
- Cica, which is just the Centella asiatica plant
- Propolis, bee-made and glowy (skip if you're allergic)
- Rice, the gentlest place to start

And the trap: that number on the label is a hint, not a grade. A good 5% formula can beat a near-pure one.
