## Primary Caption

Bingsu is not just ice. It is Korea's summer texture test. 🍧🥭🥄

Start with patbingsu if you want the classic version, go fruit-first if you want something lighter, and always check the ice texture before judging the bowl.

Most bingsu is built to share, which is why the best order is often one bowl, two spoons, and no rush.

Save this for your next Korean cafe stop.
