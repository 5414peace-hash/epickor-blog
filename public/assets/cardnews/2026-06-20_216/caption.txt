Your suitcase should not become the main character of your Seoul day.

Korea is easy to move through when your luggage plan is smart:
- hotel storage when the route comes back naturally
- lockers for short stops
- delivery for awkward check-out days
- airport bus or taxi when luggage makes transfers painful
- Seoul Station check-in only after checking airline, timing, and rules

Save this before your checkout day.
