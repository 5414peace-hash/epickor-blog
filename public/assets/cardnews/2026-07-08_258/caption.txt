Noryangjin is amazing until you panic at the fish tank. 🐟🦀🍽️

The trick is simple: buy first, cook second, confirm the cooking fee before you sit down, and do not feel pressured to order the wildest seafood in the building.

Go earlier, keep the order simple, and treat it like a two-hour food mission instead of a random market walk.

Save this if Noryangjin is on your Seoul list.
