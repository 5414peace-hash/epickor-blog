Why are there so many Mercedes-Benz cars in Korea?

The short answer is not just "foreign cars are popular."

In Seoul, especially around high-status districts, a Mercedes can work like a social signal.

It can suggest:

success
business credibility
professional polish
Gangnam lifestyle
visible proof that someone has "made it"

That does not mean every Korean thinks this way.

But it explains why the silver star carries such a strong cultural charge.
