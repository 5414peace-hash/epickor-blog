🌃 Hongdae, Itaewon, or Gangnam?

Do not rank them.
Match the mood.

Hongdae is young energy:
street performances, cafes, casual shopping, photo booths, and late-night crowds.

Itaewon is global Seoul:
international food, mixed groups, English-friendly nightlife, and flexible dinner plans.

Gangnam is polished:
K-beauty, COEX, Apgujeong, Cheongdam, and sleek modern Seoul.

📌 Save this before choosing your first Seoul night.
