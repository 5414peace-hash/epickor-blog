Seoul subway etiquette is less about memorizing rules and more about reading the flow. 🚇

Let passengers exit before you board. Keep priority seats available for riders who need them. Move your backpack to the front in a crowded car, keep calls short, and step aside before checking the map.

The six-word version: Don't block. Don't dominate. Pay attention.

Save this before your next Seoul transfer.
