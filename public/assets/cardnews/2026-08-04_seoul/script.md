## Card 02 - Ikseon-dong
image: sources/card_02-ikseon-dong.jpg
image_label: A narrow alley of single-storey hanok in Ikseon-dong
name_ko: 익선동
name_en: IKSEON-DONG
lines: 1, 3, 5
station: Jongno 3-ga
time: 1–2 hours
stay: Visit, don't stay

**Sub:** Seoul's oldest surviving hanok village — and it was built as a property development, not preserved as one.
note: Mass-produced from the late 1920s by Korea's first modern developer. It survived because redevelopment kept failing until 2018.

## Card 03 - Euljiro
image: sources/card_03-euljiro.jpg
image_label: Nogari Alley in Euljiro at night, plastic tables on the street
name_ko: 을지로
name_en: EULJIRO
lines: 2, 3
station: Euljiro 3-ga
time: An evening
stay: Workable

**Sub:** Machine shops and print works by day. After 7pm the same street is the best drinking alley in Seoul.
note: The bars exist because the printers worked night shifts. Seoul designated the alley Future Heritage in 2015.

## Card 04 - Yeonnam-dong
image: sources/card_04-yeonnam-dong.jpg
image_label: Gyeongui Line Forest Park running through Yeonnam-dong
name_ko: 연남동
name_en: YEONNAM-DONG
lines: 2, A, K
station: Hongik University
time: Half a day
stay: Good base

**Sub:** The long thin park everyone sits in is a railway line. Seoul buried the track and kept the shape.
note: The Yongsan line went underground between 2012 and 2016 and the surface became a 6km park. Its shape gives it away.

---

## Card 05 - Haebangchon
image: sources/card_05-haebangchon.jpg
image_label: The Haebangchon hillside below Namsan
name_ko: 해방촌
name_en: HAEBANGCHON
lines: 6
station: Noksapyeong
time: 2–3 hours
stay: Workable

**Sub:** Liberation Village. Refugees built it on the slope of Namsan after 1945, and the gradient is still the whole experience.
note: Walk it downhill from Namsan, not up from the station. The name is the history, not a brand.

---

## Card 06 - Mullae
image: sources/card_06-mullae.jpg
image_label: Painted shutters between working steel workshops in Mullae
name_ko: 문래동
name_en: MULLAE-DONG
lines: 2
station: Mullae
time: 2–3 hours
stay: Visit, don't stay

**Sub:** Steel workshops with artists' studios above them. Nothing here was built for visitors, which is the reason to go.
note: The workshops are working businesses. Weekday daytime is loud and real; Sunday is shuttered and quiet.
