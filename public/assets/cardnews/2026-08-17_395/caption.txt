96.3% snail secretion filtrate. On the regulated ingredient list it comes first, before water. Most "snail" products add a splash to a lotion; this one is the mucus, lightly supported.

The company: COSRX was founded in December 2013 by cosmetics researcher Jeon Sang-hoon, who read skin forums and bottled the answers. The name is literally Cosmetics + RX. Cheap packaging, no fragrance, lab-report product names.

And it is no longer indie. Korea's largest beauty group bought roughly 96% of COSRX in stages for about ₩935.1 billion and consolidated it from 2024. Revenue went ₩123bn (2021) → ₩590bn (2024). Nothing on the bottle changed.
