Your Chuseok train route has its own booking day. Turn up on the wrong morning and there is nothing left to buy. 🚄

Korea does not sell holiday train seats on the normal rolling schedule. Korail's official notice, posted 13 August, splits the Chuseok advance sale across nine days — and which day is yours depends on your line and the station you leave from.

Save this if you are travelling 23–27 September:

📌 3–4 SEP, 09:00–15:00 — priority booking, lines split across the two days
📌 7 SEP, 07:00–13:00 — conventional trains only (ITX-Saemaeul, ITX-Maeum, Mugunghwa 무궁화호), every line
📌 8 SEP — KTX from Seoul and Cheongnyangni
📌 9 SEP — KTX from Yongsan, for Honam and Jeolla
📌 10 SEP — anything to or from Suseo 수서역, which since the merger includes the old SRT trains
📌 11 SEP — KTX from Seoul on the Gyeongbu line: Daejeon, Dongdaegu, Gyeongju, Ulsan, Busan
📌 11 SEP, 15:00 onward — leftover seats, and the only stage sold at a station counter instead of online

The one that catches people: Seoul → Busan is Korea's busiest journey and it is the LAST slot. A Mugunghwa to a small town opens four days earlier.
