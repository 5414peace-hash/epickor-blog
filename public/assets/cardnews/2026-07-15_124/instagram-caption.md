## Primary Caption

A Korean wedding invitation is more than a date and a dress code. It is your operating manual. 💌

Cash gift envelopes are common, but there is no universal amount. Ceremony formats vary too: a sleek wedding hall may include candle lighting, pyebaek, both, or neither.

You may also hear “seu-deu-me,” the shorthand for studio, dress, and makeup packages. It is a useful planning bundle, not a complete definition of a Korean marriage.

For a first visit, keep it simple: check the venue floor, arrive before the ceremony, write your name on the envelope, and follow the couple's invitation.

Save this before your first Korean wedding.
