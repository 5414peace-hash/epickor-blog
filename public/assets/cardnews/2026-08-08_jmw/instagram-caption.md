# Instagram Caption — JMW (Korean Makers FILE 05)

The aircraft motor that never existed ✈️❌

Overseas listings say JMW — the dryer in seemingly every Korean salon — builds aircraft motors.
Its own corporate history page never mentions aircraft. Not once.

What actually happened is better: a ~50-person Seoul company put a brushless BLDC motor in a hair
dryer first, became the salon standard, and coined a marketing phrase so good it hardened into a
fake origin story in English.
