**Sub:** Pulmuone USA opened in 1991 and struggled for a quarter century — short shelf life, disposal costs, cold-chain pain. The 2016 fix: buy Nasoya, America's #1 tofu brand, for about $50 million. Don't build a brand. Buy one, keep its name, hide yours.
