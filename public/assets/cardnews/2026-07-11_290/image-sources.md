# Image Sources - Card News 290

Topic: Seoul Four-Palace Night Comparison

All imagery comes from the four published, source-documented palace images owned by Blog 290. Card 01 is a four-image comparison composite. Cards 06-07 use alternate square focal crops of Deoksugung and Gyeongbokgung to support the decision shortcut and ticket-warning close.

- Cards 01, 02, and 07 include `gyeongbokgung-night-kocis.jpg` (Jeon Han, Korea.net/KOCIS, CC BY-SA 2.0); Cards 02 and 07 use different focal crops.
- Cards 01 and 03 include `changdeokgung-moonlight-kocis.jpg` (Republic of Korea, CC BY-SA 2.0).
- Cards 01, 04, and 06 include `deoksugung-night-cc.jpg` (Rtflakfizer, CC BY-SA 4.0); Cards 04 and 06 use different focal crops.
- Cards 01 and 05 include `changgyeonggung-moonlight-lotus-kto.jpg` (Korea Tourism Organization, Live Studio).

Each rendered card has a unique `image:` path, and no path appears in another carousel script.
