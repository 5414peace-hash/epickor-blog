Prepared 2026-07-20. Four images are post-owned (reused from the published Blog 313 image set, per the
post-owned-first sourcing rule); three were newly sourced for this carousel. All seven are verifiably
Korean (Korean road and station signage, Gyeongbokgung and National Folk Museum architecture, hanbok,
Hangang with the Seoul skyline, Seoul City Hall atrium).

Rejected during review: Pexels 30769066, a Han River picnic showing two adults reclining on the grass
with no children present. It is a genuine Hangang scene but was off-message for a family carousel, so
it was replaced with the wider Hangang park view before the carousel was finalized.

Render notes: all cards use `image_tone: food` with tuned `image_opacity` because the renderer default
veil (rgba 0.24 -> 0.92) buries photographs. Full-bleed layouts where text sits over the photo use
0.60-0.72; split-panel layouts (B/C/E) use 0.82-0.86.
