Seoul with kids is easier than most parents expect. The hard parts are just never the ones you planned for.

Nobody worries about safety at 9pm. Everybody underestimates how many subway exits are stairs only.

Swipe for what actually matters:
- Check the elevator exit before you arrive, not after
- Kid-size hanbok gets you into the palaces free
- Children's museums let kids touch things (book timed entry)
- Hangang is the free option that works at every age
- Gimbap, mandu, bulgogi and japchae are all mild

The one rule that saves the trip: one big thing per day. Three-attraction days end in tears.
