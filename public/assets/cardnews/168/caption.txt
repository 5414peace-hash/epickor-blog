🥾 Why do Koreans hike every weekend?

It is not only exercise.
It is social life, stress relief, weekend identity, and a city reset.

You can see it on the subway:
bright jackets, hats, poles, backpacks, and groups heading toward the mountains early.

And the hike often ends at the table:
pajeon, tofu, noodles, barbecue, makgeolli, and cold drinks.

📌 Save this before joining a Korean weekend hiking route.
