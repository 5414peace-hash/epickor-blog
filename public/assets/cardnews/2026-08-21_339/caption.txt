The Squid Game arena was never a real room. Seven Seoul places in it are. 🎬

Every set — the doll's arena, the glass bridge, the dalgona tables, the tug-of-war platform — was built from scratch inside Studio Cube, a soundstage in Daejeon. Production designer Chae Kyoung-sun has said it plainly: all the game spaces are sets, not existing locations. So no, you cannot tour them.

But the ordinary scenes were shot in ordinary places, and those you can just walk into:

🎬 SCENE 01 — 양재시민의숲역 (Sinbundang Line)
The ddakji recruitment scene. The station sign overhead is the exact one in the show.

🎬 SCENE 03 — 여의도 · IFC몰 (Line 5 / Line 9)
Season 1's finale. The red-striped Parc.1 towers are that skyline.

🎬 SCENE 04 — 남산공원 (cable car, or Myeongdong Station)
Season 1's forest scenes — the same park N Seoul Tower sits on. No detour needed.

🎬 SCENE 05 — 탑골공원 (Line 1, Jonggak)
Season 2's opener, where the Salesman buys his danpatppang.

🎬 SCENE 06 — 서강대교 (Hapjeong or Dangsan)
Season 2's car chase, across the Han.

🎬 SCENE 07 — 서울대공원 (Line 4)
Season 2's workplace scenes.

And the island: 선갑도 (Seongapdo, Ongjin County, Incheon) is real — and it has no public ferry and no regular residents. Anyone telling you to book a trip there is wrong.
