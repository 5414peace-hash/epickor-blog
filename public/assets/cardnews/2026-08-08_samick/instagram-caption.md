Your guitar might be Korean.

In 1994, one company in Incheon built nearly HALF the guitars on Earth — vintage Epiphones, the first Korean Squiers, Hondo, Washburn. Almost none of them said Samick on the headstock.

Then it went bankrupt over a bounced note worth 0.1% of today's revenue. Then it came back. Then it bid to buy Steinway & Sons — and lost by one dollar per share.

The full 70-year file is on epickor.com — search "Samick." 🎸
