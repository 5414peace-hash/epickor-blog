A Korean PC bang is not a nostalgic internet cafe. 🎮

It is a gaming lounge, snack bar, social club, and one of the neighborhood spaces that helped competitive gaming feel normal in Korea. Pay by time, sit beside your friends, order ramyeon from the seat, and watch one planned hour become three.

For a first visit, bring your game login, start with one hour, and ask for help if the kiosk or account verification blocks you. Keep your voice controlled and avoid filming other players.

Save this for your first PC bang night in Korea.
