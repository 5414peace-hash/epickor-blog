Korean public toilets can surprise first-time visitors.

They are often free, easy to find, and cleaner than many travelers expect.

The practical rules:
start with subway stations, check the toilet-paper sign, and keep a small tissue pack in your bag.

Not glamorous, but it saves your trip.
