Pali pali means "quickly quickly."

But in Korea, it is not just a phrase.

It shows up in restaurants, kiosks, delivery apps, office culture, and even the way people move through daily life.

For visitors, the best tip is simple:
if you need time, step aside first.

That one habit makes Korea's fast rhythm much easier to enjoy.
