## Primary Caption

Ahjussi does not simply mean "mister."

In Korean, address words carry relationship temperature.

Oppa can feel close, youthful, or affectionate.
Samchon can feel warm, safe, and family-like.
Ahjussi feels more distant, practical, adult, and sometimes unflattering.

That is why the word can become sensitive.
It does not only describe age.
It places someone socially.

If you are learning Korean, the safest move is simple:
do not label people too quickly.

Use "jeogiyo" when you need someone's attention.
Use a job title when it clearly fits.
Save ahjussi for situations where the distance and age are obvious.

Korean vocabulary is never just vocabulary.
Sometimes it is the whole relationship in one word.
