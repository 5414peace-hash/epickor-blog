MUSINSA did not simply move from online to offline. It turned Seoul into part of the product. 👕

The company started as an online fashion community in 2001, launched musinsa.com in 2003, and opened its store in 2009. Today the system connects marketplace discovery, private label, physical retail, and global commerce.

The scale is now visible on the street. MUSINSA reported about 9.23 million visits to MUSINSA Standard stores in Q1 2026, while foreign customers accounted for roughly 44% of sales across five road shops.

The harder question is what happens next: can sizing, returns, logistics, and brand trust travel as smoothly as the clothes?
