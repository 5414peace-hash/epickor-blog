🇰🇷 Is Korean hard to learn?

The alphabet is more friendly than many beginners expect.
Hangul is logical, readable, and built around sound blocks.

The harder part is not only grammar.
It is context:
who you are,
who you are speaking to,
and how polite or close the moment should feel.

📌 Learn in this order: letters, useful phrases, then social context.
