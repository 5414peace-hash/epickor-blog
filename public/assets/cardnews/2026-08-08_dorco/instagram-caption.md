You've shaved with Korean blades — you just never got the name.

Dollar Shave Club's razors were made by Dorco, a Seoul company grinding steel since 1955. It started with razor blades scavenged from US army trash. It built the world's first 6-blade and 7-blade razors before Gillette. And at home in Korea? Everyone thinks it's Japanese.

The $1 billion brand faded. The factory kept winning.
