## Primary Caption

SKY universities are not just a ranking.

In Korea, SKY means:

SNU.
Korea University.
Yonsei University.

Together, they became a shorthand for elite education, exam pressure, family ambition, and social status.

SNU often feels like the academic peak.
Korea University carries tradition, loyalty, and school spirit.
Yonsei is often imagined as global, polished, and urban.

But the most important point is this:
SKY is powerful, but it is not the only path.

Korea has many strong universities outside SKY.
Still, the SKY label can affect jobs, networks, family conversations, and how people read someone's background.

That is why three university names can explain so much about Korean society.
