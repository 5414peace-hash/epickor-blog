## Primary Caption

Your first pojangmacha starts before you sit down. 🌙

Walk the row once. Look for open seats, visible prices, and a menu you can understand. Start with one hot, shareable dish, then confirm the portion, price, and payment method before ordering seafood or another round.

Inside the tent, keep your bags compact, film briefly, and remember that one person may be running the kitchen, tables, and payment all at once.

Save this for a Jongno night—and share it with the friend who always sits at the first open stool.
