## Primary Caption

Your first Korean pojangmacha should feel exciting, not confusing.

Walk the row before sitting. Check the menu and prices. Start with one hot, shareable dish. Ask whether cards work before the second round, especially at a small street tent.

The best etiquette is simple: use the space thoughtfully, film briefly, and remember that the host is running a kitchen, dining room, and cash desk at the same time.

Save this for a Jongno night in Seoul.
