LG quit. The factory didn't.

For 20 years, a no-name factory in Yangsan built LG's rice cookers. When the 1997 crisis made LG abandon the category, the subcontractor bet everything on its own name — in a market where 98% of homes already owned one.

Today 70%+ of Korea cooks rice on a Cuckoo. The cookers literally talk. And the quiet twist: the rental business now out-earns the rice cookers.
