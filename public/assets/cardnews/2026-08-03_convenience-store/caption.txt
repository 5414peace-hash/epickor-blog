Breakfast in Korea costs ₩3,000.

One triangle gimbap and one banana milk. That is the whole thing, and it is what the people around you in the shop are actually buying. Prices checked August 2026.

• 삼각김밥 Samgak gimbap — ₩1,200–1,900. The seaweed is kept apart from the rice inside the wrapper. Pull 1, then 2, then 3 — the numbers are printed on it, and out of order it falls apart in your hand.
• 연세우유 크림빵 Yonsei Cream Bread — ₩2,600–3,000, CU only, over 100 million sold. Roughly 80% cream by weight. Koreans put it in the freezer and eat it half-frozen.
• 바나나맛우유 Banana milk — ₩1,800, often ₩1,200 on promotion. The bottle is shaped after a Korean moon jar and has not changed since 1974.
• 박카스 F Bacchus F — ₩1,000–1,500. There are two Bacchus. Above 1,000mg of taurine it is legally a quasi-drug, so Bacchus D is pharmacy-only. The smaller bottle is the stronger one.
• 마감 할인 — gimbap, sandwiches and lunchboxes get marked down from early evening. The label on the shelf edge changes, not the price on the packet.
