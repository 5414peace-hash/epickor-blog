Seoul underground shopping can feel confusing because it is not always one obvious "mall."

Sometimes it is a huge polished complex.
Sometimes it is a station corridor full of quick fashion shops.
Sometimes it is a long bargain route where every row starts to look the same after 20 minutes.

The easiest rule:

COEX for comfort.
Gangnam Station for trends.
Goto Mall for bargains.
Hongdae for street culture, not a major underground mall.

That one distinction saves a lot of wrong-station shopping.
