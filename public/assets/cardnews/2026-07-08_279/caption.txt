Korean seaweed snacks are not one thing. Gim is a whole system. 🍙🌊🎁

Some sheets are for hot rice, some are for snacking, some are for gimbap, and some are best treated as easy souvenirs.

If you only buy one type, you may end up with the wrong texture for the way you actually eat.

Save this before your next Korean grocery or souvenir run.
