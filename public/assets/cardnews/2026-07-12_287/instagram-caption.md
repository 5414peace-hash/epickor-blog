## Primary Caption

The best home Korean BBQ grill is the one you use a second time. 🔥

Start with your room, not the product photo. Check ventilation, grease drainage, removable parts, table space, sink size, and storage before chasing maximum heat or a “smokeless” label.

Choose an electric tabletop grill for social cooking and easier controls. Choose a grill pan when compact storage and stove heat matter more. Keep charcoal outdoors—carbon monoxide is never an indoor shortcut.
