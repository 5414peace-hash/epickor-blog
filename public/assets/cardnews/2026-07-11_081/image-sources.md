# Image Sources - Card News 081

Topic: Why Korean Baseball Feels Like a Three-Hour Concert

All seven final assets are unique derivative files made from four EpicKor post-owned Korean baseball photos from practical spin-off Blog 233. The clean source set was selected because Blog 081's older video frames contain baked-in Reel captions that conflict with carousel text.

- Card 01: four-photo composite derived from Blog 233's Jamsil stadium, Gocheok cheering, stadium interior, and supporter-section images.
- Card 02: derived from `public/assets/images/posts/233/doosan-lg-game-jamsil.jpg`.
- Card 03: derived from `public/assets/images/posts/233/gocheok-dome-cheer.jpg`.
- Card 04: derived from `public/assets/images/posts/233/jamsil-baseball-supporters.jpg`.
- Card 05: derived from `public/assets/images/posts/233/jamsil-baseball-interior.jpg`.
- Card 06: alternate right-focused crop of `public/assets/images/posts/233/jamsil-baseball-supporters.jpg`; distinct from Card 04 and used to explain participation inside the same supporter section.
- Card 07: alternate left-focused crop of `public/assets/images/posts/233/doosan-lg-game-jamsil.jpg`; distinct from Card 02 and used to close on the full stadium experience.

No `image:` path is repeated within the carousel or in an existing card-news script.
