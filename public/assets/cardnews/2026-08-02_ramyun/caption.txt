Korea's scariest noodle is about one jalapeño.

Original Buldak measures 4,404 Scoville. A jalapeño runs 2,500 to 8,000. Swipe for the whole shelf, by the numbers.

• Ansungtangmyun — 570 SHU, and it isn't a milder Shin Ramyun. It's a soybean paste soup.
• Shin Ramyun — 3,400 SHU. It was 2,900 from 1986 to 2016 and was raised once, in 2017.
• The "1,320 then 2,700" ladder all over Korean forums? Those figures came from Paldo, a competitor, marketing against it.
• Buldak — 4,404, then 10,000, then 13,200. Only the last one is genuinely serrano territory.
• Chapaghetti — no chili at all. Black bean.
