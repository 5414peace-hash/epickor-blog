The hottest thing in Korea is not the noodle.

Original Buldak measures 4,404 Scoville. The cheongyang pepper (청양고추) Korean cooks slice into stews runs 10,000 to 23,000 — above every packet on this list. Swipe for the whole shelf, by the numbers.

• Ansungtangmyun (안성탕면) — 570 SHU, and it isn't a milder Shin Ramyun. It's a soybean paste soup. The powder is brown, not red.
• Shin Ramyun (신라면) — 3,400 SHU. It was 2,900 from 1986 to 2016 and has been raised exactly once, in 2017.
• That "1,320 then 2,700" ladder on Korean forums came from Paldo — a competitor, marketing against it.
• Buldak (불닭볶음면) — 4,404, then 10,000, then 13,200. No broth at all: you drain the noodles and stir the sauce through.
• Chapaghetti (짜파게티) — no chili at all. Black bean, and the powder in the packet is nearly black.
