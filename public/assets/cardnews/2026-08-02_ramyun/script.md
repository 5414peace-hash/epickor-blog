## Card 02 - The Reference Point
kicker: SET YOUR BASELINE
image: sources/card_06-ramen-chopsticks.jpg
image_label: Korean ramen lifted with chopsticks
shu_label: 2,500–8,000

**Main:** ONE JALAPEÑO
**Sub:** Keep this range in your head. Almost every Korean noodle compares back to it, and most of them land underneath.
note: Scoville measures capsaicin. It does not measure how hot a dish feels — fat, sugar and broth all change that.

## Card 03 - The Mild One
kicker: MILDEST ON THE SHELF
image: sources/card_01-ansungtangmyun-powder.jpg
image_label: Ansungtangmyun noodle block beside its brown soup powder
shu: 570

**Main:** ANSUNGTANGMYUN\n안성탕면
**Sub:** Not a milder Shin Ramyun. A different dish — the soup is built on soybean paste, not chili.
note: The powder is brown, not red. That colour is the entire answer.

## Card 04 - The Famous One
kicker: THE BENCHMARK
image: sources/card_03-shin-ramyun-powder.jpg
image_label: Shin Ramyun noodle block with red-orange soup powder
shu: 3400

**Main:** SHIN RAMYUN\n신라면
**Sub:** 2,900 SHU from 1986 to 2016, raised once in 2017. It got spicier — once, by about 17%.
note: The "1,320 then 2,700" ladder all over Korean forums? Those figures were published by Paldo, a competitor, while marketing against it.

## Card 05 - The Ladder
kicker: THE BULDAK LADDER
image: sources/card_04-spicy-ramen-bowl.jpg
image_label: A bowl of spicy Korean instant noodles with vegetables
shu: 13200

**Main:** BULDAK GOES\nUP IN THREES
**Sub:** Original 4,404, then 2x Spicy at 10,000, then 3x Haek — the hottest official version. Only the last is genuinely serrano.
note: Start with Carbo Buldak. The cream blunts the same sauce, which is why it became the export that carried the brand.

## Card 06 - Not Spicy At All
kicker: NO CHILI HERE
image: sources/card_05-seafood-ramyeon-bowl.jpg
image_label: Seafood ramyeon in a stainless bowl with a Korean spoon
shu_label: NOT SPICY

**Main:** CHAPAGHETTI\n짜파게티
**Sub:** Black bean, savoury and slightly sweet. The packet everyone forgets when they are busy arguing about heat.
note: One bagged packet is about ₩1,000 at a convenience store. Five-packs at a mart cost markedly less per serving.
