## Card 02 - The Mild One
kicker: MILDEST ON THE SHELF

image_label: Nongshim Ansungtangmyun
fit: contain
name_ko: 안성탕면
name_en: ANSUNG\nTANGMYUN
shu: 570

**Main:** ANSUNGTANGMYUN
**Sub:** Not a milder Shin Ramyun. A different dish — the soup is built on soybean paste, not chili.
note: The soup powder is brown, not red. That colour is the entire answer.

## Card 03 - The Benchmark
kicker: THE ONE EVERYTHING IS MEASURED AGAINST
image: sources/card_03-shin-ramyun-pack.png
image_label: Nongshim Shin Ramyun
fit: contain
name_ko: 신라면
name_en: SHIN RAMYUN
shu: 3400

**Main:** SHIN RAMYUN
**Sub:** 2,900 SHU from 1986 to 2016, raised once in 2017. It has got hotter exactly once, by about 17%.
note: The "1,320 then 2,700" ladder on Korean forums came from Paldo — a competitor, marketing against it.

## Card 04 - The Famous One
kicker: THE ONE FROM THE VIDEOS
image: sources/card_04-buldak-pack.png
image_label: Samyang Buldak Bokkeum-myun
fit: contain
name_ko: 불닭볶음면
name_en: BULDAK\nBOKKEUM-MYUN
shu: 4404

**Main:** BULDAK
**Sub:** No broth at all. You drain the noodles and stir the sauce through, so it coats instead of diluting.
note: 4,404 SHU is roughly one jalapeño. The crying-face videos are about volume and speed, not Scoville.

## Card 05 - The Ladder
kicker: THE BULDAK LADDER
image: sources/card_05-buldak-cooked.jpg
image_label: Cooked Buldak — no soup in the bowl
shu: 13200

**Main:** IT GOES UP\nIN THREES
**Sub:** Original 4,404, then 2x Spicy at 10,000, then 3x Haek at 13,200 — the hottest one Samyang sells.
note: Even 3x Haek stays inside one cheongyang pepper's range.

## Card 06 - No Chili At All
kicker: THE PACKET NOBODY ARGUES ABOUT
image: sources/card_06-chapagetti-pack.png
image_label: Nongshim Chapaghetti
fit: contain
name_ko: 짜파게티
name_en: CHAPAGHETTI
shu_label: NOT SPICY

**Main:** CHAPAGHETTI
**Sub:** Black bean, savoury and slightly sweet. The powder in the packet is nearly black, and there is no chili in it.
note: About ₩1,000 a bag at a convenience store. Five-packs at a mart cost markedly less per serving.
