Why do Koreans ask your blood type?

Not because they need your medical chart.

In Korea, blood type became a familiar personality shortcut:

Type A: careful and considerate.
Type B: free, passionate, but messy in dating jokes.
Type O: social and ambitious.
Type AB: mysterious, genius, or hard to read.

It is not science.
It is cultural vocabulary.

Today MBTI is newer, but the habit is older: Korea loves labels that make people easier to read.
