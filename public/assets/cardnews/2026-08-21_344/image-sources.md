# 2026-08-21_344 — Bacchus D vs F, image sources

Visual system: **spec-split** (`render-specsplit.py`), new for this batch. A pale ground, the product
large on its own stage, and the numbers beside it as labelled chips.

Not a repeat, and deliberately the opposite of the two systems made this week: `ticket-stub`
(2026-08-20) and `location-slate` (2026-08-21) are both dark, full-bleed photographs with a plate
over them. This one is **light and cutout-led**, which is what a two-object comparison needs — the
whole story is that D and F look almost the same, and that only reads if both bottles sit on a clean
ground at true relative size. `shelf-tag` is a price tag and `rx-label` is a pharmacy label; neither
leads with the object.

**The composite is the reason this carousel works.** Card 01's claim — the smaller bottle is the
stronger one — is a claim about relative size, and no single stock photograph makes it. Both cutouts
were trimmed and scaled so 100 mL and 120 mL appear at their true proportion rather than at equal
frame height, which would have quietly reversed the point.

## Subject checks
Cards 02, 03, 06 and 07 declare `name_ko`/`name_en` and are checked against their `image_label` by
`review-cardnews.mjs`. Card 04 names the 1,000 mg legal line rather than a product, and carries an F
bottle because F is the product that sits exactly on that line — the card says so.

## Rejected
- **Photographs of Korean pharmacies from Commons.** The best available are of a *museum* pharmacy
  (옵티마 웰니스 뮤지엄 약국), which is a display rather than a working 약국 with green-cross signage.
  A card telling readers to walk into a pharmacy should not illustrate it with an exhibit.
- **Generic energy-drink and taurine stock photography.** This carousel names one brand throughout;
  an unbranded bottle would be the "plausible but not the product" substitution the standard rejects.

## Cross-post uniqueness
No stock photo IDs used. Cards 01–03 and 07 are manufacturer material new to EpicKor; 04–06 are the
source post's own images and appear in no other carousel.
