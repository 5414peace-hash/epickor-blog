Korea's most famous energy drink comes in two versions, and the smaller bottle is the stronger one. 🧪

박카스 has been on Korean counters since 1963, and almost every English-language page treats it as one product. It is two.

🔵 박카스D — 100 mL, 2,000 mg taurine, 약국 (pharmacy) only
🔵 박카스F — 120 mL, 1,000 mg taurine, convenience stores and marts

Read that twice. Bacchus D gives you 20% less liquid and twice the active ingredient. If you assumed the bigger bottle was the upgrade, you had it exactly backwards.

The reason is not marketing. It is a number in the law.

Under Korea's Pharmaceutical Affairs Act, a drink carrying more than 1,000 mg of taurine is a 의약외품 that only pharmacies may sell. D sits at 2,000, so it stays behind the counter. F sits at exactly 1,000 — the highest it can be and still reach a convenience store.

And before 2011 there was no convenience-store Bacchus at all. It was classed as a general pharmaceutical, pharmacy-only, full stop. That single reclassification is what put it on ordinary shelves.

What to actually do:
✅ Just visiting? Buy F. It is everywhere, it is cheap, it needs no conversation.
✅ Want the strong one? Walk into any 약국 and say 박카스 디 주세요. No prescription, no explaining.
✅ Caffeine-sensitive? There is a green 디카페 bottle too.
