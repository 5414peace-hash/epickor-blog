## Both charts carry arguments, not decoration

The first is the article's thesis in one frame: a 1990 brief — nothing on your lips, nothing in
your teeth, no weight — set against the 2026 consumer verdict that the original fails all three.
That contrast is the piece.

The second exists because the practical decision here is **format before flavour**. One product
name covers 47 to 313 calories, a six-fold range, and the mini is the only size that delivers what
the name promises.
