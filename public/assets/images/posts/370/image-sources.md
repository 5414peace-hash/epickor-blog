## Notes

- **The two sources do different jobs and both are needed.** Commons has the contents — crushed
  noodle and the seasoning pile, which is the only way to show how small the sachet is relative to a
  ramyun soup packet — but **no pack shot at any resolution**. Ottogi's store has the bags but not
  the contents. Neither source alone covers the article.
- `ppushu-bulgogi-pack.jpg` is the hero because the character on the bag is **raising a mallet over
  a noodle block**, which states the product's entire instruction without a caption.
- The two Ottogi files are 750×750 native and were resampled up to 1050 px with Lanczos. That is a
  1.4× upscale, inside tolerance for an inline product image; neither is used at full-bleed width.
