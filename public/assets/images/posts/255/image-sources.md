## Removed 2026-08-24

- `lotte-food-avenue-seating-counters.jpg` — **removed because it contradicted the article.**
  Source was a 2018 design-award entry (`restaurantandbardesignawards.com/entry/4380-lotte-food-avenue`,
  `fa_20180116_028.jpg`). The frame shows a casual food court: red metal chairs, laminate tables,
  a colour-blocked ceiling. The article's second paragraph states that the biggest mistake is
  "treating a Korean department store food hall like a generic mall cafeteria. It is not." The photo
  showed exactly that cafeteria. Automated checks passed it; only opening the file caught it.
  Replaced by the two VISITKOREA images above, both of which show a named subject the text discusses.
