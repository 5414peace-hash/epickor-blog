# Image sources — Blog 380 (KTX/SRT merger, 1 September 2026)

All three images are Wikimedia Commons files under Creative Commons licences that permit
commercial use with attribution. Attribution is carried in the visible caption on the page,
as required. No Pexels/Unsplash stock was used: this article names a specific operator,
a specific train type and a specific station, and the Blog Reference Image Standard requires
that exact subject rather than a plausible Korean rail scene.

## 1. `srt-train-becomes-ktx-sancheon.jpg` (hero, ogImage, thumbnail)

- **Source:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:SRT_Class_120000.jpg
- **Author:** Hajoon0102
- **Licence:** CC BY-SA 4.0
- **Original:** 9248x6936, 15.5 MB. Delivered at 1400x1050, 254 KB.
- **Why this one:** the article's subject is a brand disappearing. This frame carries the
  maroon **SRT** wordmark large and legible on the nose, unit 209, **and** a blue Korail
  commuter set on the adjacent track — the two operators in one frame, which is the merger
  in a single picture. Nothing on Pexels or Unsplash shows an identifiable SRT set at all.

## 2. `suseo-station-hall.jpg`

- **Source:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Suseo_SRT_Station_hall.jpg
- **Author:** Minseong Kim
- **Licence:** CC BY-SA 4.0
- **Original:** 5184x3456, 10.7 MB. Delivered at 1400x933, 256 KB.
- **Why this one:** Suseo is the station the article says most visitors have never used, so a
  photograph of it is the point rather than decoration. The banner reading
  **"수서역 SRT 개통을 축하합니다"** (congratulating the SRT's opening) and the START SRT signage
  are legible, which gives the caption something true and slightly poignant to say: that
  launch is exactly what is now being folded back into Korail. Shot December 2016, at opening.

## 3. `srt-standard-class-seats.jpg`

## Checks run

- **Cross-post uniqueness:** all three are Wikimedia Commons files, first use on this site.
  No Commons file in `public/assets/images/posts/` or `public/assets/images/business/`
  duplicates these. (The Pexels-ID audit in `scripts/audit-image-uniqueness.mjs` keys off
  `photos/{id}` URLs and does not cover Commons; recorded here by full file URL instead.)
- **Korea-first:** all three shot in Korea, of Korean rolling stock and a Korean station,
  with Korean signage visible in two of them.
- **Size:** 131-256 KB each, 641 KB for the post. Under the 1 MB per-post ceiling.
- **Licence:** CC BY-SA 3.0/4.0 all permit commercial use with attribution. Attribution
  appears in the visible caption of each image. ShareAlike applies to derivative works of
  the image; the images are used unmodified apart from resizing and are not incorporated
  into a new combined work, so no ShareAlike obligation attaches to the article text.

## Considered and rejected

- `KTX-Sancheon on Suseo–Pyeongtaek high-speed railway.jpg` (CC BY 2.0, Masakazu Matsumoto) —
  thematically the best match in the whole search, since it shows a KTX-Sancheon on the Suseo
  line, i.e. the article's future state. Downloaded and inspected, then **dropped**: at
  24365x7865 it is a five-strip rolling-stock roster composite, not a photograph of a train in
  a scene. Any crop tall enough to use includes fragments of the neighbouring strips, which
  read as separate trains. Do not re-download it without looking at it first.
