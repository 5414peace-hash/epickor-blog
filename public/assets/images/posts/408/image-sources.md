## Why no photograph

No free-licensed photograph of this product exists that we can use.

- **Pexels**: searches for "samanco", "fish ice cream", "taiyaki ice cream" return **taiyaki
  cones** — a filled fish-shaped waffle cone. Samanco is a flat monaka sandwich. Using one
  would be the 2026-08-03 카드뉴스 defect: an image that contradicts the article naming it.
  Rejected.
- **Wikimedia Commons**: `Samanco` returns files about the **district of Samanco in Peru**
  (Ancash region). Nothing related. Rejected.
- **Manufacturer**: Binggrae's product pages are JS shells; no directly fetchable product
  render was obtained. Not used.

Both charts carry information a photograph could not: a name etymology and a retail-format
price spread are not photographable subjects.

## Cross-post uniqueness
No stock photo IDs used, so nothing to collide with. `audit:image-uniqueness` has nothing
to key off for this post by design.
