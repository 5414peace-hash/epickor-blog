## Why these were needed

The sourcing waterfall in CLAUDE.md was run in full on 2026-08-05 and failed at tiers 0 and 1:

- **Tier 0, manufacturer.** `lottewellfood.com` renders its product catalogue client-side, so nothing
  is scrapable; `/brand/hero/kancho` and every spelling variant return **HTTP 500**. `lotteconf.co.kr`
  redirects to the same site. No product API is exposed.
- **Tier 1, Wikimedia Commons.** **Zero results** at any resolution across four queries — `칸초`,
  `Lotte Kancho`, `Kancho biscuit Korea`, `롯데 칸초`.

Tier 3 — asking the representative — is what unblocked it.

## Editorial notes

- `kancho-printed-biscuits.jpg` is the hero and the article's evidence. It is the only image that
  proves the claim in the title, and no equivalent exists in any free-licensed archive.
- `kancho-lotty-lorry-box.jpg` is the most historically useful of the four. The 로티·로리 raccoons
  place the design between the 1990s and the mid-2000s, which lets the article date packaging by
  mascot generation — a check a reader can actually run on a box in front of them.
- The cup's **95 g / 460 kcal** is read directly off the package in the photo. Lotte's own September
  2025 campaign materials listed the cup at 88 g, so cup sizes have varied by run; the article cites
  the campaign figures for the campaign and does not present either number as the single truth.
