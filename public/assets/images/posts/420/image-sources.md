## Caption corrections made before publishing, 2026-08-22

I wrote two captions from the file names before opening the images, and both were wrong — the same
failure this repo keeps catching in older posts, this time caught in a new one:

- The Asiana caption said the aircraft was **taxiing** and described a "grey and red livery." It is
  **parked on stand** with ground equipment and a marshaller in frame, and the livery is white with
  the multicoloured striped tail. Corrected.
- The Air Premia caption said the aircraft was **at** Incheon and had a "dark blue tail." It is
  **airborne on departure with the gear still down**, and the tail is dark with two red discs.
  Corrected.

Both alt texts were rewritten to match. The lesson is narrow and worth repeating: a Commons file
name tells you the airline, the type and the airport. It does not tell you what the aircraft is
doing, and that is usually what the caption claims.
