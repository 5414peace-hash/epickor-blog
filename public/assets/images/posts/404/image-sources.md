## A storefront photograph was found, and deliberately not used

Pexels `23325485` is a genuine Korean neighbourhood diner with photo-menu boards outside — a very
good visual match for the format this article describes. It was rejected because the sign reads
**원조 (명동) 남산 왕 돈까스**, not 김밥천국. The article names a specific shop name, so showing a
different shop would be the "plausible Korean scene" substitution the reference standard forbids,
and captioning around it ("not actually a 김밥천국, but…") is the caption-apology pattern that is a
hard reject on its own.

The chart took that slot instead, and it carries the article's second argument — that the byword
for cheap food has doubled in price since 2014 — better than a photograph could.

`16842099` shows real Korean gimbap with the standard fillings visible: 단무지, spinach, ham,
carrot and egg. Checked against the uniqueness audit and unused site-wide, as were the rejected
alternatives `31346218`, `23325485` and `21576130`.
