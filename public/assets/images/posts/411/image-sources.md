## Why no photograph

- **Pexels / Unsplash**: no 카스타드 product exists in either. Searches return generic
  sponge-cake and custard-tart photography — a different food. Using one would show a product
  the article does not name.
- **Wikimedia Commons**: no usable files for either brand's custard cake.
- **Lotte Wellfood / Orion**: both are JS-rendered brand sites and the imagery is commercial
  brand material for two competing companies. A packshot of one and not the other would also
  read as an endorsement in a post whose subject is which brand is the original. Not used.

The comparison chart carries the article's actual claim — two launch dates and two egg
percentages side by side — which no single photograph could.
