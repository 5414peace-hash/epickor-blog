## Why no photograph

- **Pexels / Unsplash**: "tornado potato" and "spiral potato" return **non-Korean fair-food
  photographs** — American county fairs and Southeast Asian night markets. The entire thesis of
  this post is that the product is Korean and is read as generic; illustrating it with a Texas
  fair photo would argue the opposite of the article. Rejected on Korea-first grounds.
- **Wikimedia Commons**: files exist under "tornado potato" but are predominantly non-Korean
  street scenes with the same problem.
- **(주)회오리 / twistpotato.com**: product imagery is the company's commercial brand material.
  Not used.

The charts carry what a photograph cannot here: an invention timeline, a trademark distinction
visible only on signage, and a five-year revenue and farm-supply series.
