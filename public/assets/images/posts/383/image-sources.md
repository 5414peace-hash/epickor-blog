# Image sources — Blog 383 (buying luggage in Seoul)

Two Wikimedia Commons photographs, both shot inside Namdaemun Market itself.
Stock libraries have "Asian market" imagery; they do not have this market with its
building signage legible, and the article's whole point is that Namdaemun is a set of
numbered buildings rather than one shop.

## 1. `namdaemun-market-street.jpg` (hero, ogImage)

- **Source:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Namdaemun_shopping_street.jpg
- **Author:** Sgroey
- **Licence:** CC BY-SA 4.0
- **Original:** 4032x3024. Delivered 1400x1050, 315 KB.
- **Why:** the market as a visitor first meets it — awnings, stalls, Korean shop signs,
  real crowd. The caption uses it to make the article's first practical point: the bag
  buildings are not on this street.

## 2. `namdaemun-market-alley.jpg`

- **Source:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Namdaemun_Market_Alley.jpg
- **Author:** Adbar
- **Licence:** CC BY-SA 3.0
- **Original:** 2592x3872. Delivered 1400x2091, 444 KB.
- **Why:** the sign reading **남대문중앙상가 C동** is legible in frame, which is exactly the
  addressing convention the article explains — shops here give a building, a letter, a
  floor and a unit rather than a street address. The photo is the evidence for the claim.
- Portrait orientation, kept as shot. Per the 2026-08-02 representative ruling, vertical
  images stay vertical rather than being cropped to landscape.

## Considered and dropped

`Seoul-metro-425-Hoehyeon-station-entrance-7-20181125-080818.jpg` (CC BY-SA 4.0, LERK)
was downloaded and inspected. It is a clean shot of a Hoehyeon Station entrance and the
number **7** is large and legible — but the article sends readers to **Exit 5**. Publishing
a photo of the wrong exit beside that instruction invites exactly the mistake the article
exists to prevent, so it was deleted rather than captioned around. If a photo of Exit 5
appears on Commons later it is worth adding.

## Checks run

- **Cross-post uniqueness:** first use of both files on this site.
- **Korea-first:** both shot in Namdaemun, Korean signage dominant in each.
- **Size:** 315 KB and 444 KB, 759 KB for the post. Under the 1 MB per-post ceiling, though
  the alley shot is at the upper end because portrait at 1400px wide carries more pixels.
- **Captions:** each says what the photograph shows and credits the photographer. Neither
  caption explains away a weak image — the alley shot was chosen *because* of its signage,
  not despite anything.
