# Image sources — Blog 384 (Gwangjang Market vintage arcade)

Two Wikimedia Commons photographs of Gwangjang Market itself.

**Worth recording: Commons has this market almost entirely as food.** Searching
`Gwangjang Market` and `광장시장` returns yukhoe, banchan stalls, pancakes and "Eats"
series shots — which is precisely the article's thesis, that the famous half of the
market has crowded out the other half even in the photographic record. There is no
Commons photograph of the second-floor 구제상가 at all.

## 1. `gwangjang-food-alley-second-floor.jpg` (hero, ogImage)

- **Source:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gwangjang_Market.JPG
- **Author:** ChongDae
- **Licence:** CC BY-SA 3.0
- **Original:** 5472x3648. Delivered 1400x933, 222 KB.
- **Why this one, out of a dozen food shots:** it is the only candidate where the
  **second storey is visible above the awnings** — windows, railings, an 임대 sign. The
  article's whole point is that there is another market directly above the famous one,
  and this frame shows both at once. The caption instructs the reader to look up.

## 2. `gwangjang-market-entrance.jpg`

- **Source:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gwangjang_Market,_Seoul_01.jpg
- **Author:** Bgag
- **Licence:** **CC0**
- **Original:** 5184x3456. Delivered 1400x933, 230 KB.
- **Why:** the 광장시장 / KWANG JANG MARKET signage is large and legible, so a reader
  arriving from Jongno 5-ga Exit 8 can recognise the building. CC0, so no attribution is
  legally required — credited anyway, as house practice.

## What is deliberately missing

**There is no photograph of the vintage arcade in this article**, because no
openly-licensed one exists. The options were to publish a generic "rack of used clothes"
stock image or to publish neither. Per the Blog Reference Image Standard a merely
plausible scene fails, and a caption that has to explain away what it is showing is a
hard reject — so the article carries two honest photographs of the building and describes
the floor in words instead.

**If someone shoots the 구제상가 for EpicKor, that photo is worth more than anything else
this post could add.** Red 수입구제 signage, the narrow staircase between fabric stalls,
and a stall interior would each fill a real gap. Logged for the next Seoul shoot.
