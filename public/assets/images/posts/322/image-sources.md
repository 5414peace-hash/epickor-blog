## Honest labelling note

These are **not** documented as photographed in Korea, and the captions deliberately do not claim
they were. They are captioned by what they actually show — a man doing a face routine — because
the article is about a routine and a market, not about a Korean location. Per the CLAUDE.md
Korea-first rule this is the correct handling: the rule bars passing off visibly foreign scenes as
Korean, and these carry no conflicting country signals (no foreign signage, branding, or
streetscape).

Rejected during selection: Pexels 12721805 (COSRX bottles) — genuinely Korean-brand product
imagery and a strong topical fit, but already in use on Blog 312, so it fails the cross-post
uniqueness rule.
