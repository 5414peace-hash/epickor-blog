# Image sources for Blog 334 (Korea's Pet Funeral Industry)

This is a sensitive topic - no real, identifiable grieving-pet-owner or Korean funeral-facility
interior photo was used, since sourcing that without consent would be inappropriate. Selected
tasteful, real, non-exploitative photography instead.

- `memorial-candle-flowers.jpg` (hero/og) - A burning candle with white flowers and an open book,
  warm minimalist still life. Pexels, photographer Esra Korkmaz, standard Pexels free license:
  https://www.pexels.com/photo/candle-and-flowers-18809839/
- `chrysanthemums-candlelight.jpg` - White chrysanthemums with candlelight (chrysanthemums carry
  memorial/funeral connotation in Korea too). Pexels, photographer Nati, standard Pexels free
  license: https://www.pexels.com/photo/elegant-still-life-with-white-chrysanthemums-and-candlelight-29420711/
- `hand-holding-dog-paw.jpg` - Close-up of a human hand gently holding a dog's paw, illustrating
  the companion-animal bond without being morbid. Pexels, photographer Juliane Monari Fotografia,
  standard Pexels free license: https://www.pexels.com/photo/person-hand-holding-dog-paw-20195994/

No Pexels photo IDs checked against audit-image-uniqueness.mjs conflict with existing site usage.

Rejected during selection: any image depicting an actual Korean pet funeral facility interior or
an identifiable grieving owner - none was sourced, by design, since real photography of that kind
would require consent this article does not have. A Jindo dog photo was considered for a
"changing relationship with pets" intro beat but deliberately NOT reused here since Blog 333
(published the same day) already uses three different Jindo Commons/Pexels photos - reusing any
of them here would violate the site's cross-post image uniqueness rule.
