# Image Sources — Blog 375 (Korea's Exam Day / Suneung)

Four Wikimedia Commons files. Three come from the official Korea.net / KOCIS photo set shot at
**Kyungbock High School, Jongno-gu, Seoul on 12 November 2014** — real exam-day press photographs,
not stock. The fourth is the exam's required writing tools.

**Selection method matters here.** All eleven files in the Korea.net exam-day series carry an
*identical* Commons description, so the filenames and metadata say nothing about what is in any
given frame. Every candidate was downloaded and viewed before selection, per the rule recorded on
`372`. Choosing by filename would have been guessing.

- `suneung-parents-at-school-gate.jpg` — hero/OG.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Korea_College_Scholastic_Ability_Test_Day_01_(15592526927).jpg
  License: **CC BY-SA 2.0**, Korea.net / Korean Culture and Information Service.
  Original 3568×2164 → 1500px wide, 131KB.
  Parents behind the closed gate railings, one man mid-prayer. Chosen as hero because the article's
  subject is not the test paper — it is what the country does around it, and this is the frame that
  carries that. The railings partly obscure faces, which is the right level of exposure for a photo
  of private people at a public event; no one is named in the caption.

- `suneung-juniors-saluting-seniors.jpg` — the street-scene section.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Korea_College_Scholastic_Ability_Test_Day_11_(15592257588).jpg
  License: **CC BY-SA 2.0**, Korea.net / Korean Culture and Information Service.
  Original 4336×2472 → 1400px wide, 106KB.
  Uniformed second-years saluting third-years in. This is the specific behaviour the paragraph
  describes, photographed as it happens.

- `suneung-school-gate-crowd-aerial.jpg` — the street-scene section.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Korea_College_Scholastic_Ability_Test_Day_05_(15753986876).jpg
  License: **CC BY-SA 2.0**, Korea.net / Korean Culture and Information Service.
  Original 2832×4165 → 1000px wide, 247KB (the heaviest of the eight; the elevated view needs the
  resolution to stay readable).
  Elevated view of the whole gate scene: crowd, banners, mascot, broadcast trucks, autumn mountain
  behind the school. Included because it establishes scale in a way the tight frames cannot.

- `suneung-computer-sign-pen.jpg` — the closing detail.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:A_scantron_pen_and_a_mechanical_pencil_for_The_South_Korea_College_Scholastic_Ability_Test,_2018.jpg
  License: **CC BY 4.0**, 고려. Original 5312×2988 → 1300px wide, 22KB.
  The 컴퓨터용 사인펜 and mechanical pencil, i.e. the exact objects the exam requires. This is the
  "that specific thing" image rather than a generic desk shot.

**People in frame.** These are official government public-information photographs of a public,
heavily press-covered event, released under CC BY-SA specifically for reuse. Captions describe the
scene and name no individual. Same standard applied to the visible vendors in `372`'s market photo.

Cross-post uniqueness: all four are first use on EpicKor, and no other post covers Suneung at all —
verified 2026-08-10 by grepping `content/blog/*.md` for `suneung` and `CSAT`, which returned nothing.
The site-wide audit script keys off Pexels-style `photos/{id}` patterns that Commons `File:` URLs do
not contain, so these were checked by filename against `public/assets/images/`.
