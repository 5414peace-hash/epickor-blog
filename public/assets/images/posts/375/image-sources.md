Four Wikimedia Commons files. Three come from the official Korea.net / KOCIS photo set shot at
**Kyungbock High School, Jongno-gu, Seoul on 12 November 2014** — real exam-day press photographs,
not stock. The fourth is the exam's required writing tools.

**Selection method matters here.** All eleven files in the Korea.net exam-day series carry an
*identical* Commons description, so the filenames and metadata say nothing about what is in any
given frame. Every candidate was downloaded and viewed before selection, per the rule recorded on
`372`. Choosing by filename would have been guessing.

- `suneung-parents-at-school-gate.jpg` — hero/OG.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Korea_College_Scholastic_Ability_Test_Day_01_(15592526927).jpg
  License: **CC BY-SA 2.0**, Korea.net / Korean Culture and Information Service.
  Original 3568×2164 → 1500px wide, 131KB.
  Parents behind the closed gate railings, one man mid-prayer. Chosen as hero because the article's
  subject is not the test paper — it is what the country does around it, and this is the frame that
  carries that. The railings partly obscure faces, which is the right level of exposure for a photo
  of private people at a public event; no one is named in the caption.

- `suneung-juniors-saluting-seniors.jpg` — the street-scene section.

**People in frame.** These are official government public-information photographs of a public,
heavily press-covered event, released under CC BY-SA specifically for reuse. Captions describe the
scene and name no individual. Same standard applied to the visible vendors in `372`'s market photo.
