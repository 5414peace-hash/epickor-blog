## Notes

- Orion publishes product detail pages as very tall single JPEGs (a Korean e-commerce convention).
  Each file here is a **crop of a different region** of one of those strips, not a resize of the
  whole thing, so the two files taken from `2550547335.jpg` are different pictures — one is the
  Garlic Baguette pack with garlic bread, the other is a chip close-up showing the ridge depth.
- `sun-returned-hot-spicy-bag.jpg` is the hero because it carries the article's entire argument in
  one frame: **돌아온** (returned), the Latin **Sun**, and the Korean **썬** badge, all on the same bag.
- The catalogue thumbnail at `/upload/goods/3fc4589a6a6d430b4b52fa37670bb5bb.png` was rejected — it
  is only **468×468**, which would need a 2.6× upscale for a hero and would read soft.
