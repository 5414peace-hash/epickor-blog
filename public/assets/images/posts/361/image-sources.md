## The two packet photos are the article's argument

The mild-versus-spicy question is answered visually rather than asserted. Both photographs are by
the **same photographer, in the same setup, with the same lighting**, so the only variable is the
product: mild's soup powder is a pale sandy tan and spicy's is distinctly orange-red, while the
noodle block and vegetable flake are near-identical. That is the evidence for the article's claim
that these are one recipe with the chili dialled up rather than two recipes.

This is the same comparison-pair logic already used on Blog `346`, and the same photographer's
series — but **different files**. `346` uses the Ansungtangmyun and Shin Ramyun photographs; none
of the four files here has been used on any post. Checked against every existing
`image-sources.md` before download.

## Why an Ottogi image was needed as well

The Commons photographs show packet *contents*, not the bag. One of the article's target questions
is about **bag colour** — `jin ramen purple` / `blue` / `violet` are live English searches — so a
pack shot was required to show that the bag is yellow with a coloured accent, not a red or blue bag
as the search phrasing implies. Ottogi's own site was the correct source under the 0차 rule.

It is served as a transparent PNG. The first conversion flattened it onto black, which was caught
by looking at the file; it was re-downloaded and flattened onto white.

## Limitation, recorded honestly

Ottogi's product image is **625×500** — small, and used inline at modest size rather than as a
hero. No larger official file was reachable: `ottogi.co.kr/product/*` paths all return 404 and the
Ottogi mall is JavaScript-rendered. The mild bag's official pack shot could not be located at all,
so the article describes the blue accent in text rather than showing it. Worth another attempt if
Ottogi's site structure changes.
