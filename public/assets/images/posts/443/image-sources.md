## Why these

The argument is that the bowl asks three things at once — cold, chewy, no anchor flavour — so the frames
are the bowl itself (hero), the original form with raw fish, the gentler clear bowl foreigners rate "okay"
(photographed in Sokcho, where the first Hamheung shop opened in 1951), and the bare noodle that draws the
"tastes of nothing" complaint. Captions describe what is visibly in each frame.

Rejected: the three `Hamhung naengmyeon 20221209` frames (dark, low contrast on a wooden table), the KOCIS
bibim frame (1000 px), the 2005/2007 Flickr bowls (older, softer).
