# Image sources — Blog 140

Revised 2026-08-08. Two generic stock photos (a beige-tile accessible sign, a row of stainless
urinals) and a 262x349 crop of a Seoul government press image were removed. None of the three
carried a Korean marker, and the previous source record admitted as much — it described the stock
pair as "neutral explanatory visuals, not claims about a named Korean facility," which is exactly
the generic-proxy substitution the Blog Reference Image Standard rejects when a direct real
reference is available. Direct references were available.

- `korea-bin-free-toilet-sign.jpg` — Wikimedia Commons, "Toilet advice sign," public domain. An
  official Ministry of the Interior and Safety / Seoul Metropolitan Government / Hangang Park
  notice reading 「휴지통 없는 화장실 — 사용한 휴지는 변기에 버려주세요」 with English, Chinese
  and Japanese translations. This is the article's proof image for the 2018 bin-free policy.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Toilet_advice_sign.jpg
- `gusan-station-public-toilet.jpg` — Wikimedia Commons, "Gusan Station Public toilet," CC BY 3.0.
  A real restroom inside a Seoul subway station. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gusan_Station_Public_toilet.JPG
- `squat-toilet-korean-building.jpg` — Wikimedia Commons, "Squat toilet in Korean building
  2026-06 (1)," CC0, described by the uploader as 상가 화장실의 화변기. Photographed June 2026,
  which is itself the point: the article's new squat-toilet section needed evidence that these are
  current, not historical. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Squat_toilet_in_Korean_building_2026-06_(1).jpg
- `seoul-subway-platform-pexels.jpg` — Pexels photo 31892087. Retained: it shows Anguk Station on
  Line 3 with Korean platform signage, so it is Korea-specific rather than generic transit stock.
  Source: https://www.pexels.com/photo/31892087/
