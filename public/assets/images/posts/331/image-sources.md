# Image sources for Blog 331 (Korean Modern Art)

Kim Whanki (d. 1974) and Park Soo-keun (d. 1965) are both still under Korean copyright (70 years
post-death - protected until 2044 and 2035 respectively), and Lee Ufan is a living artist with
full copyright. Actual painting reproductions are therefore NOT used, even where a Commons upload
carries a CC tag on the photograph - that only covers the photographer's shot of the artwork, not
the artist's underlying copyright, which is an active gray zone not appropriate for a monetized
commercial article. Used real portrait/venue photography instead, all independently license-checked.

- `lee-ufan-portrait-2014.jpg` (hero/og) - Lee Ufan photographed at a Gary Tatintsian Gallery
  opening, standing beside one of his stone/steel Mono-ha works, Nov 13, 2014. Wikimedia Commons,
  photographer Andrew Tupalev, license CC BY-SA 4.0:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lee_Ufan.jpg
- `whanki-museum-exterior.jpg` - Exterior of the Whanki Museum, Buam-dong, Seoul, showing the
  actual "영원한 것들 / KIM WHANKI: The Immortal Nature" exhibition banner on the building. Wikimedia
  Commons, uploader PaigePhault, March 20, 2025, license CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication):
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Whanki_Museum.jpg
- `mmca-seoul-exterior.jpg` - Exterior of MMCA Seoul (National Museum of Modern and Contemporary
  Art, Korea, Seoul branch) with clear "서울 국립현대미술관" signage. Wikimedia Commons, photographer
  서울연구원 (Seoul Institute), Feb 18, 2020, license CC BY 4.0:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:MMCA_Seoul.jpg
- `leeum-museum-exterior.jpg` - Interior atrium of Leeum Museum of Art (Samsung), Seoul, with the
  "LEEUM / SAMSUNG MUSEUM OF ART" sign visible. Wikimedia Commons, photographer takato marui,
  Nov 23, 2006, license CC BY-SA 2.0:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Leeum,_Samsung_Museum_of_Art.jpg

All four checked against `scripts/audit-image-uniqueness.mjs` before use (Commons sources, not
Pexels IDs, so the photo-ID check does not apply the same way - confirmed manually that none of
these venues/subjects appear in any other post's image-sources.md).

Rejected during selection: two Dong-A Ilbo newspaper portrait photos of Kim Whanki (1935) and Park
Soo-keun (1965), both public domain in South Korea, were considered but dropped for image quality
(low resolution, heavy print-halftone degradation) rather than licensing. Painting-reproduction
files tagged CC BY-SA/CC BY 2.0 on Commons (e.g. File:Kim Whanki untitled 1.jpg, sourced from an
unreviewed 2011 Flickr upload) were rejected as a copyright gray zone - the license covers the
photograph, not Kim Whanki's underlying artwork copyright.
