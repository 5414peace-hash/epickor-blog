## Why no photograph

- **Pexels / Unsplash**: no 델라페 product exists in either library. Searches return generic
  iced-coffee and convenience-store interiors — category substitutes for a post that names a
  specific private-label product. Rejected under the 2026-08-03 rule (a card that names a
  product must show that product).
- **Wikimedia Commons**: no 델라페 / Delaffe files.
- **BGF Retail / CU**: product imagery is served from JS-rendered pages, and CU press images
  are supplied for press use. Not fetched, not used.

Both charts carry information a photograph cannot: a five-product price list and a fourteen-year
price curve are not photographable subjects.
