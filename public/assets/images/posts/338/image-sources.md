# Image sources for Blog 338 (Virtual K-pop Idols and AI Influencers)

PLAVE, MAVE:, Eternity, aespa's "ae-" avatars, and Isegye Idol are all agency/company-owned
virtual character designs. The character art itself is the copyrighted creative work here -
the same way a cartoon mascot or video game character is copyrighted independent of any single
photograph of it. Actual character renders, official promotional art, MV screenshots, or concert
photos showing the avatars are therefore NOT used, even where a Commons/social upload carries a
CC tag - that tag would only cover the uploader's rights to their specific file, not Blast/VLAST's,
Metaverse Entertainment's, SM's, or the respective agencies' underlying rights in the character
designs. This mirrors the reasoning already applied in Blog 331 (Korean Modern Art), which avoided
reproducing paintings by Kim Whanki, Park Soo-keun, and Lee Ufan for the same reason. Used real,
ordinary, non-character photography of genuine context/venues connected to the story instead, all
independently license-checked.

- `gocheok-sky-dome.jpg` (hero candidate) - Interior of Gocheok Sky Dome, Seoul, during a baseball
  game (high-school tournament banner "청룡기 고교야구 70년" visible), showing the dome's steel roof
  structure, seating bowl, and scoreboard. Gocheok Sky Dome is the real Seoul venue where Isegye
  Idol sold out a show - this photo establishes the real, physical venue without showing any
  performer, character, or event-specific branding. Wikimedia Commons, photographer/uploader
  Bigsmile20 ("own work"), Nov 12, 2015, license CC BY 3.0:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gocheok_Skydome_Inside.JPG
  - Note: two other Gocheok Sky Dome Commons files (exterior `File:Gocheok_Sky_Dome_(36530170325).jpg`
    and interior `File:Gocheok_Sky_Dome_inside_WBSC_Premier_12_2019.jpg`) are already used in Blog
    230's image-sources.md, so this post uses a different, previously-unused Commons file of the
    same real venue rather than reusing either of those exact files.
  - Rejected alternative: `File:경기장_-_panoramio.jpg` (Commons, CC BY 3.0, Bohao Zhao, 2013) shows
    the dome mid-construction with exposed unfinished steel framework - technically usable and
    unused elsewhere, but rejected because it would misrepresent the venue's current finished
    appearance to readers.
  - Rejected alternative: `File:Gocheok Sky Dome exterior.jpg` (a clean, modern 2023 exterior shot
    with "GOCHEOK SKY DOME" signage, CC BY 4.0, photographer Philharmonic, hosted on English
    Wikipedia) was visually the strongest exterior candidate found, but it carries an explicit
    `{{FoP-USonly}}`-style notice: "free content in the United States but non-free or potentially
    non-free in its country of origin" (South Korea lacks full freedom-of-panorama for contemporary
    architecture). Rejected in favor of a Commons-native file that does not carry that country-of-
    origin caveat, matching the precedent already set by Blog 230's two Gocheok images.

  official "Startup Seoul" ambassador by the Seoul mayor. Pexels, photographer Angelina Rodionow,
