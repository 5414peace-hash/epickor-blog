# 422 — 쓰리쎄븐 (Three Seven / 777) nail clippers, image sources

All four images come from the **manufacturer's own shop, i777shop.co.kr**, downloaded 2026-08-24.
This follows the 0차 rule in `CLAUDE.md`: for a packaged branded product, the manufacturer's own
site is the first place to look, because stock libraries and public archives do not contain
packshots of specific products at all.

The shop serves each product at three sizes. `_LISM_` is 140×140 (unusable), `_PRLA_` is 300×300,
and **`_PROD_` is 700×700**, which is what these are. Pattern:
`/UploadedFiles/Mall_Image/Item_prod/{year}/{itemId}_PROD_{nn}.JPG`.

Editorial use to identify the products under discussion. No sponsorship implied or claimed.

## Checked and rejected
- **Wikimedia Commons**: no Three Seven or 쓰리쎄븐 product photography.
- **Pexels / Unsplash**: `nail clipper` returns manicure and salon stock with no brand present.
  A generic clipper photograph would fail the rule that an image must show the named subject —
  this article is specifically about one brand's stamped mark and model numbers.
- **`_BIGM_`, `_DETAIL_`, `_LIST_`, `_ZOOM_`, `_MAIN_` size variants**: all return 404 on this
  shop. `_PROD_` is the largest available.

## Verified before writing
Every price in the article was read from this same shop on 2026-08-24, in two categories:
