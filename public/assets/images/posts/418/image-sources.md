## korea-price-calendar-2026-08.jpg (1400x740, 90KB) — hero / ogImage
EpicKor original chart. The four announced increases on a timeline: 풀무원 6.7% on 13 August
(30 items / 7 product groups), 농심 6% cup noodles and 5.5% snacks during August,
파리바게뜨 5.0% on 25 August (127 items — 86 breads, 41 cakes and desserts, first rise in
18 months), 삼립 about 9% in September (~50 packaged breads). Drivers shown: wheat +5.8%,
corn +3.6%, plus packaging and logistics.

Built from company announcements as reported 13–17 August 2026 (Money Today 17 August;
Newspim, Financial News, Hankyung, Herald Business 14 August). The two upcoming increases are
marked in red because that distinction — what a reader can still act on — is the article's point.

**Why a chart and not a photograph.** A price increase has no appearance. Photographing a
supermarket shelf would illustrate the topic while carrying none of the information, which is
exactly the substitution the Blog Reference Image Standard rejects.

## Rejected
- **Stock photography of Korean convenience stores** (Pexels, Unsplash): available, and rejected.
  A generic store interior would be a mood photo attached to a numbers story — the "visually
  pleasant Korea photo" the standard names as insufficient.
- **Press photographs from the news coverage**: these are company handouts distributed to outlets,
  not files served by the manufacturers, so there is no clean licence basis for reuse.
- **Screenshots of Korean price listings**: legible only to Korean readers and unstable as sources.
  The figures are reproduced as text in the article with their dates and origins instead.
