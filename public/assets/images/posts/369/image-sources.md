## Notes

- Orion publishes product detail pages as one very tall JPEG. The three files here are **crops of
  three different regions** of that strip, so they are different pictures, not three resizes of one.
- `goraebap-16-shapes-chart.jpg` is the load-bearing image of the article. It is Orion's own
  labelled chart of all sixteen shapes with species and character names, and it is the evidence for
  the correction the article makes — **English sources say nine shapes; the manufacturer says 16.**
  The chart had to be cropped across two printed rows to keep all sixteen labels legible.
- `goraebap-box-official.jpg` retains the corner badge reading **16마리 귀여운 바닷속 친구들이
  들어있어요**, which is the same claim in the packaging's own words.
