# Image sources for Blog 321 (Korean Fried Chicken Brands Compared)

All three images are real Korea photography (Pexels), manually opened and inspected for
Korea-first authenticity before selection. Checked against `scripts/audit-image-uniqueness.mjs`
prior to use.

- `korean-yangnyeom-chicken-suwon.jpg` (hero/og) - Close-up of glazed, spicy Korean fried chicken with pickled radish cubes (danmuji), shot in Suwon-si, South Korea. Pexels 31986858: https://images.pexels.com/photos/31986858/pexels-photo-31986858.jpeg
- `korean-half-half-chicken-plate.jpg` - A "반반" (half-half) style plate: plain fried chicken alongside glazed chicken with shredded cabbage and carrot garnish. Pexels 5773964: https://images.pexels.com/photos/5773964/pexels-photo-5773964.jpeg
- `goobne-chicken-storefront.jpg` - A real Goobne Chicken storefront in South Korea with "No.1 Oven Roasted Chicken" and "Goobne Beer Pub" branding, Korean-language signage and phone number visible. Pexels 33676347: https://images.pexels.com/photos/33676347/pexels-photo-33676347.jpeg

Rejected during selection: Pexels 5773996, a near-duplicate crop of the same photoshoot/plate as
5773964 (same session, different angle) - using both would have been visually redundant within
one post, so only 5773964 was kept.

Downloaded and inspected 2026-07-25. Verified unique site-wide before publish.
