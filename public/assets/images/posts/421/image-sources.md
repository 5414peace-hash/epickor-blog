# Image Sources - Blog 421

Three real photographs, free licences, no generated visuals.

- `gimhae-international-terminal.jpg` — **ogImage.** Wikimedia Commons
  `File:Gimhae international airport International terminal Departure hall 20180403 121439.jpg`,
  `CC BY-SA 4.0`. The international departure hall at Busan's Gimhae — the airport the article
  argues is the real alternative to Incheon, so it leads.
- `daegu-airport-interior.jpg` — Commons `File:Daegu Airport Interior.jpg`, photo by Ken Eckert,
  `CC BY-SA 4.0`. A small, quiet terminal hall. Used deliberately: the section's point is that
  Daegu is modest but ten minutes from the KTX station, and the photograph shows the modest half
  honestly rather than flattering it.
- `jeju-airport-terminal.jpg` — Commons `File:Jejuairport2024.jpg`, photo by Kimhs5400,
  `CC BY 4.0`, shot 2024-05-16. Terminal frontage with palm trees and a Gate 4 sign.

## Rejected

- ~~`Cheongju airport check-in counter for international flight 20090404.jpg`~~ (Commons,
  CC BY-SA 3.0). It was the only Cheongju terminal image available and it was **not usable**: shot
  in **April 2009**, seventeen years old, showing an **empty** international check-in hall with
  nobody in it. The article's Cheongju section is about the airport growing faster than any other
  regional airport in Korea — international passengers up 39.1% year on year, a million
  international passengers by August. An empty 2009 hall would have contradicted the text in the
  reader's eye.

  Publishing three images that match rather than four where one argues with the copy is the
  correct trade. The Cheongju section runs without a photograph and says so by omission.
