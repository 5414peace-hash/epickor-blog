# Image sources — Blog 397 (K-drama foods)

A drama-foods article cannot use drama stills (broadcast copyright), so every image is the
**real-world referent** of the scene it sits beside — the festival, the plate, the candy.
All three were viewed on a contact sheet before captioning.

## 1. `daegu-chimac-festival.jpg` (hero, ogImage)

- **Source:** Commons `치맥페스티벌.jpg`
- **Author/Licence:** **대구광역시 (Daegu Metropolitan City), KOGL Type 1** — commercial use
  permitted with attribution; attribution in the caption.
- **Original:** 1500x1001. Delivered 1400x934, **181 KB**.
- **What it shows, verified:** fireworks over a packed nighttime crowd at the Daegu Chimac
  Festival — festival tents in rows, 83 Tower lit in the background.
- **Why it is the hero:** the article's first claim is that chimaek is not a drama invention
  but a real institution. A city government's photograph of a festival devoted entirely to
  chicken-and-beer is that claim, at municipal scale.

## 2. `korean-fried-chicken-beer-table.jpg`

- **Source:** Commons `Iksan City 48 Korean Style Fried chicken.jpg`
- **Author/Licence:** **KOREA.NET, CC BY-SA 2.0** (the KOCIS account cleared in the approved
  source list). Attribution in the caption.
- **Original:** 2048x1102. Delivered 1300x700, 88 KB.
- **What it shows:** fried chicken plate with 치킨무 (pickled radish), salad, and a glass of
  beer — table level, Korean restaurant.
- **Why here:** the hero shows chimaek's scale; this shows its contents. The beer in frame is
  what makes it chimaek rather than just chicken.

## 3. `dalgona-candy-heart.jpg`

- **Source:** Commons `Dalgona 1.jpg`
- **Author/Licence:** PhareannaH[berhabuk], **CC BY 2.0**. Attribution in caption.
- **Original:** 1288x1936. Delivered 1000x1503, 99 KB.
- **What it shows, verified:** dalgona **candy** — the flat sugar disc with a heart stamp —
  not the 2020 dalgona *coffee*, which shares the name and pollutes image search. Checked
  precisely because the two are conflated everywhere.
- **Caption care:** the stamp in frame is a heart; Squid Game's shapes were circle, triangle,
  star and umbrella. The caption therefore describes the game generically and does not claim
  this is a Squid Game prop.

## Gap worth noting

No usable photo exists of the **양은냄비 (gold aluminum ramyeon pot)** — Commons has zero, and
it is the single most drama-specific object in the article (the CTA sells it via the Amazon

section, and any Korean kitchen can produce it in five minutes.
