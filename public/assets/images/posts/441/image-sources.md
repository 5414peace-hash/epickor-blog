## Why these

The post's claim is about *who* is on the street, so three of the four frames are streets full of the
age group the claim is about (Hongdae twice, Sinchon), and the fourth is the one piece of official
English-language infrastructure a visitor can actually walk up to. Each caption describes what is
visibly in the frame: the trilingual sign, the multilingual welcome panel on the booth.

Rejected: `Ewha Womens University` (1000 px, 2008), `Seoul-Sinchon-01` (1024 px, 2006) — both too small
and too old.
