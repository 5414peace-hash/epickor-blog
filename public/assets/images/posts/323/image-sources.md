## Fact-check note

The "라면 먹고 갈래?" origin claim in the article was verified rather than assumed. The phrase
traces to Hur Jin-ho's 2001 film *One Fine Spring Day* (봄날은 간다), but the line spoken in the
film is the gentler "라면 먹을래요?" — the blunter, better-known form is a later public reworking.
The article states this distinction explicitly instead of repeating the common simplification.
