## Why these

The post's argument is geographic — looked at here, not there — so the four frames are the four
kinds of place it names: a big-city market where people look, the one Seoul district where nobody
does, a provincial covered market, and a rural bus stop. Each caption describes what is visibly in
the frame. No photograph shows a foreigner being stared at; staging one would have been the kind of
"illustrative" image the reference standard rejects.

Rejected: `Hongdae Street 02` (already the age-line theme of 441; rainy and empty), `Jagalchi
(44835462365)` (a tray of fish with no people), the 2008 Andong entrance sign (signage, not people),
`Haebangchon alley` (no people).
