# Image sources for Blog 330 (Kkondae Explained)

Kkondae is an abstract social/workplace concept, not a place or product, so there is no literal
photo of it. Research (Pexels + Wikimedia Commons) confirmed no authentic "generational contrast"
or "condescending boss" photo exists as real Korean stock photography — generic staged stock and
at least one AI-generated-looking account (ai25studio) were explicitly rejected rather than used
as a plausible-looking substitute. Selected real, Korea-confirmed office/street photography instead.

- `seoul-businessmen-street.jpg` (hero/og) - Three men in business coats walking down a Seoul side
  street past a building hoarding. Pexels 20837269, photographer Elina Volkova (@miami302):
  https://www.pexels.com/photo/businessmen-walking-on-street-in-city-in-seoul-south-korea-20837269/
- `seoul-young-professional-rooftop.jpg` - A young office worker in a suit on a Seoul rooftop,
  overlooking the city skyline. Pexels 8460428, photographer Joon Tae Kim, geotagged Seoul:
  https://www.pexels.com/photo/man-in-black-suit-jacket-and-black-pants-holding-black-leather-sling-bag-8460428/

Both checked against `scripts/audit-image-uniqueness.mjs --check-id` before download - not used
elsewhere on the site.

Rejected during selection: several Pexels "Korean businessman" results credited to account
"ai25studio" (e.g. photo 8278854, 8278852) - excluded as likely AI-generated rather than real
photography, per the site's real-photo-first standard. Generic staged stock with no Korea markers
(pink-background office photos, studio backdrops) also excluded.
