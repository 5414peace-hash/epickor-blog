# Image sources for Blog 336 (Samgak Kimbap)

Confirmed a genuinely hard image category during research (near-zero clean Pexels hits; most
"kimbap"/"onigiri" search results are Japanese onigiri or rolled kimbap, not the Korean triangular
convenience-store product). Two real, confirmed-Korean Wikimedia Commons photos were found.

- `samgak-kimbap-1.jpg` (hero/og) - A real Korean samgak kimbap, unwrapped, showing the numbered
  "PULL ① PULL ② PULL ③" tabs on the plastic wrapper - direct photographic confirmation of the
  packaging-engineering mechanism this article is built around. Wikimedia Commons, photographer
  Teemeah ("own work"), Jan 29, 2017, license CC BY-SA 3.0 Unported:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Samgak_kimbap.jpg
- `samgak-kimbap-2.jpg` - A second real Korean triangular kimbap photo, lower resolution
  (768x512px), sourced from a Korean personal blog (박찹쌀, July 25, 2021). Wikimedia Commons,
  uploader Gksyy, Sept 23, 2023, license CC BY-SA 2.0 KR:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:삼각김밥_사진.jpg

Rejected during selection: Pexels searches for "samgak kimbap," "onigiri," "kimbap," and "korean
convenience store" all returned either Japanese onigiri (would misrepresent the Korean product),
rolled/sliced kimbap (wrong shape), or non-Korean convenience stores - none usable. Given the
site's real-photo-first standard and this category's genuine scarcity, no substitute/generated
image was used; the two Commons photos above carry the full visual load for this post.
