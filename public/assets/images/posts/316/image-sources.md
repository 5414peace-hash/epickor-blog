# Image sources for Blog 316 (Nami Island Day Trip)

All four images are verified Nami Island (Namiseom) from Wikimedia Commons, not
generic tree-avenue or lake stock. This was deliberate: a Pexels search for "Nami Island"
returns misty islets, unrelated gazebos, Japanese forests, and the Damyang metasequoia
road, none of which is Nami. Only Nami-confirmed images were used.

- `nami-metasequoia-winter.jpg` - A snow-dusted avenue of tall metasequoia trees on Nami Island. "Nami island winter", CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nami_island_winter.jpg
- `nami-tree-tunnel-summer.jpg` - A leafy summer tree-tunnel path beside the lake on Nami Island. "남이섬 (27)", by Jocelyndurrey, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:%EB%82%A8%EC%9D%B4%EC%84%AC_(27).JPG
- `nami-ferry-gapyeong.jpg` - The Gapyeong-Nami Island ferry with flags and an "In Naminara Republic / We are all Naminarians" banner crossing the lake. By Minseong Kim, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gapyeong-Nami_Island_Ferry.jpg
- `nami-peacock.jpg` - A peacock walking freely on the grounds of Nami Island. "Common Peacock in Namiseom (2023)", CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Common_Peacock_in_Namiseom_(2023).jpg

Downloaded 2026-07-22. Every image was opened and manually inspected. The ferry banner
("Naminara Republic") confirms the location beyond doubt, and the two tree-lane images and
the peacock come from Wikimedia files explicitly titled Nami Island / Namiseom. Rejected
during review: Pexels 33125408 and 10984344 (misty lake islets, not Nami), 6307198 (a lake
pavilion at night, not Nami), and 1588849 (a plausible-but-unconfirmed tree tunnel). All
optimized to <=250 KB and <=1400 px.
