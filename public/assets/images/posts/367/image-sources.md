## Notes

- `banana-kick-pack-official.jpg` — the hero, and the only image that shows the bag. Cropped to the
  top 70% of Nongshim's brand artwork to remove the Korean marketing copy underneath; the retained
  area is the pack shot plus loose puffs. The bag's own line **바나나맛 그대로** is legible, which is
  the point the article turns on.
- `banana-kick-puffs-shape.jpg` — five puffs on a plate, which is the only framing that shows the
  banana curve and the pitted, porous surface at the same time. Used under the texture section.
- `banana-kick-inside-bag.jpg` — inside the opened bag, showing how loosely the pieces sit. Supports
  the "mostly air" point in the packing and value sections.

## Why no photo of the viral packaging

The **바나나킥 베이비** custom pack reported on 2026-08-05 is a one-off manufactured for a private
individual and carries a child's face. It is not licensed for reuse and the child is not a public
figure, so the article describes it and does not reproduce it. Korean press images of it are
rights-reserved news photography.
