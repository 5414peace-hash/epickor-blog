# Image sources — Blog 451

Korea's Neighbourhood Bathhouses Are Disappearing, and the Bath Is Not: Where the Hot Water Went

Nine Commons photographs plus two company images from The Riverside Hotel. All Commons files fetched with `scripts/fetch-commons.mjs`, resized to a 1500 px long edge. None is used by another post: `176` uses four other files from the same Commons `Category:Jjimjilbang` (20.22.38, 20.38.42, 20.39.31, 22.07.47), which were excluded here.

**Representative's corrections, 2026-09-06.** (1) The first draft used Dragon Hill Spa (Yongsan) as the hero; it has been closed since 2021. (2) The second draft used Hurshimchung (Busan) as the hero and a price board second; the representative called both off-subject and asked for Korean bathhouse interiors. Hurshimchung was removed entirely (not important to the argument). Pexels has no Korean bathhouse imagery (seven queries returned Nordic saunas and hanok); the whole Commons `Category:Jjimjilbang` was listed and the unused interiors selected.
