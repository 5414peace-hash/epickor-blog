Korea's Neighbourhood Bathhouses Are Disappearing, and the Bath Is Not: Where the Hot Water Went

Nine Commons photographs plus two company images from The Riverside Hotel. All Commons files fetched with `scripts/fetch-commons.mjs`, resized to a 1500 px long edge. None is used by another post (`176` uses different jjimjilbang files).

**2026-09-06, representative's correction:** the first draft used Dragon Hill Spa (Yongsan) as the hero and recommended it. Dragon Hill closed for the pandemic in 2021, never reopened, and had its map listings deleted in 2025. The photo was removed, the text corrected, and the representative asked for old-style Korean bathhouse and hotel-sauna photographs instead.

- `Hotel Nongshim.jpg` — the hotel tower, not the bathhouse.
