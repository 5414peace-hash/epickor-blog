## kkokkalcorn-store-map.jpg (1400x780, 92KB) — hero / ogImage
EpicKor original chart. Which convenience store chain sells which Yajang-series flavour:
GS25 → 마성옥수수맛, CU → 워킹타코맛, 7-Eleven → 버터구이오징어맛, and 쌈장삼겹살맛 through

across three chains — is not a visual property of the object. No photograph of a bag can show it.
This is the same call recorded in FACTS on 2026-08-18: when the fact is not visual, a chart beats
a photo rather than substituting for one.

## Rejected
- **No official 야장시리즈 imagery is retrievable.** Lotte's press centre index did not surface the
  July/August 2026 release, and the series photographs in news coverage are press handouts carried
  by the outlets rather than files served from the manufacturer.
- **Pexels / Unsplash / Wikimedia Commons**: no 꼬깔콘. Searches return generic corn chips and
  American-style cone snacks, which would misrepresent a specific branded product — the exact
  failure recorded for the 2026-08-03 card-news incident.
- **2015 lineup shows pre-2026 packaging.** Lotte redesigned the Kkokkalcorn pack in 2026 for the
  first time since 2021, so the artwork on this image is out of date. The caption therefore
  identifies it as the permanent lineup rather than as current shelf appearance, and the body text
  warns readers explicitly that online photos may not match the bag in front of them.
