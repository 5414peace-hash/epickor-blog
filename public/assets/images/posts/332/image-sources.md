# Image sources for Blog 332 (Korean Trot Music)

First pass used only a noraebang screen photo and an unrelated Gwangju world-music-festival
crowd shot. The representative rejected it: if the post is about trot, it needs actual trot
singers in it, not generic texture. Redid the image search specifically for real, cleanly
licensed photos of real trot performers (Wikimedia Commons category/search, file-by-file
license verification) rather than defaulting to a plausible-looking substitute.

- `song-ga-in-2020.png` (hero/og) - Song Ga-in at a photo session/concert-recording event in
  Mapo-gu, Seoul, Jan 16, 2020, holding a microphone. She is the Miss Trot 2019 winner discussed
  at length in the article. Wikimedia Commons, uploader Flying1004, license CC BY 3.0 Unported,
  reviewed and confirmed 2020-06-02: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:200116_송가인.png
- `nam-jin-portrait.jpg` - Nam Jin, one of trot's first genuine idols (dominant late 1960s-mid
  1970s, discussed in the "From Foxtrot to Ppongjjak" section), performing on stage with a
  microphone. Wikimedia Commons, source Naver blog, uploader Fetx2002, license CC BY 2.0 Korea,
  reviewed 2014-12-30: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nam_Jin.jpg
- `jang-yoon-jeong-2008.jpg` - Jang Yoon-jeong at the Golden Disk Awards, Dec 10, 2008 - a real
  red-carpet event photo. She's the singer named in the article's noraebang section ("Eomeona").
  Wikimedia Commons, photographer KIYOUNG KIM (Flickr), uploader Puramyun31, license CC BY 2.0
  Generic, reviewed by FlickreviewR:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jang_Yoon-Jeong_at_the_Golden_Disk_Awards_2008.jpg
- `noraebang-screen.jpg` - A real Korean noraebang (karaoke room) accompaniment-machine screen
  showing on-screen lyrics mid-song. Wikimedia Commons, uploader Pbj1995, Nov 28, 2019, license
  CC BY-SA 4.0: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:%EB%85%B8%EB%9E%98%EB%B0%A9_%ED%99%94%EB%A9%B4.jpg

Rejected during this second pass: all 3 available Lim Young-woong files on Commons are ad/
commercial photos (a ginseng-drink ad flagged "license review needed", and a KTX ad with an
unverified "own work" claim) - no clean plain performance photo of him exists on Commons, so he
is discussed in text only, without a photo credited to him specifically. A Na Hoon-a file
("나훈아_콘서트_후기.jpg") was also rejected - thin provenance (random Tistory blog repost, content
not independently confirmed to show him performing). The official Mr. Trot logo and Miss Trot
promotional poster both exist only as Wikipedia non-free/fair-use files - legally Wikipedia-only,
not reusable on a commercial blog, and were not used for that reason. The originally selected
Gwangju Asia Culture Center concert-crowd photo (Pexels 18974471) was dropped as not trot-specific.
