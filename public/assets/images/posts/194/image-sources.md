# Image Sources - Blog 194

- `chuseok-fruit-gift-boxes.jpg` — **ogImage since 2026-08-21.** Wikimedia Commons
  `File:Seoul Before Seollal Week 07 (16330231149).jpg`, Korean Culture and Information Service /
  "Republic of Korea" Flickr, `CC BY-SA 2.0`. Shot 2015-02-13 at Shinsegae Department Store,
  Jung-gu, Seoul (Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism). Boxed Korean pears and apples in the
  red foam cradles, with price tags in frame. **The tags are 2015 prices and the caption says so** —
  the article's current figures come from text sources, not from this photograph.
- `seollal-gift-set-wall-prices.jpg` — Commons `File:Seoul Before Seollal Week 02 (16328830308).jpg`,
  same shoot, same licence. A gift-set wall merchandised by price tier, under a
  `설 기프트 대량판매 접수처` bulk-order banner. Used in the 명절 선물세트 section because it shows
  the tiering the section describes.
- `hangwa-gift-set-boxed.jpg` — Commons `File:Seoul Before Seollal Week 05 (15893954694).jpg`,
  same shoot, same licence. Boxed 한과 with a maker's card. Replaced `wrapped-gift-boxes.jpg` in the
  presentation section.
- `ikseondong-shopping-street.jpg` - Photo via Pexels (was photo 31680642 until 2026-07-25, replaced: cross-post duplicate also used by Blog 041, which keeps the original as the earlier post), image URL: `https://images.pexels.com/photos/31680629/pexels-photo-31680629.jpeg`. Hanok-roofed Ikseon-dong alley with a shop sign reading "익선들꽃" (literally referencing Ikseon), confirming location. Verified unique site-wide on 2026-07-25. **Caption corrected 2026-08-21** — it previously called the alley "gift-friendly" and said "small Seoul shops make it easy to find gifts". The frame is restaurants and cafés (노들집, a 오징어 place, Terra and Cass signage). The caption now says that, which is more useful to a reader planning where to shop.
- `seoul-market-stalls.jpg` - Photo by CK Seng via Pexels, image URL: `https://images.pexels.com/photos/37785270/pexels-photo-37785270.jpeg`. **Caption corrected 2026-08-21** — it claimed the stalls were "useful places to find small, practical gifts". The frame is a neighbourhood produce and bedding alley (며느리야채, 이불, 중외약국, 논현145). It now points at boxed fruit, which is a real gift category and is what those crates actually are.

Removed:

- ~~`seoul-outdoor-gift-shop.jpg`~~ (Pexels 29562546) - **REMOVED 2026-08-21.** It was the post's
  ogImage and was captioned "a Seoul outdoor shop, the kind of place travellers browse for small
  gifts". The photograph is a machine-parts street — 모타·전동공구 signage, scrap machinery on the
  pavement. Nothing in it is a gift, so the caption contradicted the picture and the thumbnail
  misrepresented the article. Found while sourcing the card-news carousel for this post.
- ~~`wrapped-gift-boxes.jpg`~~ (Pexels 5402562, Murugan Ettiyan) - **REMOVED 2026-08-21.** It became
  the ogImage earlier the same day as the least-bad replacement for the machine-parts photo, and
  the note recording that move already conceded it was "not a Korea-specific scene". Looking at it
  directly: the wrapped objects are **cigars and a bottle**, with English-language labels. A Korean
  gift-etiquette article was being represented in search results and social previews by a Western
  cigar bundle. Replaced by three real Korean department-store gift-set photographs, which the
  earlier session did not have available.

Rejected notes:

- Christmas-heavy gift photos and non-Korean souvenir displays were rejected because they could mislead the Korean culture context.
- Commons `Seoul Before Seollal Week 03 / 06` were reviewed and not used: `03` is a wide aisle shot
  with no legible subject at article width, `06` is an SSG own-brand seasoning set that reads as a
  single retailer's promotion rather than the category.
- These three files are the first use of the `Seoul Before Seollal Week` set anywhere in the repo
  (checked 2026-08-21 across `public/assets/` and `content/`). Note that
  `scripts/audit-image-uniqueness.mjs` keys off Pexels-style `photos/{id}` URLs and therefore does
  **not** cover Commons files — cross-post uniqueness for these has to be checked by filename.
