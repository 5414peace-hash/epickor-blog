# Image sources — Blog 386 (How to buy alcohol in Korea)

All three are Wikimedia Commons photographs of the exact subjects the article names.
The contact sheet was built and **looked at before any caption was written** — the
opposite of what happened on Blog 385 the same day, where a caption was written from
a filename and ended up contradicting its own section.

## 1. `cu-convenience-store-wine-shelf.jpg` (hero, ogImage)

- **Source:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Alcohol_sold_at_South_Korean_convenience_store.jpg
- **Author:** Sgroey — **attribution required, and in the caption.**
- **Licence:** **CC BY-SA 4.0**
- **Original:** 3024x4032. Delivered 1000px, mozjpeg q62, **176 KB**.
- **What it actually shows:** a wine rack inside a **CU** branch, bottles with price
  labels, under a fixture signed `CU BAR 쿠바`, with a customer at the drinks chiller.
- **The care point:** the article's worked example is **GS25's Wine25Plus**, and this
  photograph is **CU**. Captioning a CU photo as GS25 would be the same class of error
  as the 2026-08-03 card-news incident. The caption names CU explicitly and the image
  is placed against the general claim — convenience store as wine shop — not against
  the GS25-specific section.
- **Size note:** the first export was 610 KB, over the 400 KB gate. Resized to 1000px
  and re-encoded with mozjpeg rather than shipped at the ceiling.

## Checks run

- **Cross-post uniqueness:** first use of all three on this site.
- **Exact-subject rule:** each image is the specific thing its section discusses —
  a Korean convenience-store wine fixture, the named domestic soju brands, and
  multiple real makgeolli labels. None is a generic "alcohol" stock photo.
- **Size:** 176 + 97 + 103 KB = **376 KB** for the post.
- **Captions:** written after viewing the contact sheet; each states what is in frame
  and carries the licence attribution. None apologises for its image.

## Gap worth noting

There is **no photograph of the smart-order pickup itself** — the app screen, or a
bottle being handed over at a counter. That is the mechanism the whole article is
about, and Commons does not have it. A screenshot of the GS25 app's liquor section,
or a photo of a Wine25+ pickup, would be the single most valuable image this post
could gain.
