## No photograph, and the 0차 route failed again

Checked in order, all empty:

- **Wikimedia Commons** — nothing for 오징어땅콩 or "squid peanut".
- **Pexels** — the nearest results are raw or candied peanuts in bowls. This product is a coated
  sphere with squid strips on it; a bowl of salted peanuts under this headline would be the
  substitution the reference standard forbids.
- **orionworld.com, the manufacturer** — `/product` returns **2,129 bytes**, a JS shell. This is
  now the fourth Korean site to fail this exact way after `7-eleven.co.kr`, `ht.co.kr` and GS25's
  AJAX product list. HTTP 200 from a Korean brand site means very little on its own.

## Which is why both images are drawn

Neither is decoration standing in for a missing photograph. The anatomy diagram carries the fact
the article exists for — a peanut wrapped 28 times, 27 if it is large and 29 if it is small, under
a squid layer 0.2 mm thick — and a pack shot could not show any of that. The timeline carries the
second argument, that fifty years of flavour extensions have all been outlived by the 1976 recipe.
