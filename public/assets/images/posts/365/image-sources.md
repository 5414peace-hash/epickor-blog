## The image gate ran before this topic was shortlisted

Per the standing order set after `363`/`364`, the gate ran during the W32c cycle rather than after approval —
and it is part of why this candidate beat the alternatives. Chilsung Cider returned **four** usable Commons
files, **three of them above 3700 px**, where 포카칩 returned one at 684 px and Dashida returned none at all.

The two KOCIS files come from the **Republic of Korea / Korea.net official Flickr account**, which CLAUDE.md
already lists as an approved 2차 source with licensing verified on Wikimedia. The Flickr-style numbering in
the filenames (`15891088219`) is the tell.

## The hero photograph is evidence, not decoration

`chilsung-cider-bottles.jpg` was chosen over the other KOCIS frames because of what is legible on the label
at full resolution:

- **칠성사이다** in Hangul and `Chilsung Cider` in English — product identity beyond dispute
- the **LOTTE** mark — the current owner, which the article's history section explains
- the **stars** the name refers to (칠성 = seven stars)
- `220 kcal` — the figure the article quotes
- and **"PURE, CLEAN, CAFFEINE FREE"**, printed in English on the bottle

That last line matters more than usual. **`does korean cider have caffeine` is one of the target queries**,
and the answer is printed inside the hero image. The photograph is doing the same evidential work the Yakult
nutrition panel did on `362`.

## The poured glass is the article's central claim

`chilsung-cider-poured-glass.jpg` exists in this post for one reason: the piece opens by explaining that
Korean 사이다 has **no apples**, and apple cider is amber and cloudy. A photograph of a **water-clear** drink
over ice settles that in less time than the sentence takes to read.

It also visually supports the no-caramel-colouring point made later.

## Uniqueness

All three are first-use on this site. `scripts/audit-image-uniqueness.mjs` keys off Pexels/stock photo IDs
and does not parse Commons filenames, so uniqueness here was confirmed by searching existing
`image-sources.md` records for the Commons file titles — no prior use of any of the three.
