## Checks run

- **Cross-post uniqueness:** first use of all three on this site.
- **Exact-subject rule:** every image is the specific model named in the article —
  not "an air purifier". The Winix photo is the 5500-2, the model the discontinuation
  section is about.
- **Size:** 78 + 257 + 222 KB = **557 KB** for the post, inside the 1 MB folder ceiling.
- **Captions:** each states what the photo shows and credits the source; the Commons
  image carries the CC BY-SA 4.0 attribution required by its licence. No caption
  apologises for or explains away its image.
