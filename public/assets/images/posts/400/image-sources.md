- **Source:** Commons `Coway Neo Plus White.jpg`
- **Author/Licence:** Danielrayyan09, **CC BY 4.0** — attribution in caption.
- **Original:** 4080x3072. Delivered 1400x1054, **80 KB**.
- **What it shows, verified:** a white Coway purifier (COWAY wordmark legible, two dispense
  taps, control panel) on a counter in a genuinely lived-in kitchen.
- **The care point — country.** The kitchen's furnishings and the uploader's name suggest this

  Southeast-Asian home supports the export thesis without a false Korea claim. The same photo

  it is the right product.
