## The photograph is the Italian original, and the caption says so

This is the first post in the batch where a real photograph could carry the argument, because the
argument is about a European pastry. The frame shows sfogliatine glassate in Italy — glazed puff
pastry with apricot jam piped in a lattice — which is exactly the object the Korean product is a
version of. The caption states plainly that it is **not** the Korean product, because it is not,
and because the whole article turns on the two being the same thing under different names.

There is still no free-licensed photograph of 누네띠네 itself. Commons has nothing, and SPC's own
imagery is not free. That absence is fine here: showing the Italian original is more useful than a
pack shot would have been.

A second Commons file, `Sfogliatine glassate in Poland.jpg` (2849×1263, CC BY 4.0), was compared
and rejected — it shows the pastries through plastic packaging, so the jam lattice reads less
clearly at blog width.
