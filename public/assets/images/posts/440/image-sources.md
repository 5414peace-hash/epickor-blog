## Why these and not others

The article's subject is a *boundary* — Koreans neutral to strangers, warm inside a group — so
the pictures had to show both sides rather than "Korea in general". The hero is the stranger side
(a full carriage, nobody speaking); the market vendor is the inside (a named person handing you
food); Myeongdong is the tourist's Korea that the 82.3% figure measures. The chart is a
supplement after three real photographs, per the reference-image standard, and every number on
it was read from the report text extracted with pypdf.

Rejected on the contact sheet: `Korea-Pojangmacha-01.jpg` (500×375, too small),
`People engaging with their phones on the Seoul Metro` (2010, dated interior), the two
`Friday Night` frames (bokeh, no subject), `Hongdae Street 01` (empty street in rain).
