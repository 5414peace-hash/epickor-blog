## Why buses, for a company profile

A licensing company is hard to photograph. There is no factory floor and no product line — the
business is contracts. The Tayo bus livery is the rare case where the licensing programme is
physically visible in public space, on Korean streets, in an image we can license cleanly.

The second image earns its place editorially rather than decoratively: the Tayo face is the
licensed element, while the antlers and red nose are the operator's own addition. That difference
is the evidence for the article's point about licensee-volunteered effort, so the photograph is
carrying an argument rather than filling a gap.

## What was rejected

- **Pororo character imagery.** Commons holds a small number of Pororo appearance photos

- **`File:제주 뽀로로파크.jpg`** — the actual Jeju Pororo Park, correctly licensed, but only
  387×267. Downloaded and deleted rather than published at that size.
- **Paldo's Pororo drink pack shots.** These exist in this repo but are already used by Blog `358`,
  and the cross-post image uniqueness rule applies to business posts as well as blog posts.
