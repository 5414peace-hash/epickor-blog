# Image sources for /business/korean-apparel-suppliers

All three images are verified Dongdaemun / Seoul fashion-district locations from Wikimedia Commons.

- `pyounghwa-fashion-plaza.jpg` - Pyounghwa Fashion Plaza (평화시장 / PYOUNG HWA FASHION PLAZA, 의류생산도매), a historic Dongdaemun wholesale apparel building along Cheonggyecheon. "Pyoung Hwa Fashion Plaza, Seoul 2020-02-01", by Stck w, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pyoung_Hwa_Fashion_Plaza,_Seoul_2020-02-01.jpg
- `dongdaemun-market.jpg` - Dongdaemun's cluster of wholesale fashion malls and shopping towers (동대문쇼핑타운 signage visible), with N Seoul Tower in the background. "Korea-Seoul-Dongdaemun Market-01", by Martin Röll, CC BY-SA 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Korea-Seoul-Dongdaemun_Market-01.jpg
- `ddp-night.jpg` - Dongdaemun Design Plaza (DDP) lit at night, the modern landmark of Seoul's fashion district. "Dongdaemun Design Plaza - DDP2376", by lumoplank, CC0, via Wikimedia Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dongdaemun_Design_Plaza_-_DDP2376.jpg
