# Image sources — Navien deep dive

Both images are **Navien's own**, taken from its North American site (`navieninc.com`) on
2026-08-14. This follows the 0차 rule in `CLAUDE.md`: for a branded product, the manufacturer
comes before stock libraries. Editorial identification of the company under discussion; no
sponsorship exists or is implied, and the article states that plainly.

## 1. `navien-npe-a2-tankless.jpg` (hero, ogImage)

- **Source:** Navien NPE-240A2 product page, image alt "Navien NPE-A2 series condensing tankless water heater"
- **Delivered:** 839x1513, 207 KB (original resolution, only recompressed)
- **Why:** it is a cutaway with the **Navien Condensing Heat Exchanger** labelled in the middle
  of the frame. The article's argument is that the condensing heat exchanger — refined for Korean
  ondol boilers from 1988 — is what let the company enter America with a product the market did
  not yet know it wanted. The photograph shows the actual component the argument is about.

## 2. `navien-global-map.jpg`

- **Source:** `navieninc.com/about`, image alt "Navien-global-map"
- **Delivered:** 1100x667, 53 KB (converted from PNG)
- **Why:** a company deep dive about 70% overseas revenue needs to show the footprint, and this
  is the company's own statement of it.
- **Caption is deliberately hedged.** It is corporate marketing, not neutral data, and the caption
  says so while noting the underlying 30-countries claim is consistent with reported revenue.

## Rejected

- **Commons `Navien.jpg`** — 453x604, CC BY-SA 3.0, description "This is my photo", and its
  neighbours in the search result are minerals (Tremolite, Maripozite). Subject unverifiable and
  too small. Not used.
- **Generic Commons tankless-water-heater photos** (Paloma, Noritz, Rinnai units) — those are
  *competitors'* products. Using one to illustrate a Navien article would be actively misleading,
  not merely weak.
- A second Navien image at 378x687 turned out to be the same cutaway at a smaller size; deleted
  rather than published as a second view.
