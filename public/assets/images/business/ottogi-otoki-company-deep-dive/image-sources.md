# Ottogi / Otoki Company Deep Dive — Image Sources

Every asset below comes from Otoki's own corporate website (`otoki.com`). They are used
editorially to identify the company, its product, and the overseas sites named in the
article. No client relationship or endorsement is implied.

Sourcing note: this followed the tier-0 rule in `CLAUDE.md` — for a packaged product or a
named company, go to the manufacturer's own site first. Pexels, Unsplash and Wikimedia
Commons have no category for "the Otoki America building in Norwalk," so searching them
first would only have produced a generic office block.

## Used In Article

- `jin-ramen-export-pack.jpg`
  - Source: `https://www.otoki.com/pds/product/prd_en/2506/2f5e3132d2d2478981ad702efa4d0217.png`

  - Use: opening image and OG image. Chosen specifically because it is the **export** pack —
