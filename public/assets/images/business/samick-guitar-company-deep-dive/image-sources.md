# Image Sources - Business: Samick deep dive

- `seiler-grand-piano-samick.jpg` — Samick's own official main visual from samick.co.kr, showing a
  Seiler grand piano. Used deliberately: Seiler is the German piano house Samick acquired, so the
  company's own homepage hero is visual evidence of the ownership the article discusses.
  Source: https://samick.co.kr/upload/mainimg/1783678865_RCxEP4_0.png (1920x1001 original).
  Fetch note: samick.co.kr serves images only over https with redirect — http paths 301 to an HTML
  shell; follow redirects.
- `samick-jtr-guitar.jpg` — Wikimedia Commons, "Samick JTR RoseAnne," CC BY 2.0. A Samick-branded
  electric guitar — the company's own-name instrument line. Source:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Samick_JTR_RoseAnne.jpg

Rejected: two other samick.co.kr visuals showing Gibson Les Pauls and what appears to be
Schecter-branded instruments. Samick's Korean site mixes its manufacturing identity with its role
distributing global brands domestically, and using those images would have implied Samick made
them. Caption accuracy beats image quality.
