This is a public-information-only company spotlight (Type B-2). Both images are real photographs
of genuine Coupang-branded physical assets (a delivery truck, a delivery bag) documenting the
company's logistics operations - not studio/marketing photography, and not implying any client
or endorsement relationship between EpicKor and Coupang.
