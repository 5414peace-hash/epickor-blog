# Pulmuone Company Deep Dive — Image Sources

All assets are official Pulmuone material: the company's own corporate history pages and a
press image the company distributed through PR Newswire. They are used editorially to
identify the company, its brands and its operations. No client relationship or endorsement
is implied.

## Used In Article

- `pulmuone-us-tofu-brand-lineup.jpg`
  - Source: `https://mma.prnewswire.com/media/1703941/Tofu_Family_Shot_10x.jpg?p=original`
  - Context: official Pulmuone press image distributed with the "Pulmuone, Makers of
    America's Leading Tofu Brands including Nasoya and Wildwood, Expands California Facility"
    release (PR Newswire, release 301438890).
  - Use: opening image and OG image. This single frame carries the article's thesis — five
    US-facing brands (Nasoya, Wildwood, Azumaya, Soga, Pulmuone) owned by one Korean
    company, with only one of them naming the parent. Nothing sourced elsewhere states
    that as economically.
  - Note: the default PR Newswire URL serves a 400x180 thumbnail; `?p=original` returns
    the 800x360 asset actually used here.

- `pulmuone-us-supermarket-tofu-shelf.jpg`
  - Source: `https://www.pulmuone.co.kr/pulmuone/images/sub/history/overseas/3.jpg`
  - Context: official Pulmuone overseas-history page.
  - Use: the US retail section. Shows Wildwood tofu in an American refrigerated case with
    dollar shelf tags beside its competitors — the "we compete as an American brand" point
    as an actual photograph rather than an assertion.

- `pulmuone-early-plant-cold-chain.jpg`
  - Source: `https://www.pulmuone.co.kr/en/images/sub/history/enterprise/3.jpg`
  - Context: official Pulmuone corporate-history page.
  - Use: the origin section. Early Pulmuone Foods plant with its refrigerated delivery
    fleet, supporting the argument that packaged tofu was a cold-chain investment before it
    was a food product — which is also why the US business lost money for two decades.

- `pulmuone-china-pumeiduo-booth.jpg`
  - Source: `https://www.pulmuone.co.kr/pulmuone/images/sub/history/overseas/9.jpg`
  - Context: official Pulmuone overseas-history page.
  - Use: the China section, showing the 圃美多 (Pumeiduo) brand under which Pulmuone trades
    in China — the group's one clearly profitable overseas market.

## Considered And Not Used

- `enterprise/2.jpg` (GMP plant groundbreaking), `enterprise/10.jpg` (Foodmerce founding),

  people at an event rather than the operation being described.
