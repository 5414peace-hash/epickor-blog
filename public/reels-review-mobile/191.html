# Reels Script - Blog 191 Target duration: 35-40 seconds ## Part 1 Korean college is not just study hard. It is study hard, socialize hard, and start planning your future early. After years of entrance-exam pressure, university can feel like freedom. But the pressure does not disappear. It changes shape. ## Part 2 Students join clubs, go to festivals, take MT trips, build friend groups, and sometimes drink way more than they expected. But at the same time, they are thinking about grades, internships, language scores, certificates, and jobs. That is the Korean campus mix. ## Part 3 It can look like a party from the outside. But underneath it, there is hierarchy, career anxiety, school prestige, and a lot of social effort. So Korean university life is not just school. It is a mirror of modern Korea. Save this if you want to understand young Korea. ## Caption Beat Notes - Keep `It changes shape.` as a clean pause. - Do not overemphasize drinking; it is one element, not the whole story. - Keep `a mirror of modern Korea` as the payoff line.
