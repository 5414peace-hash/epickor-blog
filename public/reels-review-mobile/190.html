# Reels Script - Blog 190 Target duration: 34-38 seconds ## Part 1 Save these three numbers before you get sick in Korea. 119 is for ambulance, fire, and real emergencies. 1330 is the Korea travel hotline, useful when you need tourist help or interpretation. 1339 is for public-health guidance. ## Part 2 Korean healthcare is strong, but you still need to choose the right door. Mild cold, stomach issue, or motion sickness? Try a pharmacy first. Need a doctor but it is not an emergency? Look for a clinic. ## Part 3 If it is serious, do not overthink it. Use a hospital or call 119. Bring travel insurance, your passport, medication names, and allergy information. This is travel prep, not medical advice. Save it before your Korea trip. ## Caption Beat Notes - Keep `119`, `1330`, and `1339` visually large and readable. - Do not split `travel insurance, passport, medication names, and allergy information` into tiny fragments. - Keep the final disclaimer calm and short.
