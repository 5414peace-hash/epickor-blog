## Strategic Finding

EpicKor's strongest Reels were not random. The winning pattern is:

- Korea explained through a simple cultural mystery.
- Everyday Korean life that feels surprising to outsiders.
- Seoul alternatives and travel decision helpers.
- Korean food rules, social rules, and unspoken habits.
- Topics that can be understood from one strong hook without prior context.

This supports the current revival strategy: build the first 30 Instagram card-news carousels from historically validated Reels and GSC-backed topics before pushing many brand-new ideas.
