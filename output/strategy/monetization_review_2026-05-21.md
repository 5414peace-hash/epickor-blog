# EpicKor Monetization Review - 2026-05-21

## Current Operating Reality

- Goal: turn EpicKor content into Amazon affiliate revenue through a loop of Instagram Reels, Instagram card news, and EpicKor.com articles.
- Upload rhythm confirmed by representative:
  - Tuesday / Wednesday / Thursday: card news.
  - Friday / Saturday / Sunday: Reels.
- Card-news uploads are scheduled through `output/cardnews/2026-05-03_132` by 2026-05-28.
- Card-news inventory is still deep.
- Reels inventory is thin, and current Reels views are often below 1,000.

## What To Measure

The main question is not "did a Reel go viral?" The main question is whether the whole funnel is moving.

| Funnel stage | Main metric | Healthy signal | Review cadence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Instagram reach | Reel views, carousel reach | Reach is stable or rising by batch | Weekly |
| Instagram quality | Saves, shares, comments, follows per post | Saves/shares rise even before views spike | Weekly |
| Click intent | Profile visits, link clicks, story/link sticker clicks if used | More users leave Instagram for EpicKor | Weekly |
| Website demand | GSC clicks, impressions, CTR, top pages | More search and social traffic lands on relevant guides | Weekly quick check, monthly deep check |
| Amazon intent | Affiliate link clicks per post/topic | Clicks concentrate around food, beauty, shopping, culture products | Weekly |
| Amazon result | Ordered items, shipped revenue, commission | Any consistent click-to-earning path appears | Monthly |

## Minimum Weekly Scorecard

Track this once per week, preferably Monday after the Fri-Sun Reels cycle.

| Item | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Reels posted | Count and slugs |
| Card-news posted | Count and slugs |

| Worst Reel | Same metrics, plus why it likely underperformed |
| Best card news | Reach, saves, shares, profile visits |
| Website clicks | GSC clicks and top gaining pages |
| Amazon clicks | Total clicks, top products, top tracking IDs if available |
| Amazon earnings | Ordered items, shipped items, commission |
| Action for next week | One content improvement and one monetization improvement |

## Monthly Deep Review

Run this every 4 weeks, not daily.

1. Compare Instagram batches, not single posts.
2. Group content by topic:
   - food
   - beauty
   - travel
   - shopping
   - language/culture
   - celebrity/K-pop
3. Mark each topic by:
   - reach
   - save/share strength
   - website clicks
   - Amazon click fit
   - production effort
4. Double down only when at least two of these are true:
   - high reach
   - high saves/shares
   - rising GSC demand
   - clear Amazon product fit
   - low production risk

## Reels Diagnosis

Under-1,000-view Reels are disappointing, but they are still useful if they reveal which hooks fail.

Likely current problem areas:

- The account is still warming back up after a long posting pause.
- Reels may be too educational before they are emotionally clickable.
- Some topics are useful but not instantly thumb-stopping.
- Reels supply is too thin for repeated testing.
- The CTA path from Instagram to site/Amazon is probably not measured tightly enough yet.

## Reels Improvement Plan

For the next Reels batch, test sharper first-second hooks.

Use one of these hook families:

| Hook type | Example direction |
| --- | --- |
| Mistake | "Tourists get this Korean BBQ rule wrong." |
| Hidden system | "This is why one Korean meal becomes a whole table." |
| Before you go | "Before you ride the Seoul subway, know this." |
| Korea vs expectation | "Korean markets are not just old shopping streets." |
| Save reason | "Save this before your first Korea shopping day." |

Production rule:

- Each new batch should include at least one high-intent Amazon topic, such as Korean snacks, ramen, sunscreen, beauty, Korean BBQ sauce, Korean cookware, Korean study/language tools, or travel-prep products.
- Keep Reels useful, but make the opening more emotional and specific.
- Do not wait for a perfect Reel format before testing; the current need is learning velocity.

## Card News Role

Card news should keep warming the account because inventory is strong.

Card news success should be judged mainly by:

- saves
- shares
- profile visits
- comments/questions
- whether the topic later performs as a Reel or blog update

Card news is not only direct monetization. It is audience warming and topic testing.

## Website And Amazon Improvements

Immediate improvements:

- Add consistent UTM parameters to Instagram profile links and any campaign links.
- Keep source post URLs visible in captions, but treat actual clickable conversion as coming from profile/story/link tools.
- Build or maintain a simple weekly KPI sheet.
- Audit which public posts have `amazon: true`.
- Expand affiliate coverage only where the product match is natural.

Priority Amazon topic clusters:

1. Korean food at home: ramen, sauces, snacks, banchan tools, Korean BBQ items.
2. K-beauty: sunscreen, sun sticks, cleanser, toner, travel-friendly skincare.
3. Korea travel prep: adapters, transit/card wallets, packing tools, walking shoes only if relevant.
4. Korean language/culture: Hangeul workbooks and beginner learning tools.

Avoid forcing Amazon links into purely cultural posts unless the product match is obvious.
