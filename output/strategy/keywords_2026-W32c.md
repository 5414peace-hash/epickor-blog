# Keyword cycle — 2026-W32c

**Run:** 2026-08-04 · **ISO week 32** (Tuesday)
**Method change this cycle:** Korean news-feed scan first, instead of category enumeration.

> **Naming:** filed as **W32c**, not W33. 2026-08-04 is ISO week 32, the same week as W32 and W32b.
> The playbook forbids bumping the week number; letters are the mechanism. This is the second time
> the correction has been needed (see W31b), so it is worth stating plainly in the artifact.

---

## Why the method changed

W32b was run by enumerating product categories, and every candidate it produced was a **steady seller** —
다시다 (1975), 맛동산 (1975). That method works but its inventory runs dry, because the set of famous old
Korean products is finite and we are consuming it at two per cycle.

So this cycle led with a **Korean-language news and sales-data scan**: 편의점 신제품 rundowns, 식품음료신문,
2026 H1 category sales rankings, and 브랜드평판 data.

**It found candidates immediately, and then the coverage gate killed the best of them.** That result is the
main finding of this cycle and it generalises.

Carbonated drinks, 2026 Q1 offline purchase penetration: **Coca-Cola 21.4%, 칠성사이다 18.7%**, Coke Zero 10.8%.

Then the coverage gate ran, and **the higher the sales rank, the more thoroughly English had already covered it.**

- **초코파이** — Korea Herald, Korea Times, ZenKimchi (*"Myth: Choco Pies were Invented in Korea"*),
  myfreshdash, two dedicated Orion-vs-Lotte comparison blogs, namu EN. **Dead.** The Orion-vs-Lotte angle,
  the 1974/1979 copycat suit, and the generic-trademark ruling are all already in English.
- **새우깡** — killed by our **own** duplicate audit before the coverage gate got to it. Blog `029` already
  carries the full origin story: 1971 as Nongshim's first snack, the name taken from the chairman's
  four-year-old mispronouncing *아리랑* as *아리깡*, the whole `-깡` suffix lineage, and current pricing
  including the ₩1,500 → ₩1,600 rise on 2026-08-01. Google returns only 2 weak branches
  (`saewoo kkang`, `nongshim saeukkang`). **Retread with no demand upside.**
- **빼빼로** — partial overlap with `147` (Pepero Day inside the couple-anniversary post).

**This is the same law W31b found from the other direction** — "viral in Korea means published in English
within days by higher-authority outlets." W31b learned it about *new* products. W32c extends it: it is also
true of *the biggest* products. The exploitable middle is a product that is **ordinary in Korea and a line
item in an English listicle** — which is exactly the profile of everything that has worked
(`359` 너구리, `360` 밀키스, `361` 진라면, `363` 다시다, `364` 맛동산).

## ✅ Candidate 1 — 칠성사이다 (Chilsung Cider)

**Target query:** `korean cider` / `chilsung cider vs sprite`
**Shape:** confusion-correction + comparison

This one survived the coverage gate on a technicality that turns out to be the whole story.

### The demand is in the confusion, not the product

Google autocomplete, and note what people are actually anxious about:

```
is korean cider alcoholic          does korean cider have alcohol
is korean cider halal              does korean cider have caffeine
what is cider in korea             is cider korean brand
chilsung cider vs sprite taste     chilsung cider vs sprite calories
is chilsung cider healthy          chilsung cider ingredients
```

**In English, "cider" means an alcoholic apple drink.** Korean 사이다 is a clear, non-alcoholic lemon-lime
soda with no apple in it. Every one of those queries is a person trying to resolve that collision — and the
halal branch means some of them cannot drink it if the answer is wrong.

Naver, by contrast, is asking about **format and variants**: `제로`, `제로 유자`, `500`, `캔`, `355`, `190`,
`유자`, `병`. Amazon carries it: `chilsung cider zero`, `zero sugar`, `yuzu`, `bottles` — 5 branches.

### The coverage gate: contested, but the existing pages are wrong

English supply is not empty. **Daebak** and **SnackFever** — both K-snack-box retailers — have brand-magazine
guides, plus a small SEO page (`summerstirs`) answering *"Is Chilsung Cider Same As Sprite?"*, plus namu EN.

**But they all frame it as "Korea's answer to Sprite," and the dates say otherwise:**

| | Launch |
|---|---|
| **칠성사이다** | **1950** |
| Sprite | **1961** |
| Coca-Cola, officially produced in Korea | 1968 |

**Chilsung Cider is eleven years older than Sprite.** It is not Korea's answer to Sprite; Sprite arrived in a
market Chilsung already owned. Every English page has the causality backwards, and none of them answers the
alcohol / halal / caffeine cluster where the actual anxiety is.

The etymology is a Korean-source fact and is not in the English pages either: 사이다 entered Korean **via
Japanese サイダー**, which took late-19th-century English "cider" and shifted its meaning to *any clear
carbonated fruit-flavoured drink*. The apples were lost in Japan, not in Korea.

### Image gate: strongest of any candidate this year

Wikimedia Commons, all verified as the actual product:

|---|---|---|---|
| `Korea Chilsung Cider 01` | **4012×2288** | CC BY-SA 2.0 | **KOCIS / Korea.net official** |
| `Korea Chilsung Cider 02` | **4288×2400** | CC BY-SA 2.0 | KOCIS / Korea.net official |

Inspected, not just listed. The hero candidate shows three 500 ml bottles in snow with **칠성사이다** in
Hangul, `Chilsung Cider` in English, the LOTTE mark, the seven stars the name refers to, `220 kcal` — and,
printed on the label, **"PURE, CLEAN, CAFFEINE FREE."** One of the target queries is answered *inside the
photograph.*

KOCIS Flickr is already an approved 2차 source in CLAUDE.md.

**Verdict: write.** Best demand, best images, and a headline-grade correction of what English currently says.

## ✅ Candidate 2 — 포카칩 (Pocachip)

**Target query:** `pocachip` / which flavour
**Shape:** identity + flavour decision

Korea's **No. 2 snack by revenue** (₩54.4bn H1) and its **fastest grower** (+8%), and English has no
explainer for it at all.

**Demand.** Google 8 branches, almost all flavour-decision: `pocachip original`, `onion`, `sweet cheese`,
`fresh potato`, `korean`, `orion`. Naver 9: `햇감자`, `어니언`, `오리지널`, `트리플페퍼`, `스윗치즈`, `칼로리`,
`페퍼`, and **`포카칩 뜻`** — Koreans themselves ask what the name means.

**Coverage gate: passes.** English supply is retailer listings (Amazon, HMart, Weee, Nikan, TryTheWorld),
`taquitos.net` (a snack database entry), namu EN, and one "best Korean chips" listicle. **No dedicated
explainer.** Same profile as 맛동산, which shipped successfully yesterday.

**The hook is already visible:** the **onion flavour launched in July 1988 and the "Original" only in 1992** —
it was called *Salted*, gained the English name in 1996, and was relabelled *Original* around 2006. The
original is not the original. That is the same class of fact as 밀키스/암바사 and 다시다/혼다시.

**Weaknesses, stated plainly.** Amazon autocomplete returns **1 branch** — the product is on Amazon
(Orion Poca Chip Original 60g, pack of 5) but the query is thin, so monetisation is weaker than 칠성사이다.
And the image gate returns **one** photo: `포카칩 사진.jpg`, 684×512, CC BY 2.0 KR. That is the Matdongsan
situation exactly — thin but sufficient, and it will need a comparison image to carry the page.

**Verdict: write, second.**

## ⏸ Candidate 3 — 꼬깔콘 (Kkokkal Corn)

Coverage gate **passes** — retailer listings, namu EN, a Tridge trade item, nothing else. And it has the best
*physical* hook of any candidate: **you put them on your fingertips and eat them off**, which is standard
behaviour in Korea and unknown abroad. Launched 1983-09-23; the name is a pun on 고깔 (cone) and corn.

**Held because demand is thin** — Google returns 2 branches. Good post, poor keyword. Reconsider as a section
inside a broader Korean-snack piece rather than a standalone.

## What to change next cycle

1. **The feed scan worked as intended and should stay** — it produced candidates in one pass and gave a
   sales-ranked frame the enumeration method never had. Keep it.
2. **But run the coverage gate against the sales ranking directly, early.** Half this cycle's effort went into
   candidates whose fame guaranteed they were taken. Testing rank 1–7 for English coverage *first* would have
   pointed at 포카칩 in one step.
3. **Run the duplicate audit before the demand test, not after.** 새우깡 was tested on three autocomplete
   engines before `029` was checked. The audit is cheaper.
4. **The image gate now earns its place in the shortlist, not after approval.** It separated the two survivors
   by a wide margin — 칠성사이다 has four verified files including three at 4K, 포카칩 has one at 684 px — and
   that difference will shape both articles.
