- Name: `EpicKor`
- One-line bio: `Korea travel, food, beauty, and culture guides from EpicKor Reels.`
- Optional short line: `Start with the guide that matches the Reel you just watched.`

## Operating Rule

- Keep only one direct blog link near the top: the current Reel guide.
- Rotate that current Reel guide whenever a new Reel becomes the active campaign.
- Keep the first button fixed as the `/instagram` hub.

## Measurement

- Littly analytics: first-button clicks and current-Reel button clicks.
- Site analytics/search tools: UTM landing sessions where available.
- Amazon: clicks and ordered items after traffic is routed through higher-intent guides.
