# Reel F — Suneung

## The one surprise this Reel proves

**A country grounds its aviation for a school listening test.** Aircraft hold above 3km, artillery
drills stop, the stock market opens an hour late — for twenty-five minutes of English audio.

## What makes this Reel different, and why it is an approved experiment

**There is no video of this event.** Suneung is not a category stock libraries carry, and we do not
shoot. The representative approved running it as a **one-off measurement**: does a stills-led Reel
land at all?

**Correction, after the cut plan was built: it does reach the floor.** Measured **58.8% video**
across 10 cuts — six video, four stills. The prediction above was wrong, and the reason is worth
recording: the shortfall I expected assumed the *only* honest footage would be of the exam itself.
It is not. The script names a school, streets, an intersection, a university — all of which exist as
Korea-verified footage, and none of which is padding, because each sits under the sentence that
names it. **The lesson is that a "no footage exists" topic often has footage for everything the
script says *around* the un-filmable thing.**

The stills still carry the human beats, which is the right division of labour: video for places,
stills for the four moments no camera crew was ever going to sell to a stock library — parents
praying at a closed gate, juniors saluting seniors in, the marker pen, the aerial crush at the gate.

If F still underperforms D and E on saves and completion, the conclusion is about the *topic* being
un-filmable at its core, not about the still/video ratio — which is now a cleaner experiment than
the one originally designed.

## Hook archetype

**Mystery, stated flat.** One sentence, no qualifiers: *"Once a year, South Korea stops every plane
in the sky."* No context until the viewer has already decided to stay.

## Visual system — **HOLD**

Designed for this Reel only. The subject is a country pausing, so the design language is an airport
information board and a countdown.

- **Palette:** night navy `#0b1220`, signal amber `#ffb020`, runway white `#f5f7fa`, alert red `#e03131`.
- **ONS:** **departure-board rows**. A label column and a status column, split-flap flipping into
  place one row at a time: `AIRCRAFT — HELD` · `ARTILLERY — STOPPED` · `MARKETS — 10:00` ·
  `HORNS — 200 m`. Each row lands on its own narration beat, so the board builds as the list is read.
- **The signature move:** a **35:00 countdown** runs in the corner through the middle section. It is
  the only persistent element in the batch. At the final beat it is **removed entirely** — the board
  and the clock both go, because the last beat is not data, it is the parents at the gate.
- **Captions:** **amber monospace on a thin dark bar** with a blinking cursor block, like a terminal
  readout. Visually unrelated to D's survey tape and E's paper index card.

## Stills discipline

Stills carry this Reel, so they must be treated as shots rather than filler:

- Every still is pre-composed to 1080×1920 as a blur-cover plate; none is left to `objectFit: cover`.
- Opposing drifts on consecutive plates so a run does not read as a slideshow.
- **Never three consecutive stills without a video cut** unless the board is actively building — the
  board animation counts as motion for that rule, but only where it is genuinely animating.

## Voice lane

`male_friend`, quiet. This script does not need selling; the facts are already loud. The last two
lines drop in pace deliberately.

## Funnel

Lands on `/culture` or `375`. Outro hook: **WE WROTE IT ALL DOWN** (reassurance mechanism) —
different from D and E in the same week.

## Verified facts used

- Next exam **Thursday 19 November 2026** — the third Thursday. Results 11 December 2026.
- English listening: aircraft takeoffs and landings suspended nationwide for about **35 minutes**
  (roughly 13:05–13:40); aircraft already airborne **hold above 3km**; helicopters and drones grounded.
- **Artillery fire and tank movement halt.**
- Markets shift to **10:00–16:30**; public offices and large firms asked to start at **10:00**.
- Within **200m** of a test centre: vehicle access restricted, parking banned, loudspeakers prohibited,
  horns discouraged; construction suspended nearby.
- **554,174** registered for the most recent exam — highest in seven years — of whom **28.9%** were
  graduates, i.e. resitting.
- Doors close **08:10**; the last section ends **17:45**.
