Once a year, South Korea stops every plane in the sky. ✈️

The next Suneung is Thursday 19 November 2026. Half a million people sit one exam, and for about 35 minutes in the early afternoon the country goes quiet for them:

✈️ Aircraft takeoffs and landings suspended nationwide — planes already up hold above 3 km
💥 Artillery drills and tank movement stop
📈 The stock market shifts to 10:00–16:30
🏢 Offices are asked to start at 10:00
🔇 No horns or loudspeakers within 200 m of a test centre
🚓 Police cars run late students to the gate with the sirens on

All of it for 25 minutes of English listening.

Because there's no second date. One Thursday a year — which is why about three in every ten people in that room are sitting it again.

And once the gate shuts, the parents don't go anywhere. They stand there for nine hours.
