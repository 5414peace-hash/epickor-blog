# Reel 260 Script

## Title

Korean Rice Cooker Guide 2026: Cuckoo, Cuchen, Home Rice

## Scene Script

1. Koreans do not pick a rice cooker like a cheap gadget.
2. The mistake is buying by price first, before you know how you eat rice.
3. Pressure cookers make stickier rice and handle mixed grains better. Basic cookers are simpler.
4. Size matters. Too big can dry out leftovers, and too small makes batch cooking annoying.
5. Check five things: household size, pressure type, mixed grains, keep-warm, and cleaning.
6. If you eat Korean meals often, the cooker becomes a daily appliance, not a once-a-week pot.
7. Save this before comparing Cuckoo or Cuchen. More Korean home guides at EpicKor.com.
