📊 THE NUMBER THAT MOVED
Foreign customers were 2% of Olive Young's domestic offline sales in 2022. They are now roughly 33%. Cumulative foreign spending in its Korean stores passed ₩1 trillion in August 2026 — three months earlier than the company expected.

There are about 1,300 branches nationwide. Nine of them are in Myeongdong, one to three minutes apart.

🛂 WHY EVERY TILL CARRIES THE SAME SIGN
The Myeongdong checkout hall prints it in three languages along fourteen lanes: PLEASE PREPARE YOUR PHYSICAL PASSPORT (FOR IMMEDIATE TAX REFUND) · 请准备好护照原件.

A photo of your passport does not work. Neither does a photocopy. The store prints it fourteen times because of how often people try.

💴 THE REFUND RULES, PLAINLY
▪️ Minimum ₩15,000 in a single store
▪️ Refund is roughly 5–8% of the purchase
▪️ Immediate refund at the till: keep one payment under ₩1,000,000
▪️ ₩5,000,000 total across your whole stay
▪️ Both caps were doubled on 1 January 2024 — any guide written before that understates them by half
▪️ Hygiene products, shopping bags and dried fruit are excluded

🗓️ WHEN TO GO
Olive Young publishes a sale calendar. A quarterly 빅세일 of about a week straddles the month boundary; 올영데이 runs monthly around the 25th–27th; a November Black Friday event leans toward imported and premium brands. Go in the morning, to a tourist-facing branch.

🧴 WHAT IT ACTUALLY COSTS
A mid-tier sun serum lists around ₩22,000. A 70-sheet toner pad tub lists ₩28,000 and was showing at ₩19,200 — 31% off — when this was checked. Lip tints sit around ₩10,000, which is why they are the correct answer to "I need six small gifts."
