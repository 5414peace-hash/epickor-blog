"Ajumma" is not simply the Korean word for "ma'am."

The word can carry age, familiarity, relationship, and social distance. That is why copying it directly from subtitles can sound warmer, rougher, or more personal than you intended.

With a stranger, "jeogiyo" is usually the safer way to get attention. Use the relationship and the setting, not an age guess.
