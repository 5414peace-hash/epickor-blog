Korea's most famous banana snack has been on the same shelf since 1978. It is 1.6% banana. 🇰🇷🍌

**May 1978** — the company that makes it is called 롯데공업. That month it renames itself 농심 (Nongshim), and 바나나킥 Banana Kick is the first snack released under the new name. Nearly fifty years later the bag still looks the same, and it still says 바나나맛 그대로 — "banana taste, just as it is."

**What is actually in it** — puffed corn, shaped like a banana, under a thin sugar coat. The banana is 1.6% powder. Variants run lower still, between roughly 0.2% and 0.9%.

**March 2025** — Jennie of BLACKPINK names it on a US talk show, and Korean sources put the resulting jump in Nongshim's market capitalisation at roughly ₩264 billion. Worth being precise about what happened there, because the English-language internet has it backwards: she did not make Banana Kick popular in Korea. It had been a staple for forty-seven years. She made it visible everywhere else.

**March 2026** — a twelve-month-old reaches into a bag on Instagram. Her mother says 안 되는데 — "you shouldn't." The baby freezes, looks at the bag, and scratches the back of her head. The clip passes 25.8 million views.

She can eat it because every puff is hollow and porous — it collapses against the roof of the mouth rather than crunching. Korean sources describe the intended eaters directly: children, people with weak teeth, and elderly denture wearers. There is nothing in the bag to bite through. 👶

**August 2026** — Nongshim makes one custom set with the child's real face on the packaging and the words 바나나킥 베이비, in a bag taller than she is. Korean press reported there are no plans to sell it.

So 1.6% is not a scandal. It is not competing with a banana — it is competing with the memory of one, which is why the recipe has not changed in nearly fifty years. 🥇
