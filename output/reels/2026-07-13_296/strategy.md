# Reel 296 Strategy - Hanok After Dark

## Source Gate

- Source: Blog 296, `Seoul Hanok Experience Guide 2026: Bukchon, Seochon, Crafts, Tea, and Summer Nights`
- Public URL: `https://www.epickor.com/blog/296`
- Status: representative-final-reviewed, published, deployed, and publicly verified before Reels production, as recorded in `HANDOFF.md`.
- Official fact check: Seoul Metropolitan Government confirms that tourist visits to the Bukchon-ro 11-gil red zone are restricted from 5:00 p.m. to 10:00 a.m., enforcement began March 1, 2025, and violations carry a KRW 100,000 fine. The rule applies to the designated red zone, not all of Bukchon.
- Current program check: Seoul Metropolitan Government confirms July-September 2026 public-hanok programs and Seoul Hanok Week from October 2-11, 2026. Program access does not create blanket permission to enter the restricted residential red zone; visitors must follow the venue's route and instructions.

## Target Viewer

An English-speaking Seoul traveler who treats Bukchon like an always-open attraction, searches for a cinematic night walk, or assumes an evening public-hanok event means every residential lane is open after dark.

## Hook System

Hook archetype: `mistake` plus `social rule`.

Thumbnail options:

1. Mystery: `BUKCHON HAS A CURFEW`
2. Mistake: `DON'T ENTER AFTER 5`
3. Decision: `BUKCHON OR SEOCHON?`

Spoken opening options:

1. `One of Seoul's prettiest hanok lanes can fine tourists who enter after five.`
2. `Bukchon looks like an open-air museum, but part of it closes to tourists at five.`
3. `If you want hanok after dark, don't wander into Bukchon's residential red zone.`

Selected hook unit:

- Thumbnail: `DON'T ENTER AFTER 5`
- Narration: option 1.
- First-frame visual requirement: real Bukchon residential-lane motion with the roofline and visitor context both visible; no generic palace, Japanese streetscape, empty title card, or misleading blanket `Bukchon closes` claim.
- Reason: this pair gives a concrete consequence immediately. The next sentence must clarify `the red zone around Bukchon-ro 11-gil`, preventing the hook from implying that every Bukchon business or authorized venue closes at 5 p.m.

## Story And Payoff

- Misconception: Bukchon is an always-open photo attraction.
- Concrete surprise: tourist visits to the designated red zone are restricted from 5 p.m. to 10 a.m., with a KRW 100,000 fine after enforcement began in 2025.
- Twist: authorized public-hanok night programs can exist while restricted residential lanes remain off-limits; the venue's route and instructions control access.
- Practical payoff: visit the red zone from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m.; after 5, use an authorized program or move to evening-friendly commercial areas such as Insadong or Ikseon-dong.
- Human line: `Pretty doesn't mean public.`
- Save/share reason: save the three-line time-and-place plan before a Seoul itinerary, or send it to the person planning the Bukchon photo stop.
- Funnel expectation: save first, profile/full-guide visit second; Blog 296 then supports a longer half-day route and practical travel preparation.

## Design System

- KEEP parent: `02 Steel After Dark` from Reel 294.
- Adaptation name: `Hanok After Dark`.
- Preserve: dramatic full-bleed photography, charcoal/ink foundation, large condensed uppercase promise, warm copper accent, ivory supporting text, restrained rules and fine grain.
- Replace: all literal metal, spoon, chopstick, banchan, and food motifs.
- New visual vocabulary: black roof tiles, warm wood, paper-window glow, stone alleys, shadowed eaves, one red-zone/time accent, and cinematic but truthful Seoul night color.
- Guardrail: dark does not mean crushed-black. Roof texture, people, signs, and lane boundaries must stay readable on a phone.

## Single Motion Card

- Count: exactly one.
- Placement: approximately 22-30 seconds, around 60-75% of the target runtime.
- Role: a three-row evening decision board over real, moving Korean hanok footage with a controlled dark veil and semi-transparent warm-black card.
- Spoken setup: `The easy plan is this.`
- Synchronized rows:
  1. `10 TO 5  →  BUKCHON RED ZONE`
  2. `AFTER 5  →  OFFICIAL HANOK EVENT`
  3. `EASY NIGHT  →  INSADONG / IKSEON`
- Payoff line: `Pretty doesn't mean public—plan the neighborhood like people live there.`
- Caption zone: a dedicated band above the lower Instagram UI and below the card rows; no badge, rule line, or footer may enter it.

## AI And Context Boundary

- Do not generate Bukchon streets, red-zone signs, residents, Korean text, enforcement scenes, official programs, or access routes.
- Do not use Japanese machiya, Chinese hutong/courtyard, palace-only imagery, or generic `Asian old town` footage.
- Official/reference images are acceptable when they are the most truthful evidence; record each source and use permission/context in `clip-sources.md`.
- Google Flow is unnecessary unless a later approved non-factual transition gap remains. Gemini Developer API remains prohibited without explicit representative approval.
