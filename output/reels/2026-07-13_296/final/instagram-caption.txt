One of Seoul’s prettiest hanok areas is also a real neighborhood—and one designated Bukchon zone has restricted tourist hours.

In the red-zone section of Bukchon-ro 11-gil, tourist visits are restricted from 5 p.m. to 10 a.m. Violations can result in a ₩100,000 fine.

The easy plan:
• Visit the designated red zone between 10 a.m. and 5 p.m.
• After five, choose an official public-hanok program.
• Or move to an evening-friendly commercial area in Jongno.
