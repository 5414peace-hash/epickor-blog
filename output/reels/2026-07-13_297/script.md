## Narration

If you call every Korean rice cake mochi, you miss most of the dessert table.

Tteok is a whole family. Injeolmi is soft and chewy with roasted soybean powder. Yakgwa is different: fried, dense, and syrup-soaked.

Then hangwa opens another lane, with crisp yugwa and gangjeong.

And the drinks split too. Sikhye is mild, cold, and malty, with rice grains. Sujeonggwa is cinnamon and ginger served chilled.

So pick by texture: chewy tteok, rich yakgwa, crisp hangwa, or a drink.

For a first tasting, pair one chewy sweet, one crisp or fried sweet, and tea. Save this before a Korean cafe, and get the full guide at epickor.com.
