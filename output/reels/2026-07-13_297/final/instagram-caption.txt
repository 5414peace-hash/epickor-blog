Korean traditional dessert is much bigger than “mochi.” The easiest way to choose is by texture.

• Chewy: tteok or injeolmi
• Rich and syrup-soaked: yakgwa
• Crisp: yugwa or gangjeong
• Drinkable: mild, malty sikhye or cinnamon-ginger sujeonggwa

For a first tasting, build contrast: one chewy sweet, one crisp or fried sweet, and tea. Fresh tteok is best eaten quickly; packaged yakgwa or crisp hangwa usually travels more easily.
