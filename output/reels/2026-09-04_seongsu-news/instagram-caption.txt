Korea's biggest fashion store is not in a fashion district. 👟

📍 IT IS IN SEONGSU
무신사 메가스토어 성수 opened on 24 April 2026 at 성동구 성수이로 62 — five floors from B1 to 4F, about a thousand brands. It is the largest Musinsa store in the country, and it is in an old shoe-and-leather factory quarter rather than Apgujeong or Myeongdong.

It is not alone there. Haus Nowhere Seoul opened in September 2025 at 뚝섬로 433, about fifteen minutes away on foot. Two of the city's largest K-fashion spaces, both in Seongdong-gu, both under eighteen months old. Guides written before that send you to the wrong side of the river.

📰 AND ABOUT THAT HEADLINE
Several Korean outlets ran "two-thirds of shoppers are foreigners." That was one day — 9 June 2026, a single-day peak of 66%.

The real figure across the first fifty days, 24 April to 13 June: about ₩3 billion of ₩7 billion in transactions came from foreign customers. Just over 40%. In the week ending 13 June it reached 56%.

Still remarkable, and it does not need exaggerating: four in every ten won spent in Korea's biggest fashion store, in its first two months, came from visitors.

🕚 THE PART NOBODY TELLS YOU
Nothing in Seongsu opens before 11:00. Both big buildings open at 11, Amomento Hannam at 12, and the Apgujeong pair stay open until 20:00 and 21:00. So the day runs Seongsu late morning → Hannam afternoon → Apgujeong before closing. Coffee at 10:30 is not a failure of planning; it is the schedule.

▪️ Korean visitors report queues at Haus Nowhere from about 13:00 at weekends — go before lunch
