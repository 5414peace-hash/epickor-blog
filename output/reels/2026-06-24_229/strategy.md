## Target Viewer

- Non-Korean travelers, expats, culture fans, and Korea-curious viewers who know "take off shoes" but do not understand the floor-centered logic behind it.
- Viewer misconception: "Koreans remove shoes because it is just a strict etiquette rule." The payoff is that the floor functions as clean living space.

## Creative Direction

- Hook archetype: `mystery` with `social rule` and `mistake` support.
- First-frame promise: `WHY THE FLOOR?`
- Open loop: "Why does one doorway rule explain shoes, slippers, low tables, luggage, and even heating?"
- Payoff: Korean floor culture is a clean-zone system shaped by floor living and ondol, not a random manners checklist.
- Keep-watching reason: each scene reveals a new layer: floor as furniture, shoes boundary, warm floor/ondol, modern flexibility, then the practical 3-zone rule.
- Save/share reason: save before visiting a Korean home, hanok stay, floor-seat restaurant, temple stay, jjimjilbang, clinic, or any shoe-off space.

5. Motion card payoff: the `3-zone rule` for shoes, socks/slippers, bathroom slippers.

7. Photo/outro: save cue and `epickor.com`.
