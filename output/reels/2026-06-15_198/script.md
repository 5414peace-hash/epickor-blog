## Title

Waterbomb Seoul Survival Guide

## Scene Script

1. Waterbomb is where your cute Seoul outfit can betray you.
2. It is a music festival, a water fight, and late-July humidity all at once.
3. So dress to dry, not just to pose.
4. Before you enter, seal your phone. Test the pouch with tissue at the hotel.
5. Keep the bag tiny: phone pouch, small towel, SPF, dry shirt. Leave the best bag at the hotel.
6. And plan the exit before your battery is red, because the real ending is getting home wet and tired.
7. Save this before Waterbomb Seoul. More Korea travel guides at epickor.com.
