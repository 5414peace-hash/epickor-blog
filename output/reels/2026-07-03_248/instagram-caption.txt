In Seoul, some night outs start with five kilometers, not a bar.

The Han River gives running crews a stage: bridges, lights, flat paths, and the easiest cool-down in the city.

If you join, check the pace first, stay to one side, watch bikes, and plan the cool-down. That is where people actually talk.
