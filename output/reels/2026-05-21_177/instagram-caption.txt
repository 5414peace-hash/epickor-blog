Korean cafe culture is not really about caffeine.

It is about finding a place:
a date spot,
a study room,
a shopping break,
a charging station,
a dessert stop,
or just somewhere to pause.

The trick is reading the cafe type.
Cheap chain, roaster, dessert cafe, work cafe, concept cafe.
Each one has different rules.

Save this before planning cafe stops in Seoul.
