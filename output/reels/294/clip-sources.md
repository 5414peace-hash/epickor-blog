## Research Conclusion

The opening and grip correction will therefore use direct article-owned metal-chopstick images with restrained motion/annotation. This is more truthful than using a wooden/dark chopstick video or an AI-generated hand demonstration. The accepted package still supplies four real-motion beats without requiring Google Flow or paid Gemini API generation.
