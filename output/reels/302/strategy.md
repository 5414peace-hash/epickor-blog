An English-speaking traveler who expects a single photogenic `traditional Korean breakfast`, assumes savory soup is unusual in the morning, or needs a practical food choice before an early Seoul itinerary.

## Hook System

Hook archetype: `mystery` plus `myth-bust`.

Thumbnail options:

1. Mystery: `SOUP AT 7AM?`
2. Mistake: `BREAKFAST ISN'T TOAST`
3. Decision: `PICK YOUR KOREAN MORNING`

Spoken opening options:

1. `Soup at seven in the morning? In Korea, that's not even the surprising part.`
2. `Korean breakfast isn't one traditional tray. It might be soup, toast, gimbap, or just coffee.`
3. `Don't search “breakfast” in Seoul. Search the dish you actually need.`

Selected hook unit:

- Thumbnail: `SOUP AT 7AM?`
- Narration: option 1.
- First-frame visual requirement: real steaming Korean soup/rice or hansik breakfast in portrait motion, with visible dish evidence before any clock graphic.
- Reason: the food proves the hook instantly, while the following line opens a broader misconception instead of treating soup itself as strange.

## Story And Payoff

- Misconception: Korea has one ceremonial breakfast tray or a fixed list of morning-only dishes.
- Concrete surprise: rice, soup, leftovers, gimbap, street toast, convenience food, bakery items, or only coffee can all fit a real morning.
- Twist: the most useful choice is not `authentic versus modern`; it is appetite, timing, and travel plan.
- Practical payoff: jet lag → juk; big walking day → gukbap; early train → gimbap; fun portable first bite → gilgeori toast.
- Human line: `The clock changes. The food doesn't have to.`
- Save/share reason: save the four-way chooser before an early Seoul day.
- Funnel expectation: saves and food shares first, profile/full-guide visit second, then qualified pantry/tool clicks.

## Design System

- Name: `Seoul 7:03 AM`.
- Palette: dawn blue `#2449d8`, yolk `#ffc928`, kimchi red `#ee3e2b`, rice `#fff8e8`, charcoal `#15171b`.
- Visual vocabulary: live clock stamps, tray-grid crops, steam wipes, breakfast receipt strips, and rapid morning-location changes.
- Rhythm: four distinct mornings in 35-40 seconds; real food motion leads, time graphics support.
- Guardrail: this is a city-morning broadcast, not a menu carousel. Avoid more than one structured board and avoid fake Korean store signage.

## Voice

- Lane: `female_culture_travel`.
- Delivery: awake, friendly, and curious; a sharp friend building a morning plan, not a food-documentary narrator.
- Pace: natural 1.0x with a light lift on `Soup at seven?` and clean dish pronunciation.
- Audition: opening through `The clock changes...` before full TTS.

## Single Motion Card

- Count: exactly one.
- Placement: approximately 22-30 seconds.
- Role: `choose your morning` board over real moving gimbap, soup, or Seoul-market footage with a warm translucent panel.
- Synchronized rows:
  1. `JET-LAGGED → JUK`
  2. `BIG WALKING DAY → GUKBAP`
  3. `EARLY TRAIN → GIMBAP`
  4. `FUN FIRST BITE → STREET TOAST`
- Payoff line: `Pick the morning you have—not the breakfast photo you expected.`
- Caption zone: clean lower band; no clock, receipt, or card row enters it.

## AI And Context Boundary

- Do not generate food handling, toast assembly, gimbap rolling, Korean packaging, store signage, or factual restaurant context.
- Reject Japanese sushi, Chinese soup, Indonesian instant noodles, or generic supermarket footage presented as Korea.
- Code-native clock and receipt graphics are allowed; they cannot replace direct food evidence.
- Gemini Developer API remains prohibited without explicit representative approval.
