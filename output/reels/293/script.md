# EpicKor Reels 2.0 Pilot — Seoul Book Space Decision Guide

Format: Korea Decision Guide  
Target length: 50 seconds  
Viewer payoff: one four-way rule for choosing a Seoul book space

## Narration

Seoul's most famous library might be the wrong stop for you. Don't choose by the prettiest photo. Choose what you want to do: buy, photograph, read quietly, or hunt used books. Each goal points to a different place.

For books, stationery, and K-pop albums in one stop, pick Kyobo Gwanghwamun. For the giant shelf photo, pick Starfield Library—but expect a busy public mall space, not silent study. For real quiet, Seoul Metropolitan Library is the better fit.

For used books and indie publishing, choose Seoul Book Bogo. Save this rule: buy, Kyobo; photo, Starfield; quiet, Metropolitan; used, Book Bogo. Check same-day hours before you cross Seoul. The full comparison is on epickor.com.

## Autonomous approval contract

- Topic selection, sourcing, scene design, narration, rights checks, render QA, and internal revisions are handled without representative review.
- A source with unclear reuse rights, visible non-Korean context presented as Korea, or an unverifiable place claim is rejected automatically.

- A rejection starts one internal revision pass based on the stated reason; it does not reopen all intermediate choices.
