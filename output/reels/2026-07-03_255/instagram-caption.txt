Seoul food halls are not mall food courts. That is the first mistake.

They are part bakery street, part gift market, part lunch stop, and part rainy-day shelter.

Browse once, then buy with a plan: one main bite, one dessert, one gift, seating, receipts, then leave before you overbuy.

Save this for a rainy Seoul shopping day.
