# Reels Script - 185

## Part 1

Seoul is famous for moving fast. So of course it made doing nothing a competition.

At the Hangang Space-Out Competition, people sit still for 90 minutes. No phone. No talking. No productive little task.

And that is why the joke works. In a city built around speed, stillness suddenly looks radical.

## Part 2

The Han River matters here. It is not just a view. It is where Seoul goes to recover.

People bring mats, snacks, drinks, tiny lamps, and just enough room to breathe for a while.

Korea often turns rest into a format: cafe break, jjimjilbang, temple stay, river picnic.

## Part 3

So the lesson for travelers is simple. Do less in Seoul, but do it better.

Leave one evening open. Sit by the river. Take the cafe break without performing the whole trip.

If your Seoul itinerary is already packed, do not add wellness as one more task. Replace something.

Save this before you overplan Seoul.
