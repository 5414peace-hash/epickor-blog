# Reels Script - Blog 188

## Voiceover

Seoul is the Korea most travelers meet first.

Busan is the Korea that changes the mood.

If this is your first trip and you have under five full days, pick Seoul.

It gives you palaces, shopping, K-beauty, nightlife, cafes, and the easiest transit in one tight city.

But if you want sea air, seafood, beaches, coastal walks, and a slower night, Busan is not a side quest.

It is a different Korea texture.

Here is the rule.

Short first trip? Seoul first.

Six or seven days? Seoul plus Busan works.

Repeat trip? Give Busan real time.

Do not add Busan because you feel guilty.

Travel by fit, not by checklist.

Save this before you book Korea.
