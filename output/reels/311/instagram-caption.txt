Tteokbokki isn't one dish. 🌶️

Six versions share one rice cake — the original soy-based gungjung, classic gochujang heat, rose cream, mala, cheese-buried, and soupy jjukkumi-style. Order the wrong one and you'll wonder what the hype was about. Order the right one for your spice level and it all makes sense.

The full guide breaks down all six, how spicy each one really is, and what to add at the counter.
