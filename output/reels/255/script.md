# Reel 255 Script

## Title

Korean Department Store Food Halls 2026: What To Eat In Seoul

## Scene Script

1. Seoul food halls are not mall food courts. That is the first mistake.
2. They are part bakery street, part gift market, part lunch stop, and part rainy-day shelter.
3. Start with one real food, then one dessert. Do not turn the basement into a panic buffet.
4. For travelers, the hidden value is weather-proof food, receipts, restrooms, gifts, and subway access.
5. Use the food hall map: main bite, dessert, gift, seating, receipts, then leave before you overbuy.
6. Buy fragile cream cakes last. Pack sealed snacks, tea, seaweed, or sweets if they need to fly.
7. Save this for a rainy Seoul shopping day. More Seoul food guides at epickor.com.
