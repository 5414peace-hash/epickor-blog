# Reels Script - Blog 189

Target duration: 34-38 seconds

## Part 1

Most tourists think the DMZ is one place.

It is not.

Imjingak, Dora Observatory, the Third Tunnel, and the JSA are different levels of access.

And a normal DMZ tour does not automatically include the JSA.

## Part 2

The biggest thing to understand is control.

You are not wandering around a border like a museum.

You are following a tour route, with passport checks, military rules, and access that can change.

## Part 3

Bring your passport.

Wear real walking shoes, because the Third Tunnel is more physical than it looks.

And do not book something tight right after, because the schedule can shift.

Save this before you book a DMZ tour from Seoul.
