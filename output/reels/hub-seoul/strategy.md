# Reel C — Seoul neighbourhoods · creative brief

**Slug**: `hub-seoul` · **Destination**: `https://www.epickor.com/seoul`
**Approved**: representative, 2026-08-04 (2nd script draft)

## Hook archetype — **Mystery (rule first)**

Opens on a rule that sounds invented: **there is a street in Seoul where you may not be a tourist
after 5pm, and the fine is ₩100,000.** True since March 2025, and almost no English travel content
carries it.

**First-frame promise**: a crowd in rented hanbok at the palace, 59.94 fps — people moving.
**Viewer misconception**: that the famous neighbourhood is the one worth going to.

## Payoff — a cut, not a sentence

**Euljiro: metal shops all day, same street, same doors — and bars at night.** The reveal is a hard
cut from the daytime tool-shop alley to the neon night alley. The line that lands on it is
*"Nobody puts a sign up. You're just supposed to know."*

## Save / share reason

Four neighbourhood facts that each invert an assumption: Bukchon has enforced quiet hours because
people live there; Ikseon-dong is a **1920s speculative housing development**, not a preserved
village; Yeonnam's park is a **buried railway**; Mullae's steel workshops never left.
