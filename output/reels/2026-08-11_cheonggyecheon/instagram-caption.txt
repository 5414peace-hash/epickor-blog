Twenty years ago, this was a six-lane motorway. 🛣️

Cheonggyecheon was buried under concrete in the 1950s — it smelled, the city was broke, and paving it over was the cheap answer. Then they built an elevated freeway on top, and for thirty years that was simply what this part of Seoul was.

In 2003 the city did the thing almost nobody does. They tore the freeway down. 5.84 km, two years and three months, about ₩386.7 billion. They pulled the lid off and let the water back out.

The mayor who pushed it through was president two years later. And the city measures the corridor at roughly 3°C cooler than the street above — so that breeze isn't landscaping. It's a road that isn't there any more.
