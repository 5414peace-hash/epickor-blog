# Reel D — Cheonggyecheon

## The one surprise this Reel proves

**A city deleted a motorway.** Everywhere else, cities build them. Seoul took a six-lane elevated
freeway off its stilts and dug the buried stream back out from underneath it.

The whole Reel exists to land that reversal. Everything else is evidence.

## Hook archetype

**Contradiction in frame one.** The opening image is water, trees and people walking — and the first
spoken words are "you're looking at a highway." The viewer's eyes and ears disagree for two seconds,
which is the reason to stay.

Not a title card. Not a mood shot. The stream is already moving in frame 1 (`31714020` — people
walking toward camera down the corridor, towers behind).

## Viewer misconception → payoff

**Misconception:** this is an old stream that Seoul happens to have kept, i.e. heritage.
**Payoff:** it is the *opposite* of kept. It was buried, paved, built over, and then all of that was
demolished on purpose. The green is younger than most of the people walking in it.

## Why 9:16 is lucky here

The stream is a vertical corridor between buildings. Every other place topic loses its subject in the
crop; this one gains — the water leads the eye straight up the frame into the skyline, which is the
composition the thesis needs. Recorded because it is the exception to the `namsan-hillside` failure.

## Visual system — **EXCAVATION**

Designed for this Reel only. Nothing from the drinks/ramyun/seoul batch is reused.

- **Palette:** wet concrete `#2c3033`, survey yellow `#ffd200`, water teal `#1d7d8c`, chalk `#f2f0eb`.
- **ONS:** copy arrives from *behind a horizontal cut line* that slides down the frame like a
  section being opened — text is revealed by the passing line, not by a wipe from the left. Kicker is
  a **survey-tape strip** (black/yellow diagonal hazard rule), not a solid chip.
- **The signature move:** the buried→uncovered beat is a **strike-through**: `HIGHWAY` is drawn, then
  a yellow rule cuts through it and `STREAM` rises underneath. That is the entire film in one gesture.
- **Captions:** a low, flat **survey-tape bar** — no rounded corners, no drop shadow. Left edge is a
  yellow/black hazard end-cap. Reads as site signage rather than a subtitle box.
- **Numbers get a monospace treatment** (`5.84 km`, `₩386.7 bn`, `2003`) because the argument is
  civil-engineering, and figures should look measured rather than shouted.

## Verified facts used

- 청계고가도로 demolition completed **3 August 2003**.
- Restoration **1 July 2003 → 1 October 2005**, 2 years 3 months.
- **5.84 km**, Dong-A Ilbo building at Gwanghwamun → Sindap railway bridge, Seongdong-gu.
- Cost approximately **₩386.7 billion**.
- Driven by mayor **이명박**, who became president in 2008.
- Cooling: Seoul Facilities Corporation reports the restored corridor runs **about 3°C cooler** than
  surrounding streets. Academic measurements vary by method and point (0.3–3°C+), so the Reel
  attributes the figure to the city rather than stating it flat.
