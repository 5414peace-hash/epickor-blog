Seoul's busiest weekend subway station is not Hongdae. It is not Gangnam. 🚇

Seoul Metro published its first-half ridership analysis on 23 July 2026, and one station stood out for a reason that has nothing to do with commuting.

📊 동묘앞 DONGMYO-AP · Line 1 and Line 6
Weekend daily average — 28,785
Weekday daily average — 19,673
That is 9,112 more people on a weekend day, up 46.3%, the largest gap of any station in Seoul. Seoul Metro named the flea market as the cause.

Look at the company it keeps. Second is World Cup Stadium at 40.6%, which fills because there is a match on. Hongdae, the shopping district everyone has heard of, swings 8.7%.

🪦 AND TWO YEARS AGO THIS MARKET WAS DECLARED FINISHED
In December 2024 Korean business media ran its obituary. A vendor of thirty years told The Scoop the market "feels finished" — customers browsed and left. Clothing had fallen to 3.9% of household spending, a record low against 7–8% a decade earlier. Supply of used clothing reaching wholesalers was down 20–30%.

Then foreign shoppers arrived. English-language coverage tells only that second half.

🧭 IF YOU GO
▪️ 동묘앞역, Line 1 or Line 6, Exit 3, about five minutes on foot
▪️ Open year-round, no closing day, roughly 600 stalls
▪️ New stock lands Saturday afternoon — which is also the worst crowding of the week. There is no version where you get both
▪️ The street piles are cash only. Small notes
