# Reels V2 Script - 186 Honbap / Honsul

Target duration: 34-38 seconds

## Part 1

Eating alone in Korea used to feel awkward.

Now it has a name.

Honbap means eating alone. Honsul means drinking alone.

And no, it does not automatically mean lonely.

## Part 2

Korea still loves group meals.

But more people live alone, work weird hours, travel solo, and eat on their own schedule.

So the food system adapted: counter seats, convenience-store meals, delivery, cafes, and one-person bowls.

## Part 3

Start with kimbap, noodles, gukbap, food courts, or convenience stores.

For Korean BBQ, ask first.

Say: honja meogeodo dwaeyo?

That means, is it okay to eat alone?

Save this before your solo Korea trip.
