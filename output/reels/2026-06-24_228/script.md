# Reel 228 Script - Korea Temple Stay

## Scene 1
A Korean temple stay is not a spa night with better scenery. It is a real temple schedule.

## Scene 2
You are joining a hosted cultural program inside an active Buddhist temple, with guides, monks, staff, and rules.

## Scene 3
So pick the program type first: day program, rest stay, experience stay, or overnight. Do not book by the prettiest photo.

## Scene 4
Depending on the temple, you might meditate, drink tea, eat simply, join chanting, or do one hundred eight bows.

## Scene 5
The basic rule is simple: dress modestly, keep your phone quiet, arrive on time, and ask before filming people or ceremonies.

## Scene 6
For Seoul, choose easy access. For a mountain temple, check weather, stairs, bedding, luggage, and the last bus.
