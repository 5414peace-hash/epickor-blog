## Target Viewer

- Non-Korean travelers, Korea-curious culture fans, wellness travelers, and first-time visitors considering a temple stay.
- Viewer misconception: "A temple stay is basically a scenic spa or quiet hotel night."
- Real payoff: it is a structured cultural program in an active Buddhist temple; the right choice depends on program type, rules, energy, transport, and expectations.

## Creative Direction

- Hook archetype: `mistake` plus `decision`.
- First-frame promise: `NOT A SPA NIGHT`.
- Open loop: "If it is not a spa, what actually happens and how do I avoid booking the wrong one?"
- Payoff: choose the program type before choosing the prettiest photo, then follow a few simple etiquette/logistics rules.
- Keep-watching reason: each scene reveals a practical hidden layer: active temple, program choice, real activities, rules, mountain logistics.
- Save/share reason: save before booking so you do not choose the wrong temple stay or behave like it is a themed hotel.
