In 1989 an anonymous letter destroyed Korea's oldest noodle company. It was not true. 🇰🇷

**1963** — Samyang Foods launches 삼양라면, the first instant noodle made in Korea. Their own television advertisement calls it 우리나라 최초의 INSTANT 라면. By the late 1980s the company holds roughly 60% of the Korean ramyeon market.

**3 November 1989** — prosecutors receive an anonymous letter claiming Samyang fries its noodles in industrial-grade beef tallow. Executives are arrested. The press runs with it.

Within about three months the Dobong-dong plant has shut and market share has fallen from roughly 60% to 15% — three quarters of the business, over an allegation nobody had proven. Koreans still call it 우지 파동, the beef tallow crisis.

**26 August 1997** — investigators, and then the courts, find the tallow was edible all along. The Supreme Court acquits the company, nearly eight years after the accusation. By then it does not matter.

**3 November 2025** — the same calendar date, thirty-six years later, Samyang launches 삼양1963. And it is fried in beef tallow. The exact ingredient. 🍜

Look at the front of the bag: 제품 중 우지 6.87%, 비프추출물 1.05%, 사골풍미분 1.02% 함유 — on a 131 g packet, printed on the front, not buried in a panel on the back. About five months in it was selling roughly seven million packets a month.
