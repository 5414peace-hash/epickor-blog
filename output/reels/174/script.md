## Why this Reel exists

Reel 171 published 2026-07-22 and averaged **5 seconds of watch time on a 41-second Reel,
with zero saves and zero shares** (`output/reels/metrics.json`). It listed information.
Nobody saves a list. Etiquette is different: getting it wrong is embarrassing in public,
so "don't be the person everyone notices" is a reason to save and to send to the friend
you're travelling with. That is the specific failure this Reel is built to fix.

## The one surprise this Reel proves

A Seoul train can be completely packed and still be silent. Not polite-quiet — actually
silent. The Reel earns that claim by showing the crowd first and the silence second.

## Narration — natural American English, mid-twenties, conversational

There are like a thousand people moving through this station right now, and it's completely
silent.

Not quiet. Silent. Nobody's on speakerphone, nobody's taking a call, nobody's playing a video
out loud — and honestly that's the first thing that messes with you.

The rules aren't posted anywhere. They're just understood. You don't eat on the train. You
don't put your bag on the seat next to you.

And those pink seats? Those are for pregnant passengers, and people will stand right next to
an empty one for forty minutes rather than take it.

So the fastest way to look like you've done this before is honestly just to say nothing.
Headphones in, face the doors.

Everybody else already is.
