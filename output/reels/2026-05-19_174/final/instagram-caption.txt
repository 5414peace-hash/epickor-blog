🚇 Seoul subway etiquette is not only about reading the map.

🇰🇷 The quiet rules matter:
Let people exit first
Keep your voice low
Use headphones
Leave priority seats open if you do not need them
Take backpacks off in crowded cars
Keep food and strong smells off the train
