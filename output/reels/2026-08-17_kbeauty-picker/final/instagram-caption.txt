Nobody in Korea pays the list price. 🧾

Four Korean bottles, four different problems, and the real Olive Young numbers as of August 2026 — not the list price on the box.

COSRX Advanced Snail 96 Mucin Essence (스네일 96 뮤신 에센스) lists at ₩23,000. It sells between roughly ₩14,000 and ₩20,000 depending on the promotion week, and it is stocked in every branch in the country. For flaking and that tight feeling after a wash.

Anua Heartleaf 77 Toner (어성초 77 수딩 토너) lists at ₩25,000, cycles near ₩19,900, and yes — it smells faintly of fish for about a minute. That is the heartleaf, not a bad bottle. For skin that is shiny by noon and red after washing.

Round Lab 1025 Dokdo Toner (1025 독도 토너) is the cheapest of the four at about ₩10,900 for the 200ml refill. The 1025 is not a formula code. It is a date — October 25, Korea's Dokdo Day.

Then the one that should change how you shop.

Torriden Dive-In Serum (다이브인 히알루론산 세럼) lists at ₩25,500. Olive Young's standing bundle lands it near ₩22,000. Torriden's own Korean mall lists it at ₩16,900. The manufacturer undercuts the retailer by ₩5,100 — so on this one, the big chain is not the cheap option. 💧

That is the thing about buying Korean skincare from outside Korea: the price you are shown is almost never the price Koreans pay, and the cheapest seller is not always the one you would guess.
