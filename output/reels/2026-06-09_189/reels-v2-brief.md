## Core Hook

Most tourists think the DMZ is one place. It is not.

## Viewer Promise

In under 40 seconds, explain the three mistakes first-time DMZ visitors make: confusing DMZ with JSA, forgetting passport/control rules, and treating the Third Tunnel like an easy museum hallway.

## Tone

Calm, serious, practical. The Reel should feel like a smart travel warning, not fear content. Respect the history without making the video heavy.
