Eating alone in Korea is not automatically sad. It has a whole vocabulary now.

Honbap = eating alone.
Honsul = drinking alone.

Korea still loves group meals, but solo travelers, one-person households, convenience stores, delivery apps, and counter seats changed the table.

Save this before your solo Korea trip.
