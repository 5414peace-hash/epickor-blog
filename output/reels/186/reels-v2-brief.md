# Reels V2 Brief - 186 Honbap / Honsul

Source post: `content/blog/186.md`

Working title: `Eating alone in Korea has a name`

## V2 Objective

Make honbap feel like a clear cultural shift, not a sad solo-dining story. The viewer should understand that Korea still has strong group meals, but solo meals are now normal enough to have everyday vocabulary.

## Hook

Primary hook:

> Eating alone in Korea used to feel awkward. Now it has a name.

Backup hooks:

- Korea did not stop loving group meals. It just made room for one.
- If you travel Korea alone, learn these two words.
- Honbap is not lonely by default. It is modern Korea at one seat.

## Viewer Promise

In under 40 seconds, explain:

- what honbap means.
- what honsul means.
- why one-person households changed the food table.
- where solo travelers should eat first.

## Scene Plan

| Scene | Beat | Visual Direction | Screen Text | Motion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Hook | one person at a noodle/counter seat | `Eating alone?` | Fast zoom |
| 2 | Name the behavior | typography over solo meal | `Honbap.` | Word pop |
| 3 | Add honsul | quiet drink/snack table | `Honsul.` | Word pop |
| 4 | Cultural shift | mini stat card: one-person households | `Korea made room for one.` | Motion card 1 |
| 5 | Solo-friendly foods | kimbap, ramyeon, gukbap, convenience store | `Start here.` | Quick cuts |

| 8 | Payoff / CTA | clean solo table, epickor.com | `Save this before Seoul.` | Outro |

## Production Notes

- Duration target: 34-38 seconds.
- Exactly two motion inserts:
  - Scene 4: stat card.
  - Scene 7: phrase card.
- Keep tone light, not lonely.
- Use one playful beat around Korean BBQ: "Do not force a group meal to become a solo mission."
- CTA: `Save this before your solo Korea trip. More Korea culture: epickor.com`
