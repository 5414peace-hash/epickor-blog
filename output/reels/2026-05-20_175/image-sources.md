**Consequence stated plainly:** the narration says Dongdaemun runs till dawn, but no footage exists
that shows that market at night. On-screen copy therefore claims only what the frame proves —
`AFTER DARK / It doesn't stop` — and the false `NAMDAEMUN` label was removed from the 방산시장 shot,
because Bangsan Market is a different market.
