# Reels Script - Blog 190

Target duration: 34-38 seconds

## Part 1

Save these three numbers before you get sick in Korea.

119 is for ambulance, fire, and real emergencies.

1330 is the Korea travel hotline.

1339 is for public-health guidance.

## Part 2

Korean healthcare is strong, but you still need to choose the right door.

For a mild cold, stomach issue, or motion sickness, a pharmacy may be the fastest first stop.

Need a doctor, but it is not an emergency?

Look for a local clinic.

## Part 3

If it is serious, do not overthink it.

Use a hospital or call 119.

Bring travel insurance, your passport, medication names, and allergy info.

This is travel prep, not medical advice.
