# Reels V2 Brief - Blog 190

## Source Post

- Draft path: `content/blog/190.md`

## Core Hook

Save these three numbers before you get sick in Korea.

## Viewer Promise

In under 40 seconds, give travelers the practical healthcare triage they need: 119 for emergencies, 1330 for tourist help, 1339 for public-health guidance, and when to choose pharmacy vs clinic vs hospital.

## Tone

Practical, calm, non-alarming. This should feel like a useful travel safety note, not medical advice or fear content.

## Visual Direction

- Open with a bold number-card intro, then cut to National Medical Center or emergency center.
- Use the ambulance visual for 119.
- Use pharmacy/clinic-style motion card for non-emergency triage if no pharmacy photo is available.
- Avoid graphic medical imagery.

## Motion Cards

Use exactly two motion cards.

1. `number_board`
   - Rows: `119 emergency`, `1330 travel help`, `1339 health guidance`
   - Beat: right after hook.

2. `decision_table`
   - Rows: `Mild symptoms -> pharmacy`, `Doctor needed -> clinic`, `Urgent -> hospital / 119`
   - Beat: after explaining not every problem starts at a big hospital.

## Scene Beats

1. Number-card hook: `Save these before Korea.`
2. Hospital exterior: `Korea has strong healthcare.`
3. Motion card 1: `119 / 1330 / 1339`
4. Emergency center: `Use emergency care for real emergencies.`
5. Motion card 2: `Pharmacy, clinic, hospital.`
6. Ambulance: `Do not wait if symptoms are serious.`
7. CTA: `Save before your Korea trip.`

## Production Notes

- Duration target: 34-38 seconds.
- Must not sound like medical diagnosis.
- Keep the phrase `This is travel prep, not medical advice` if space allows.
- CTA: `Save these numbers before your Korea trip. More Korea travel: epickor.com`
