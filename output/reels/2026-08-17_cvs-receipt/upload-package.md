## Why this topic was chosen for the kit

`korean convenience store breakfast` is the site's highest-CTR query at **14.75%**, and post `171`
already carries an itemised price table with Hangul. That table is the entire input the RECEIPT
kit needs — no photography at all, which is the reason the kit exists.

## The reversal

The receipt prints, the total lands, and then a 2025 Hankyung headline arrives:
**1000원으로 삼각김밥도 못 산다** — the cheapest line on the receipt has moved out of reach of the
smallest banknote. That only works because it comes *after* the total, which is an ordering a
carousel cannot enforce.

## Ranges stay ranges

Every figure prints as post `171` verified it. A receipt with a price range on it is slightly odd,
and inventing a single number to make it look like a real transaction would be worse.

**Post 171 was corrected alongside this reel**: it described ₩1,000 as a coin. Korean coins stop at
₩500 — ₩1,000 is a banknote — and the whole point of the headline depends on that.
