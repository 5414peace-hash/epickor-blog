A Korean convenience-store breakfast, itemised. Prices read August 2026. 🇰🇷🧾

삼각김밥 triangle gimbap — ₩1,200–1,900. The two-pack, smaller ones, ₩2,000–2,300.
바나나맛우유 banana milk, 240 ml — ₩1,800. The brown-and-yellow pot-shaped bottle everyone photographs.
Brewed coffee — from ₩1,000, poured from the machine by the counter.
샌드위치 sandwich — ₩2,500–4,500.

A real breakfast: ₩3,000–5,000. One gimbap and one drink. That is what it actually comes to, and it is why the convenience store is a genuine breakfast option in Korea rather than a snack stop. 🍙

Here is the part that changed. A 2025 Hankyung headline ran: 1000원으로 삼각김밥도 못 산다 — "you can't even buy a triangle gimbap with ₩1,000." ₩1,000 is Korea's smallest banknote; the coins stop at ₩500. The cheapest hot-counter staple has moved out of reach of the smallest note you carry.

So budget ₩3,000 rather than ₩1,000, and you will eat well before 8am. 💸
