## Creative Brief

- Target viewer: someone planning a Seoul trip who defaults to "N Seoul Tower" as the only observatory option.
- Misconception: N Seoul Tower is the only/best Seoul view — in fact three towers compete on genuinely different strengths (height, view content, price, crowd).
- Hook archetype: **decision**.
- First-frame promise: **"Wrong tower, wasted night"**.
- Tension/open loop: three towers, three different views — which one matches the trip you're actually planning?
- Payoff: a fast decision rule by what the viewer actually wants (romance/couples view, tallest/bragging-rights view, or budget/skip-the-line view).
- Why keep watching: rapid skyline comparison building to a clear pick.
- Save/share reason: practical pre-trip decision tool.
- Voice lane: **female_culture_travel**.
- Thumbnail directions:
  - Mystery: "Not The One You Think"
  - Mistake: "Wrong Tower, Wasted Night"
  - Decision: "Which Seoul View Wins?"
  - **Selected: Mistake** — "Wrong Tower, Wasted Night" — highest stakes framing, pairs with skyline hook.
- Spoken opening variants:
  1. "Everyone defaults to the same Seoul tower. It's not always the right call."
  2. "Three towers. Only one is right for your night."
  3. "You've picked the wrong Seoul view before you even land."
  - **Selected: #3** — sharpest stakes, pairs with the Mistake thumbnail.
- Motion card: **zero** (Reels 2.2 default; payoff is the on-screen decision line over real skyline footage).
