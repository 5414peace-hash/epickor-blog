🌶️ Everyone's terrified of Korean spicy food. Most of it isn't what you think.

🇰🇷 The real numbers:
Buldak fire noodle: ~4,400 Scoville (barely more than a jalapeño)
A jalapeño: up to 8,000 Scoville
Cheongyang chili: 5x a jalapeño — the pepper Koreans actually reach for
Gochujang: usually milder than Sriracha

😤 Next time, just say "an-map-gae" (not spicy) before you order.
