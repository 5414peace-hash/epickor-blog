# Reel A — Korean drinks · creative brief

**Slug**: `hub-drinks` · **Destination**: `https://www.epickor.com/drinks`
**Approved**: representative, 2026-08-04 (2nd script draft)

## Why this is a category Reel

The first plan tried one Reel per post and seven of ten topics failed the footage gate. The
representative's correction: build by category, as the hubs do. That widened the pool from a
six-clip slice to the whole 68-clip inventory, and it changed the architecture — **video carries
the scenes, our own stills carry the products.** Stock has no Korean pack shots; we own 241.

## Hook archetype — **Mystery (object first)**

Not "here are three Korean drinks." A refrigerator is driving down a road, and we ask who is
riding it. The object is strange enough to hold three seconds on its own.

**First-frame promise**: a moving vehicle on a Seoul road, then the reveal that it is a fridge.
**First 1.5 seconds**: traffic in motion — the movement is in the footage, not in a camera push.

## Viewer misconception

That Korean drinks are a novelty aisle — sweet, cartoonish, interchangeable. The Reel replaces
that with: *the names are translated wrong, and the delivery system is stranger than the drinks.*

## Payoff

Not saved to the end. **Given away in the first five seconds and then explained** — that is the
change from the rejected draft. The satisfaction is the accumulation: a fridge that is legally a
moped → a drink that is not yoghurt → a cider with no apples that predates Sprite → a tonic that
is legally a drug.

## Save / share reason

It answers a question people actually type — *is Korean cider alcoholic, is it halal, does it have
caffeine* — and it hands over a fact that survives retelling: **Sprite arrived eleven years late.**

## Voice lane

**`female_culture_travel`.** The script is observational and slightly amused ("이런 게 영어로는 어디에도
안 적혀 있더라고요"). The male_friend lane pushes toward challenge-and-dare, which is the wrong
register for a piece whose punchline is a municipal licensing rule.

Natural speaking speed. No blanket 0.8x slowdown.

## Motion cards

**Zero.** Per the 2026-07-21 rule. The payoff is the cart itself, which is an image, not a board.

## Thumbnail directions — three, to be rendered and picked

| Direction | Copy (3–5 words) | Frame |
|---|---|---|
| **Mystery** | `WHO DRIVES THIS?` | COCO cart, full frame |
| **Mistake** | `IT'S NOT YOGURT` | Yakult bottle mid-pour |
| **Decision** | `CIDER WITH NO APPLES` | Chilsung green bottle, label legible |

Copy must not restate the article title. All three sit inside the profile-grid safe area.

## Funnel expectation

Reel → `/drinks` hub, not a single post. The hub carries the comparison table and eight product
articles, so a viewer who wants one answer and a viewer who wants all of them both land correctly.
The hub is four days old and has no inbound social traffic yet.

## Viral Fit Score — **86 / 100**

| | |
|---|---|
| Hook strength | 19/20 — a fridge in traffic is self-explaining |
| Payoff clarity | 17/20 — four beats, all concrete, none requiring a graphic |
| Korea specificity | 20/20 — nothing here exists outside Korea |
| Visual supply | 16/20 — 8 video + 8 stills confirmed, but stills carry every product |

Above the 80 threshold. Weakest column is visual supply, which is the known pack-shot constraint.

## Cut discipline — the representative's instruction

**Cuts land on narration, not on a grid.** Production order is therefore inverted:
TTS first → forced alignment → cut points derived from word timings → assets trimmed to fit.
Not storyboard-then-fit, which drifts.
