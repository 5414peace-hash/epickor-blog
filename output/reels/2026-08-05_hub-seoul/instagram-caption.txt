There's a street in Seoul where you can't be a tourist after 5pm. 🏘️

Bukchon fines you ₩100,000, and they do collect it — because those hanok are houses with people inside them.

That's the rule for the whole city. The neighbourhoods that look like attractions usually aren't, and the ones that look like nothing usually are. Ikseon-dong looks 300 years old and went up in the 1920s as a housing development. Yeonnam's park is shaped like a railway because it was one. Mullae is a working steel district that artists moved into, not over. Euljiro sells tools all day and becomes bars at night, with no sign anywhere.
