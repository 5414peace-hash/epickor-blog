# Reels Script - Blog 187

## Voiceover

Some Korean superstitions sound fake until they change what people actually do.

Like this: in Korea, some elevators use F instead of 4, because four sounds close to death.

And this one matters more: do not write a living person's name in red ink.

It can feel rude, unlucky, or death-coded. Use black or blue. Easy.

Then there is fan death. No, a normal fan is not secretly magic.

But the belief is famous enough that Korean fans often have timers.

So if your host says, "Set the timer," do not turn it into a debate.

There are also exam rules, dating rules, and old night warnings.

Seaweed soup before a test? Bad joke. Shoes for your partner? Risky joke.

You do not have to believe every superstition.

You just need to know which ones make a moment awkward.

Save this before your Korea trip.
