## Why this topic gets the Timeline frame

Post 379's spine is a schedule, and its actual advice is a **date problem**: the show is 7 November, but
the only thing a reader has to act on falls in late August. Timeline makes time the layout axis — a
media band across the top, a large readout beneath it, and a calendar rail whose head sits wherever the
current sentence is.

So when the narration reaches tickets, the rail head jumps **backwards**, from `NOV 7` to `LATE AUG`.
That is the article's argument drawn rather than narrated. It is not a bug and it should not be
smoothed into a monotonic sweep.

## The 2026 dates are not published, and the frame says so

The official site lists the date and venue and nothing else; its notice board has not moved since
November 2025. A timeline graphic is precisely the kind of thing that implies certainty it does not
have, so:

- The HUD reads `2025 PATTERN` / `2026 UNCONFIRMED` on both ticket cuts.
- The rail's `LATE AUG` and `1 SEP` stops are **2025** dates (early-bird opened 22 August; general sale
  1 September at 2:00pm through YES24 and the Busan Bank app). Only `NOV 7` is a confirmed 2026 fact.
- Cut 4's readout `LATE AUG` sits under the label *"When to watch, if 2026 follows"*.
- The narration hedges in the same place: *"For 2026 nothing has been published yet, and if it follows
  last year, that window is late August."*

## Hook archetype

**Structural correction.** The first sentence claims the bridge is the launcher, and the first frame is
fireworks running along its span with cascades falling from the deck. The claim and its evidence arrive
together.

## Payoff

A reversal rather than a bigger number. Fourteen thousand paid seats against a beach-wide crowd means
the overwhelming majority watch free, from sand facing exactly the same bridge — so the ticket that
sold out in one minute buys a guaranteed chair, not a better angle. A viewer who misses the window has
not lost the night.

## Licensing note — one asset was deliberately left out

Commons holds the single best image for this reel: `2008 Busan Firework Festival-Niagara1.JPG`, the
cascade falling from the bridge deck, which is the article's central visual claim. **It is CC BY-SA
2.0 kr.** ShareAlike would propagate to the whole video, which is not available to brand content, so it
was excluded despite being the best content match. Attribution-only material was skipped too, rather
than carry a credit in frame. Every plate in this reel is public domain or already documented in the
post. Do not revisit this without re-checking the licence.
