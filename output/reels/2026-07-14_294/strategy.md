## Hook System

Hook archetype: `mistake` with a cultural `myth-bust` payoff.

Thumbnail options:

1. Mystery: `WHY METAL CHOPSTICKS?`
2. Mistake: `CHOPSTICKS AREN'T THE PROBLEM`
3. Decision: `SPOON OR CHOPSTICKS?`

Spoken opening options:

1. `If Korean metal chopsticks keep slipping, squeezing harder is probably the mistake.`
2. `Korean chopsticks feel impossible when you make them do the spoon's job.`
3. `Tourists blame the metal. The real problem is that they ignore the spoon.`

Selected hook unit:

- Thumbnail: `CHOPSTICKS AREN'T THE PROBLEM`
- Narration: option 2.
- Reason: the exact slipping-hand action could not be sourced at the required Korea/metal/rights standard. This hook still creates blame reversal and strong stakes while remaining truthful over the article-owned flat-metal-chopsticks image. It avoids using non-Korean wooden/dark chopstick footage just to simulate motion.

## Story And Payoff

- Misconception: stronger pressure creates better control.
- Twist: flat smooth metal can rotate more under excess pressure, and chopsticks are only half of the `sujeo` system.
- Practical payoff: spoon for rice/soup, chopsticks for distinct bites, lighter grip with aligned flat sides.
- Save reason: a three-line cheat code for a first Korean meal.
- Funnel expectation: save/profile visit first, then the full etiquette and buying guide on EpicKor.com.
