Korean metal chopsticks aren't supposed to do everything.

At a Korean table, the spoon and chopsticks work as a pair: sujeo.

🥄 Rice or soup → spoon
🥢 Kimchi, meat, noodles, or banchan → chopsticks

Still slipping? Loosen your grip and line up the flat sides instead of squeezing harder.

Save this before your first Korean meal—and send it to the friend who always blames the chopsticks.
