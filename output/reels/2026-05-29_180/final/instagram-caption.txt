Bukchon is one of Seoul's most photographed places.

But it is not a photo set.

It is a real neighborhood, with hanok homes, narrow alleys, resident life, and Red Zone visitor rules you should understand before you go.

Take the photo.
Keep your voice low.
Do not block doors.
Leave the street calm.
