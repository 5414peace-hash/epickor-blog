# Reel B — Korean ramyun · creative brief

**Slug**: `hub-ramyun` · **Destination**: `https://www.epickor.com/ramyun`
**Approved**: representative, 2026-08-04 (2nd script draft)

## Hook archetype — **Mistake**

The viewer already believes something: that Korean food's defining trait is punishing heat, and that
Buldak is the proof. The Reel opens inside that belief and then removes it.

**First-frame promise**: a 뚝배기 boiling on an open flame — motion in the footage, not a camera push.
**Viewer misconception**: that the scariest noodle on the internet is what Korea actually eats.

## Payoff — a visual comparison, not a number on a card

**Buldak original is 4,404 SHU. A cheongyang pepper starts at 10,000** and gets sliced raw into
soup without anyone remarking on it. The reveal is the green pepper filling the frame.

Deliberately understated: the 3x Haek version (13,200) sits *inside* the pepper's 10,000–23,000
range, so "the hottest Buldak always loses" would be an overstatement. The script uses the original
against the weakest pepper, which is unarguable.

## Save / share reason

Two facts that survive retelling — **26.4% purchase frequency**, and **the free ramyun at Korean
restaurants is always Jin Ramen because Ottogi froze the price for thirteen years.**

## Voice lane

`female_culture_travel`, natural speed. The script is amused rather than challenging — the joke is
that the rest of the world is scared of something Koreans consider mild.
