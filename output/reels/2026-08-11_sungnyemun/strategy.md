# Reel E — Sungnyemun

## The one surprise this Reel proves

**The thing you are photographing is twelve years old, and the label on it was cancelled.**

Two corrections in one gate: the wood was cut in 2013, and "National Treasure No. 1" stopped being a
designation in 2021 because the numbering came off the Japanese colonial list.

## Hook archetype

**Mistake.** The viewer is told, gently, that the caption they were about to write is wrong. Frame
one is people walking through the arch (`37094297`) — a shot that looks like every tourist photo
ever taken here, which is exactly the assumption being set up.

## Viewer misconception → payoff

**Misconception:** an unbroken 600-year-old monument, ranked first among Korea's treasures.
**Payoff:** it burned down within living memory, the arsonist was a repeat offender out on a
suspended sentence who chose it *because it was badly guarded*, and the ranking was never Korean.

## Visual system — **RECORD CORRECTION**

Designed for this Reel only.

- **Palette:** charcoal `#171512`, ember `#e8552d`, aged paper `#e9e1d1`, dancheong green `#2f7d63`.
- **ONS:** an **archival record card**. A monospace field label sits above a hairline rule
  (`BUILT` / `BURNED` / `REBUILT` / `DESIGNATION`) and the value types in beneath it. The card is
  paper-coloured, not a dark overlay — the opposite tonal choice from D and F.
- **The signature move:** the **strike-and-stamp**. `NATIONAL TREASURE No. 1` is drawn in full, an
  ember rule is dragged through it, and a rotated stamp lands reading **국보 숭례문**. The correction
  is the payoff, so it gets the film's only stamp animation.
- **Captions:** an **index-card strip** — cream paper, *dark* text, thin ember rule down the left.
  Inverting the usual white-on-black subtitle is the point: this Reel is about a written record.
- **Fire beat:** during the arson section the paper stock warms and the ember rule thickens. No fire
  stock footage — we do not have Korea-verified 2008 fire footage and will not fake it.

## Voice lane

`male_friend`, but pitched lower and slower through the arson section. The "still stings" line is
the emotional centre and must not be read briskly.

## Funnel

Lands on `/culture`. Outro hook: **LOCALS KNOW THE REST** (insider mechanism) — different from D
(`THERE'S MORE`, open loop) and F (`WE WROTE IT ALL DOWN`, reassurance).

## Verified facts used

- Arson on the night of **10 February 2008**; the gate collapsed after roughly five hours.
- Arsonist **채종기**, then 69, of Chilgok. Motive: a land-compensation dispute over his Ilsan land
  taken for apartment redevelopment; he lost the suit and petitioned repeatedly.
- **He had set fire to 창경궁 문정전 in April 2006**, received 1 year 6 months suspended for 2 years,
  and committed this offence while still inside that suspended term.
- He told police he chose Sungnyemun **because security was lax** (경비 허술해 숭례문 선택했다).
- Restoration completed **29 April 2013**, reopened to the public **4 May 2013** — 5 years 2 months
  20 days, about **₩27.67 billion**, roughly **35,000** workers.
- Heritage **designation numbers were abolished on 19 November 2021** after sixty years; the
  numbering originated with the 1930s colonial-era listing. It is now 국보 숭례문.

## Deliberately left out

The failed dancheong — the repainting cracked within a year and 2022 reporting found it still
unrepaired. It is a real and damning detail, but the Reel already carries two corrections and a
third would flatten all of them. It belongs in the post, not here.
