Korean BBQ is not a steak dinner with a grill in the middle.

The table is the point.

Meat, banchan, leaves, sauces, rice, and stew all work together.
The grill has a rhythm: wait, flip, cut, move, then eat.
And ssam is supposed to be one bite, not a giant leafy sandwich.

Save this before your first Korean BBQ meal.
