## The one surprise this Reel proves

Everyone tells you to do both markets. They are not the same kind of place — one is a
daytime market that shuts down in the evening, and the other genuinely does not get going
until around 10pm and runs until dawn. Doing both in one day means doing both badly.

**Fact check:** the "around 10pm until dawn" framing comes from post 175, which sources
Seoul Metropolitan Government describing Dongdaemun Wholesale Apparel Market as Korea's
largest wholesale apparel market. The narration deliberately says *"till dawn"* rather than
naming a specific hour, because shop-by-shop hours vary.

## Narration — natural American English, mid-twenties, conversational

Seoul's got two huge markets, and everybody tells you to do both — which is honestly a
great way to waste a whole day, because they're not the same kind of place at all.

Namdaemun is a daytime thing. You go in the morning, you eat way too much, you buy stuff
you didn't plan on buying, and by evening most of it's closing up.

Dongdaemun does the exact opposite. A lot of it doesn't even wake up until it's dark, and
the wholesale buildings just keep going till dawn.

So it's really one question. Are you shopping in daylight, or are you shopping after
midnight?

Pick one. Do it properly. Skip the other one — you'll enjoy your trip a lot more.

## Excluded during the footage gate — and what it cost

`28869249` was cut for Japanese katakana. Then the **payoff clip itself** (`38522593`) was cut at
high-resolution inspection: 新宿 (Shinjuku), Shibuya and Japanese noren sit alongside its Hangul
충무김밥 / 24시간. A replacement sweep frame-checked **57 portrait clips** across seven Korea-anchored
queries and found Tokyo, Türkiye and Vietnam — `38489828` was the only verified Korean night result.

**So this Reel ships with a real limitation the representative should weigh.** The narration says
Dongdaemun runs till dawn; the footage can only show that Korea is awake after dark, not that
market. On-screen copy was rewritten so nothing claims otherwise, and the false `NAMDAEMUN` label
was removed from the 방산시장 shot. Shipping a Tokyo alley to fill the gap was the alternative and
was not acceptable.
