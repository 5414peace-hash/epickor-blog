# Reel 299 Strategy - K-Kitchen Heat Lab

## Source Gate

- Source: Blog 299, `Korean Cookware Starter Guide 2026: Ttukbaegi, Ramen Pots, Grill Pans, and Kitchen Scissors`
- Public URL: `https://www.epickor.com/blog/299`
- Status: representative-final-reviewed, published, deployed, and publicly verified before Reels production, as recorded in `HANDOFF.md`.
- Safety boundary: stove compatibility, thermal shock, ventilation, grease, and manufacturer instructions vary by product. The Reel can teach a buying decision but must not certify any specific pot or pan as universally safe.

## Target Viewer

An English-speaking Korean-food fan who wants the familiar gold ramen pot, grill pan, or stone-looking vessel because of appearance, but has not matched the tool to a repeated cooking job, heat source, ventilation, or cleanup routine.

## Hook System

Hook archetype: `mystery` plus `mistake`.

Thumbnail options:

1. Mystery: `WHY IT KEEPS BOILING`
2. Mistake: `DON'T BUY THE PAN`
3. Decision: `YOUR FIRST KITCHEN TOOL`

Spoken opening options:

1. `Turn off the flame—and this Korean pot keeps cooking.`
2. `The gold ramen pot looks iconic, but it may be the wrong first Korean kitchen tool.`
3. `Before you buy Korean cookware, name the one meal you actually repeat.`

Selected hook unit:

- Thumbnail: `WHY IT KEEPS BOILING`
- Narration: option 1.
- First-frame visual requirement: exact real bubbling Korean earthenware/stone-pot context in portrait motion; no generic saucepan or generated steam.
- Reason: residual heat is a visual mystery that naturally leads to the broader `buy for the job, not the photograph` rule.

## Story And Payoff

- Misconception: the most recognizable Korean-looking cookware is automatically the best first purchase.
- Concrete surprise: retained heat can continue cooking; thin ramen pots react quickly; a BBQ pan is only one part of a ventilation, grease, and tool system.
- Twist: scissors or an existing saucepan may solve more meals than a specialized pan.
- Practical payoff: stew → verified ttukbaegi; ramyeon → compatible small pot; home BBQ → scissors/tongs before a purpose-built grill pan; mixed cooking → use the saucepan already owned.
- Human line: `Buy for the heat, not the photograph.`
- Save/share reason: save the four-way tool check before buying a Korean cookware kit.
- Funnel expectation: saves first, full-guide and Amazon comparison clicks second.

## Design System

- Name: `K-Kitchen Heat Lab`.
- Palette: graphite `#11151a`, steel `#b7c2c9`, flame `#ff5a1f`, electric teal `#00d0c6`, hot yellow `#ffc928`.
- Visual vocabulary: residual-heat traces, stove compatibility stamps, grease-flow arrows, thermal contours, and macro action cuts.
- Rhythm: action footage leads; data appears as overlays attached to the object, never as a black presentation slide.
- Distinction: no cream editorial frame from Reel 294 and no repeated metal-chopstick grammar. This system feels like a kinetic kitchen test bench.

## Voice

- Lane: `male_friend`.
- Delivery: quick, practical, and lightly skeptical of impulse purchases; never a product ad or safety lecture.
- Pace: natural 1.0x with short pauses only after the hook and before the buying result.
- Audition: opening through `Thin pots do the opposite...` before full TTS.

## Single Motion Card

- Count: exactly one.
- Placement: approximately 22-30 seconds.
- Role: `heat-lab result` over real moving Korean cooking footage with a dark translucent panel and bright thermal lines.
- Synchronized rows:
  1. `STEW → TTUKBAEGI`
  2. `RAMYEON → COMPATIBLE SMALL POT`
  3. `HOME BBQ → SCISSORS + TONGS FIRST`
  4. `MIXED USE → KEEP YOUR SAUCEPAN`
- Payoff line: `The smartest first tool is the one that fixes a repeated job.`
- Caption zone: clean 410px-safe lower lane; no heat map or row crosses it.

## AI And Context Boundary

- Do not generate boiling behavior, steam, hands, scissors, tongs, grease drainage, Korean cookware, burners, or safety proof.
- Reject Japanese yakiniku, outdoor camping stew, generic Western cookware, and any clip whose tool does not match the spoken claim.
- Thermal contours and arrows must be clearly editorial overlays, not fake temperature measurements.
- Gemini Developer API remains prohibited without explicit representative approval.
