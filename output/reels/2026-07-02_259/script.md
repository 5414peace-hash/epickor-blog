# Reel 259 Script

## Title

Korean Bingsu Guide 2026: Patbingsu And Summer Orders

## Scene Script

1. Bingsu is not one dessert. That is the first ordering mistake.
2. Classic patbingsu is red beans, shaved ice, and chewy tteok. It is calmer than it looks.
3. Modern bingsu can be mango, melon, milk ice, or whatever the cafe wants to show off.
4. The real choice is texture: light ice, creamy milk ice, or toppings so heavy they bury the bowl.
5. Use the first bowl rule: classic, fruit, milk ice, share size, then add coffee if needed.
6. In Korea, bingsu is usually shared. Pick one bowl for the table, then balance it with coffee.
7. Save this before a Seoul dessert stop. More Korean food guides at EpicKor.com.
